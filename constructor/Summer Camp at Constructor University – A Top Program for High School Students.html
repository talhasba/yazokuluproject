Summer Camp 2027 at Constructor University
Take part in one of the top summer schools in Europe and explore exciting academic programs in natural sciences, computer science, business and more.

Learn, connect, and explore at Constructor University’s international Summer Camp for high school students.

Through our summer program, you’ll explore subjects like computer science, chemistry, biology, physics, and economics, while experiencing university life in Germany and immersing yourself in its vibrant culture.

Live on our beautiful Bremen campus, take part in outdoor activities and sports, challenge yourself academically, and connect with students from around the world right in the middle of Europe!

  • Language of instruction: English

Program beyond the classroom

At Summer at Constructor University, you'll have the opportunity to participate in a variety of engaging activities and excursions. These activities are designed to complement your experience, ensuring that you have a well-rounded and enriching time on campus.

Social activities

Our summer camp offers a range of engaging social events that vary each year, hosted on campus and in residential spaces by our student mentors. These events are designed to connect you with other participants and enhance your international summer camp experience. Past events have included arts and crafts sessions, culture nights, movie nights, study breaks, and other relaxed gatherings where you can socialize and make new friends. In addition, we offer daily sports activities such as basketball, soccer, badminton, and volleyball.

Excursions & company visits

Participants have the opportunity to immerse themselves in German culture through a variety of exciting excursions. 

Explore the history and charm of cities like Bremen and Hamburg, visit local landmarks, and take part in activities that blend adventure, education, and cultural immersion.  

The program also includes at least one company visit, giving students a glimpse beyond university life. In past years, participants have visited organizations such as Mercedes-Benz, Airbus Planes & Space, Botanic Bremen, and the Werder Bremen Stadium. Together, these excursions offer a memorable way to experience Germany and connect with peers.

FAQ
What are the application requirements?

Applicants must be between 16 and 18 years old, demonstrate a minimum B2 level of English, and submit a clear scan/photo of their passport or ID showing their full name and date of birth.

How can I apply to the Summer Camp?

After signing up on our application portal, start completing your application and make sure to select the following details to open the correct form:

Application Type: First Year Student
Term: Summer 2026
Degree: Summer Camp
Study Mode: On Campus

If you encounter any issues, don’t hesitate to contact us at summer@constructor.university.
 

What are some application tips?
  • No fee is required to complete your application.
  • Use a desktop or laptop when completing your application. You will be asked to write short essays as part of your application. This may be challenging to do on a mobile phone. Therefore, we advise using a desktop or laptop computer to complete your application.
  • Watch your inbox - we will send you updates regarding your applications as well as further steps. Make sure to always check your inbox and spam folder.
What should I specify while I’m applying to the Summer Camp?

We would like to know which academic fields you are most interested in, why, and your high school grades. Feel free to tell us a bit about yourself, such as your hobbies and any extracurricular activities you are involved in.

Do I have to stay on campus?

Yes, all Summer Camp participants are required to stay on campus throughout the program, except for planned excursions. You will have your own private bedroom, and a bathroom which you share with another student of the same gender. Three meals a day will also be provided for you.

When should I plan to arrive and depart for the Summer Camp?

Participants should plan to arrive on July 24 (before 18:00) and depart on August 4 (the entire day). Please note that early arrivals and late departures are not available, so make sure your travel plans align with these dates.

Do you provide transportation to campus?

During arrival / departure days, we will organize pick-up services from Bremen's main station or airport. We will request all travel details at least one week before arrival to arrange and support your campus arrival and departure.

Do I require a visa to participate in the Summer Camp?

To determine whether you require a visa, please refer to our Visa Information System. The required visa type is the German Schengen Visa, which allows a stay of up to 90 days.
 

How do I obtain a visa?

Once you have been accepted and confirmed your participation in the Summer Camp, please review the visitor visa conditions on the German Embassy website. We will provide you with an admission letter to the Summer Camp which you can include in your visa application.

How long does it take to obtain a visa?

If you have prepared all required documents and depending on embassy appointment availability, the visa processing time can range from 10 days to 8 weeks. We recommend booking a visa appointment as soon as possible, taking into consideration your expected travel date.

I secured my spot at the camp and paid the fee, but my plans have changed and I can not come to the camp anymore. Is there a cancellation policy?
  • Cancellation 2 months or more before the start of the summer camp that is being cancelled: full refund.
  • Cancellation 1-2 months before the start of summer camp that is being cancelled: 50% refund.
  • Cancellation within 1 month or less before the start of camp: no refund. 
  • Cancellation due to visa rejection: full refund (please note that confirmation documents from the Consulate/Embassy will be required).

Please note that a transfer commission might be applied.

Are linen and towels provided during the Summer Camp?

Yes, both bed linen and towels are provided for all participants during their stay. There's no need to bring your own.

What does the living arrangement look like?

Participants will have a private bedroom in a two-bedroom apartment with a shared bathroom (Room Type B). Students will be paired with another summer camp participant, same gender, from around the world. If you would like to request a specific roommate, please email us at summer@constructor.university.
