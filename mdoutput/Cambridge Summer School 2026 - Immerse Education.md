## Where you will be staying

Participants are typically accommodated in historic colleges at Cambridge University, providing an authentic university experience. Accommodation, meals, and extracurricular activities are included in the programme package. The experience of staying at Cambridge colleges is like no other. Each of the colleges has its own individual history and architectural style, and staying within close quarters allows programme participants to forge lifelong bonds. Male and female students will have separate accommodations, and Wi-Fi is available at every college.

##  Career Insights: Cambridge is your classroom

 Journey through the London–Cambridge corridor, the UK’s premier knowledge hub. This trail blends industrial heritage with cutting-edge exploration, offering a unique mix of lived history and future-shaping innovation.

-  Thinkers in Education: Criminology & Forensic Science

######  The Bradfield Centre

At the heart of Cambridge Science Park, students step into one of the UK’s leading knowledge hubs, exploring startups and scale-ups among 150 acres and 1.7 million square feet of high-tech laboratory space.

######  Thinkers in Education: Criminology & Forensic Science

Bringing academic exploration to life in the London–Cambridge corridor, this immersive criminology and forensic science workshop challenges students to analyse evidence, investigate crime scenes and think like real detectives using professional techniques.

######  David Parr House

At David Parr House, students explore David Parr’s work with William Morris in a remarkable home shaped over 40 years, before taking part in an artist workshop and gaining career insights from the Director, Curator and Marketing Manager.

######  Walthamstow Pumphouse Museum

Built in 1877, Walthamstow Pumphouse Museum gives students hands-on insight into engineering heritage, touring historic engine rooms with working engineers, running the engine themselves, and asking career-focused questions about the machinery and industry.

##  Academic Insights: Learn like an undergraduate

 Students on our Academic Insights programmes in Cambridge will study in university classrooms with expert tutors.

-  Small-group learning
-  A taste of undergraduate study
-  Academic role models

######  Small-group learning

Students are taught in small cohorts that reflect the supervision-style teaching associated with Cambridge, encouraging close discussion, rigorous questioning, and detailed academic feedback. Emphasis is placed not only on precision and problem-solving, but on intellectual curiosity and the ability to connect ideas across fields.

######  A taste of undergraduate study

Students engage with the foundational principles that shape undergraduate study across Cambridge’s diverse academic disciplines. From mathematical reasoning and engineering systems to biomedical science, psychology, and the analytical study of human behaviour, students explore the conceptual depth and interdisciplinary thinking characteristic of degree-level learning.

######  Academic role models

Our Cambridge summer school tutors bring university-level teaching experience to design a curriculum that extends beyond the syllabus through supervision-style discussion, analytical problem-solving, and visits to Cambridge’s museums, scientific collections, and academic institutions. Alongside them, dedicated undergraduate mentors provide guidance, encouragement, and day-to-day support within the historic college setting.

######  Portolio-worthy projects

Each student completes a substantial academic Personal Project. Whether solving complex engineering problems, modelling mathematical systems, analysing biomedical case studies, or writing a supervision-style humanities essay, the result is work that demonstrates intellectual depth, independent thinking, and academic maturity.

##  Experience the best of Cambridge this summer

####  Punting on the River Cam

 Punting is a classic Cambridge summer school activity. It involves gliding along the River Cam in a flat-bottomed boat using a long pole to push against the riverbed. It’s a relaxing and enjoyable way to see the city from a different angle. You’ll pass by the beautiful college buildings and famous bridges like the Bridge of Sighs. This traditional pastime is a highlight for many visitors to Cambridge.

####  Visiting King’s College Chapel

 King’s College Chapel is a masterpiece of Gothic architecture and a must-visit during your time in Cambridge summer school. If you enjoy taking photos, this is the perfect spot. The chapel’s grandeur makes it a great backdrop for any picture. You’ll also learn about Cambridge’s rich heritage as you explore the chapel and its surroundings. King’s College Chapel is a place where history, art, and culture come together, making it a must-see while you’re in Cambridge.

####  Mentor family activities

 During your time in Cambridge, you’ll get the chance to spend time with current students as part of your mentor family. These undergraduates are here to help you feel at home. They’ll share their personal experiences, offer advice, and answer any questions you have about university life. Our Cambridge summer program is a great opportunity to hear stories, get tips, and really feel like part of the Cambridge community.

####  Unbelievable Cambridge landmarks

 Cambridge is a city full of history and beautiful sights. Have you ever walked down a medieval street or seen the famous Mathematical Bridge? There’s so much to explore. Whether you’re taking a Cambridge summer school tour or walking on your own, you’ll discover parts of the city that tell fascinating stories from the past.

####  Cultural and recreational activities

 Your time at the Cambridge summer programme is more than just studying. It’s about exploring the city, too. Have you ever wanted to try some traditional British activities? Cambridge offers plenty of chances to experience local pastimes, from enjoying afternoon tea to watching a cricket match in the park. There’s also so much to discover in the city’s museums, where you can learn about history, art, and science.
