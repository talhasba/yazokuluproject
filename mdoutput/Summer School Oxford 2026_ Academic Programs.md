## Where you will be staying

Participants will be based at Oxford University colleges, living and learning in the same historic surroundings as Oxford students. This unique residential experience allows you to fully immerse yourself in university life, form lasting friendships with peers from around the world, and gain authentic insight into life as an Oxford student. The experience of residing within the colleges at Oxford University is unparalleled. Each college possesses its own distinct history and architectural flair. Male and female students will have separate accommodation, and Wi-Fi is available at every college.

##  Exciting activities to experience in Oxford

####  Experience traditional Oxford

 At the Oxford summer course, students experience life beyond the classroom by exploring the traditions and culture that shape the city. Pick up a little local slang, test your knowledge in Oxford-themed quizzes, discover lunch spots in the historic Covered Market, and explore the city’s renowned museums. These shared experiences give students time build friendships with peers from around the world, making the Oxford summer course both memorable and enriching.

####  Explore Oxford’s historic landmarks

 Oxford’s historic buildings aren’t just pretty landmarks, but living pieces of the university’s story. The Radcliffe Camera and Bodleian Library, for instance, have been central to scholarship for centuries, with the Bodleian dating back to the early 1600s and still serving as one of the largest libraries in Europe. Walking through the courtyards and cloisters, you’re seeing the very spaces where countless discoveries and debates unfolded, from medieval scholars to modern Nobel Prize winners.

####  Visit the Oxford Botanic Garden

 The Oxford Botanic Garden will be the best place for those who love nature. It’s a calm and peaceful place where you can relax and enjoy the beauty of the plants and flowers. The garden is one of the oldest in the UK, and it’s home to a variety of interesting plants from all over the world. It’s the perfect spot to take a break from your studies during the Oxford summer course and appreciate nature at its finest.

####  Punting on the River Cherwell

 One of the classic experiences during the Oxford summer school is punting on the River Cherwell. Gliding along the water offers a unique view of Oxford’s colleges, gardens, and historic bridges while learning more about the city’s traditions. It’s a calm and memorable way to experience Oxford from a different perspective.

####  Take a walking tour of the city

 Oxford’s streets are full of stories. Early in the programme, students take part in a guided walking tour of the city – a great chance to get familiar with Oxford while learning about its history. You’ll see famous colleges, historic buildings, and hear stories about the scholars and traditions that shaped the university. You’ll get your bearings, hear tales that are hard to believe, and maybe even spot a café to try later. It’s an ideal introduction to the city and a memorable start to your Oxford summer school courses.
