### Exciting activities in

### Singapore

#### Nature & Urban Exploration

Singapore is a city that beautifully blends lush greenery with world-class architecture, and our students get to experience both. Whether it’s a walk through the serene Botanic Gardens, a scenic canopy walk along the Southern Ridges, or a fun afternoon on campus facilities, there are endless ways to enjoy the outdoors. For those who love a mix of nature and skyline views, Marina Bay Sands and Gardens by the Bay offer stunning backdrops for evening strolls and social activities.

#### Singapore’s cultural heritage

Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam are some of the city’s most famous districts where students can experience Singapore’s multicultural heritage. Here, they can try local delicacies, explore markets, and visit historic sites that reflect the country’s diverse background. Singapore is a hub for cultural exchange, allowing students to make lifelong friendships and network with peers from around the world. This combination of academic excellence and cultural immersion makes our summer school in Singapore an unforgettable experience that sets students up for future success.

#### Discovering Singapore

Singapore is a vibrant city where cultures converge, and our summer school students get to experience this firsthand. From exploring iconic landmarks to discovering hidden gems like the colourful streets of Kampong Glam, every corner of the city offers a new story. Our cultural and social activities not only enrich students’ understanding of the city but also encourage curiosity, connection, and global awareness.
