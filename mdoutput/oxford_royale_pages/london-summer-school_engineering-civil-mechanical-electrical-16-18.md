## Why Attend an Engineering Summer School?

Engineering is curiosity in action and this course combines theory with real world challenges. Explore Mechanics, Civil and Electrical, gaining the tools and mindset to tackle engineering challenges with confidence.

London is a global hub for engineering innovation, where cutting-edge research meets real-world application. From advanced technology firms to pioneering infrastructure projects, the city offers unmatched exposure to the industries shaping the future. Your campus, Imperial College London, places you at the centre of this dynamic ecosystem, surrounded by world-leading research, industry partnerships, and transformative ideas.
