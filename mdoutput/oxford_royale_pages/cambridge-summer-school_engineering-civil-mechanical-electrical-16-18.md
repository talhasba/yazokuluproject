## Why attend an Engineering Summer School?

Engineering is curiosity in action, and this summer school blends core theory with real-world challenges. You’ll explore mechanical, civil, and electrical engineering, developing the analytical skills and problem-solving mindset engineers rely on. From learning key principles to building, testing, and refining your own designs, you’ll gain practical experience and the confidence to tackle complex challenges.

Cambridge has long been at the forefront of scientific and engineering breakthroughs, making it an inspiring place to study the subject. Living and learning in the colleges of the University of Cambridge, you’ll experience campus life within a world-renowned centre of innovation. Surrounded by cutting-edge research and a culture of discovery, you’ll see how engineering ideas evolve from theory to transformative real-world solutions.

## Step Into the World of Engineering

Want to step into engineering? Explore design, problem-solving, and real-world innovation, then apply your skills through practical build challenges and experiments using industry-standard equipment. Engineering is more than equations. Learn to think creatively, work collaboratively, and turn ideas into working solutions through hands-on projects that mirror real engineering practice.

## An unforgettable summer

Exploring Cambridge feels like stepping into centuries of discovery and innovation. Our excursions help students experience the city’s rich academic heritage through guided tours, college visits, and riverside highlights - plus an optional London trip for anyone wanting even more adventure.

- Guided walking tours around historic Cambridge landmarks
- Visits to renowned colleges, libraries, and heritage sites
- Exploration of Market Square, scenic quads, and riverside paths
- Optional full-day London excursion for a capital-city experience

## Make connections to last a lifetime

The social programme in Cambridge blends creativity, culture, and connection. Students can enjoy punting on the Cam, hands-on workshops, debate evenings, welcome activities, and visits to the city’s museums, galleries, and vibrant Market Square.

- Punting along the River Cam with friends
- Creative workshops that encourage expression and new ideas
- Debate society evenings for thoughtful discussion and confidence
- Visits to museums, galleries, and the lively Market Square
- Welcoming social activities designed to help everyone settle in

## Enjoy your favourites, or try something new

Our sports offering stays energetic and inclusive, giving every student the chance to join in. From football tournaments and badminton to classic English lawn games, capture the flag, and our upbeat “Olympics in the Park,” there’s something for everyone.

- Football tournaments open to all skill levels
- Badminton and fast-paced indoor mini-games
- Traditional English lawn games
- Capture-the-flag sessions that get everyone moving
- “Olympics in the Park” with a mix of challenges
