## Why attend a Medicine Summer School?

An Explore Medicine Summer School with Oxford Royale introduces you to the world of healthcare through hands-on, practical learning. You’ll study anatomy and diagnosis, practise essential clinical skills, and develop the ability to think critically and act decisively. Working with like-minded students from around the world, you’ll build confidence while discovering what it takes to pursue a future in medicine.

Based in the colleges of the University of Cambridge, you’ll live and study on campus in a city renowned for scientific discovery and medical breakthroughs. Walking through historic surroundings that have inspired generations of researchers and doctors, you’ll experience student life in a setting defined by innovation, ambition, and achievement.

Be inspired by Cambridge’s rich history of medical research and discovery: it’s the perfect place to discover your ambitions.

### Walking Tour of Cambridge

Experience the magic of Cambridge on a walking tour of the city. Wander through historic colleges with stunning architecture, see the pub where the discovery of DNA was announced, or uncover hidden gems from coffee shops to art galleries.

### Graduation

Experience an Oxford Royale graduation, on campus at the University of Cambridge. Dress up with your new friends, dine in a magnificent venue, and celebrate everything you have achieved on your programme.

### Punting

Feel like a real Cambridge student and try your hand at punting, a type of rowing that uses a metal pole to push the boat forward. Relax in the sun and view some of Cambridge’s spectacular colleges as you punt along the River Cam.

### Museums and Galleries

Immerse yourself in the arts and sciences at Cambridge’s renowned museums. See the works of old masters at the Fitzwilliam Museum, explore the natural world at the University Museum of Zoology, and discover prehistory at the Sedgwick Museum of Earth Sciences.

- Guided walking tours around historic Cambridge landmarks
- Visits to renowned colleges, libraries, and heritage sites

## Make connections to last a lifetime

The social programme in Cambridge blends creativity, culture, and connection. Students can enjoy punting on the Cam, hands-on workshops, debate evenings, welcome activities, and visits to the city’s museums, galleries, and vibrant Market Square.

- Punting along the River Cam with friends
- Creative workshops that encourage expression and new ideas
- Debate society evenings for thoughtful discussion and confidence
- Visits to museums, galleries, and the lively Market Square
- Welcoming social activities designed to help everyone settle in
