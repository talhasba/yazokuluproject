## Why attend a Medicine Summer School?

Discover the world of modern medicine through hands-on practicals, workshops and simulations. Explore biology, genetics, neuroscience, genomics, emergency medicine and anatomy while gaining insight into careers as a Doctor or medical researcher.

Studying medicine on campus with Oxford Royale at Yale offers the opportunity to experience learning in one of the world’s most prestigious academic environments. Our programme places you in a historic centre of medical scholarship and scientific discovery, where innovation and research have shaped modern healthcare for generations.

## Step Into the World of Medicine

Want to step into medicine? Study anatomy, diagnosis, and patient care, then practise your skills in simulations - with state of the art medical training equipment - designed to feel like the real thing. Medicine is more than memorising facts. Learn how to think critically, act decisively, and care for patients through hands-on practice and teamwork.

## Yale: Experience the Ivy League Tradition

Explore the historic campus and vibrant cultural scene of New Haven, a city known for its arts, architecture and academic heritage. With striking Gothic buildings, renowned museums and a close-knit student atmosphere, Yale offers a distinctive Ivy League experience.

### New York City Excursion

Thrilling excursion to New York City - from the Empire State Building and MoMA to Central Park, Times Square and Fifth Avenue.

### Visit Yale Faculties

Experience the buzz of academic life and explore Yale University’s renowned faculties, stepping into the spaces where groundbreaking ideas continue to be developed and a legacy of leadership has shaped global history for over three centuries! You will have the opportunity to see areas such as the Yale School of Management, the Yale Law Library and the Center for Engineering Innovation and Design (CEID).

**

### Friday Night Party

Nothing captures the quintessential "American College Experience" like a high-energy Friday night social, where the campus transforms into a vibrant hub of music, dancing, and school spirit. This exclusive event provides the perfect atmosphere for students to unwind after a week of elite academics, fostering lifelong international friendships and unforgettable memories in the heart of a storied Ivy League setting.

### Walking Tour of New Haven

A guided tour of New Haven will help you feel like a local in this historic university city: as the first planned city in the whole of the US it is easy to navigate, and your guide gives you a chance to see the magnificent Gothic architecture and vibrant local scenery.

### Visit Famous Yale Museums & Galleries

Yale is a centre of culture with many fascinating museums and galleries to explore. You can visit The Peabody Museum, the Cushing Center, Yale Museum and the Yale Art Gallery for a taste of Yale’s history.
