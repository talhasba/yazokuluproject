## Why attend a Psychology Summer School?

At the Oxford Royale Psychology Summer School, you’ll explore the science of the human mind, from memory and personality to behaviour and psychological disorders. Design your own investigations, consider the ethics of research, and examine questions such as how early experiences shape adulthood or why misinformation spreads. Build critical thinking skills while discovering how psychology helps us better understand people and society.

Based in the colleges of the University of Cambridge, you’ll live and learn in a city internationally recognised for excellence in psychological research. While studying with Oxford Royale, you’ll experience campus life in this historic academic setting, an inspiring environment that encourages curiosity, discussion, and deeper thinking.

## Step Into the World of Psychology
