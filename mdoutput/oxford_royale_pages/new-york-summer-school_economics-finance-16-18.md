## Why attend an Economics & Finance Summer School?

Understand how economies work and why money moves. Study markets, investment, and global finance, then apply your knowledge in real-world projects. Learn to think like an economist and act like a strategist.

Explore the world of economics and finance through hands-on learning. Build core foundations in micro and macroeconomics, markets and investment, tackle real-world business and policy challenges, and practise analysing data, testing strategies, and responding to shifting global trends through simulations and case studies.

New York becomes the classroom, with daily opportunities to explore its culture, energy, and iconic neighbourhoods. Students dive into museums, parks, and city highlights, discovering something new each day while experiencing the momentum and creativity that define life in NYC.

- Visits to major museums and cultural institutions across the city
- Daily exploration of vibrant neighbourhoods and local landmarks
- Time to enjoy cafés, bookstores, and street-level discoveries
- Walks through Central Park, the High Line, and historic districts
- A dynamic mix of guided outings and independent exploration

Social life in New York is lively, creative, and full of inspiration. From journalism and debate clubs to evenings discovering the city, students connect through unforgettable shared experiences in a setting that invites curiosity, expression, and big conversations.

- Journalism club exploring storytelling and media skills
- Debate society sessions for confident, thoughtful discussion
- Group outings to galleries, parks, and neighbourhood hotspots
- Welcome activities to help everyone settle in fast
- Evenings discovering local cafés, culture, and creative corners

Sports in New York make the most of the city’s iconic green spaces and waterfront energy. Students enjoy active sessions that mix fitness, teamwork, and fun—from football to run club to park-based games in unforgettable locations.

- Walks along scenic riverside paths
- Active games and fitness sessions
- Inclusive, social sports open to all experience levels
- A lively mix of outdoor activities
