## Why Attend a Film Summer School?

Write, shoot, and edit your own short film on location in Oxford — the backdrop to Harry Potter, James Bond, and more. Film is more than watching stories unfold. Learn how to craft them yourself, gaining hands-on experience in production, directing, and digital editing as you bring your vision to life.

## Step Into the World of Film

Master professional cameras, sound, and lighting as you film your own project with Oxford Royale, then edit it to craft a compelling story. Work in teams to develop and pitch a full production package, explore future pathways in filmmaking, learn from industry experts and experience life in historic Oxford colleges alongside ambitious, like-minded peers.

- Direct, shoot and edit a film from scratch
- Learn on professional camera and equipment
- Discover the key stages of the production process
- Work with film industry experts
- Academic Showcase project

### Bruce Windwood

Filmmaker

Windwood is a multi-award winning filmmaker with credits including the comedy feature film 12 In A Box. Bruce is a commissioned writer for TV and audio as well as a composer for film, audio and TV soundtracks. Having helped to design, develop and expand ORA’s filmmaking classes over the last 11 years, Bruce is one of the most senior tutors. He also regularly teaches short and long-form filmmaking at the prestigious National Film & TV School as well as Film Oxford.

### The Ashmolean Museum

The Ashmolean Museum of Art and Archaeology is one of the largest and most significant museums in the UK. Home to remarkable artefacts, masterpieces, and objects from civilisations across the world, it offers an inspiring window into global history and culture. Join Oxford Royale to explore its collections and encounter art and archaeology that span thousands of years.

## An unforgettable summer

Exploring Oxford should feel like stepping into a story you get to live in. Our excursions bring the city’s history to life through guided tours, college visits, and hidden gems - plus an optional London trip for those craving a bigger adventure.

- Guided walking tours around historic Oxford landmarks
- Visits to iconic colleges, libraries, and heritage sites
- Exploration of local markets, scenic quads, and riverside paths
- Engaging stories from expert guides who know Oxford inside out
- Optional full-day London excursion for an unforgettable city experience

## Make connections to last a lifetime

Our social programme is all about connection, creativity, and giving students space to try new things. From punting and hands-on workshops to debate evenings, gallery visits, and relaxed welcome events, there’s always something inspiring happening after classes.

- Punting on the Cherwell with friends
- Creative workshops designed to spark imagination and confidence
- Debate society evenings for big ideas and bold voices
- Karaoke, talent shows and fashion workshops
- Visits to museums, galleries, the Covered Market, and Westgate
- Friendly welcome activities to help everyone settle in quickly

## Enjoy your favourites, or try something new

Sports sessions are designed to be lively, inclusive, and full of friendly competition. Whether you're into team sports or just want to try something new, you’ll enjoy everything from football tournaments to classic English lawn games and our energetic park “Olympics.”

- Football tournaments for all skill levels
- Badminton matches and fast-paced mini-games
- Morning run club
- Traditional English lawn games
- Capture-the-flag sessions that get everyone moving
- “Olympics in the Park” with a mix of challenges

## End your summer in style

Our graduation celebration marks the end of the programme in truly memorable style. Held in a beautiful Oxford venue, it brings everyone together to reflect, celebrate achievements, and enjoy a formal dinner before dancing the night away at the graduation party.

- Graduation ceremony in a historic Oxford location
- Recognition of achievements and programme milestones
- Formal dinner in a classic Oxford dining hall
- Celebration with teachers, friends, and peers
- Graduation party to mark an unforgettable final night
