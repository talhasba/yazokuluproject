#### Yale University: Where Global Leaders Begin Their Journey

Live and study at Yale University's historic campus and experience the tradition and innovation which continue to shape global leaders.

- Founded in 1701, Yale is one of the world’s most prestigious Ivy League universities
- Alumni include five U.S. Presidents and global leaders across politics and business
- Consistently ranked among the top Ivy League universities globally

#### Visit Cambridge, Massachusetts: home of Harvard University

Enjoy an exclusive, privately guided journey to Massachusetts, famous world-wide for its education system.

- Founded in 1636, Harvard is older than the United States
- Alumni include 8 U.S. Presidents and over 150 Nobel laureates
- Harvard Business School produces more FTSE 100 CEOs than any non-UK university

#### Columbia University: Learn in the Heart of New York City

Live and learn in Manhattan, at Columbia's classical campus which provides the perfect backdrop for exploring leadership.

- Founded in 1754, Columbia University is a world-leading Ivy League institution in New York City
- Alumni include Franklin D. Roosevelt, Ruth Bader Ginsburg, and Barack Obama
- Manhattan campus places students at the centre of global finance, politics, culture, and innovation

## Three Ivy League Campuses. Two Unforgettable weeks.

At Yale, past and future converge. In New York, you’re at the heart of culture and leadership. Don’t miss out on the opportunity to get a hands-on insight into Ivy League life.

### Wicked the Musical - with Behind the Scenes Workshop

In the morning, students attend an exclusive Broadway Bound Workshop with a professional producer to deconstruct the high-stakes financing and management of a multi-million-dollar production. The day culminates in the evening with a performance of the global phenomenon Wicked, allowing the cohort to witness the spectacular result of that strategic leadership in action.

### Exclusive Guest Speakers

**Virtual Admissions Workshop: Tamy-Feé Meneide**

With admissions experience from both MIT and Dartmouth, Tamy-Feé is an educational consultant and admissions strategist with a background in higher education and over a decade of experience guiding students through highly selective admissions processes.

**Live Admissions Workshop: Dr. Sergey R, Ivy League Admissions Expert**

Dr. Sergey R has helped students gain admission to Stanford, Princeton, Brown, Cornell, NYU, UC Berkeley, UChicago, and more.

**Psychology-Focused Session: Monroe Egbuchunam**

Monroe Egbuchunam is social media UX researcher in cognitive psychology, HCI, and algorithmic bias studying marginalized users’ experiences with digital platforms and AI.

### Night in K-Town

For a true taste of New York City’s vibrant energy, students head to K-Town (Koreatown) for an evening of high-energy fun and cultural immersion. Nestled in the heart of Midtown, this neon-lit district serves as the perfect backdrop for a quintessential NYC experience. The night culminates in a private karaoke session, where students can let loose, bond with their peers, and build lasting memories.

### Visit to Wall Street

A professional visit to Wall Street places students at the epicenter of global capitalism and economic leadership. Walking through the Financial District, the cohort will analyze the historical and modern-day impact of the New York Stock Exchange and the Federal Reserve, observing how high-stakes decision-making here ripples across international markets.

### Visit the UN Headquarters

A visit to the United Nations Headquarters offers students an elite look at international diplomacy within a designated international territory. Walking through the halls where global history is shaped, the cohort will explore iconic spaces like the General Assembly Hall and the Security Council Chamber.

### The Met

A visit to The Metropolitan Museum of Art offers an unparalleled look at 5,000 years of global history through the lens of creative and strategic leadership. As students explore world-class masterpieces—from the ancient Temple of Dendur to the Renaissance galleries—they witness how art has been used as a tool for political influence, cultural identity, and social change

### Sightseeing

This is the opportunity of a lifetime to explore New Haven, a historic university town, and New York, one of the most exciting cities on the planet!

Stroll through ivy-covered courtyards and Yale’s picturesque Old Campus, soaking in the atmosphere of one of the world’s most historic universities.

In your second week travel to NYC: Not your conventional classroom. Enjoy a shopping trip to Soho, a walking tour of Wall Street and trips to Central Park. Plus the opportunity to see New York’s most famous sights from the water with a river cruise.

### Museums & Galleries at Yale

Explore where we have come from to what makes us human in Yale’s world-class museums on campus. Including:

- The Yale Peabody Museum at Yale University one of the oldest, largest and most prolific university natural history museums in the world.
- The Cushing Center inspires curiosity about the brain while teaching the history of neurosurgery.
- Yale University Art Gallery, the oldest university art museum in America.

Visit two of New York’s most famous museums to explore both the science of our planet and the art and cultures that shape human history.

- Guided tour of the Met, one of the world’s leading art museums
- Guided tour of the Natural History Museum

### Manuscript Library

Experience history at the Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, the literary archive of Yale University. One of the largest buildings dedicated to rare books, the library contains more than a million books — a book-lover’s dream!
