## Why attend an Artificial Intelligence Summer School?

An Explore Artificial Intelligence Summer School with Oxford Royale invites you to discover the technologies shaping the future in a hands-on, interactive environment. You’ll explore the foundations of AI, strengthen your coding and problem-solving skills, and learn what it means to design intelligent systems. Alongside ambitious peers from around the world, you’ll ignite your passion for technology and innovation.

Based in the colleges of the University of Cambridge, you’ll live and study on campus in one of the world’s most prestigious university cities. Walking through historic courts that have inspired generations of scientists and innovators, you’ll experience student life in a setting known for discovery and breakthrough thinking — the perfect place to explore the future of AI.

### Punting in Cambridge

Feel like a real Cambridge student and try your hand at punting, a type of rowing that uses a metal pole to push the boat forward! Relax in the sun and view some of Cambridge’s spectacular colleges as you punt along the River Cam.

### Walking Tour of Cambridge

Experience the magic of Cambridge on a walking tour of the city. Wander through historic colleges with stunning architecture, see the pub where the discovery of DNA was announced, or uncover hidden gems from coffee shops to art galleries.

### Oxford Royale Graduation

Experience an Oxford Royale graduation, on campus at Clare College, University of Cambridge. Dress up with your new friends, dine in a magnificent venue, and celebrate everything you have achieved on your programme.

### Optional London Excursion

Discover the UK's most exciting city, from Trafalgar Square and Buckingham Palace to the Museum of Natural History, there are endless ways to be entertained. With luxury shopping and opportunities for souvenirs, London is a must visit location.
