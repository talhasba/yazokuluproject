## Why Attend an Architecture Summer School?

Architecture is creativity in action, where history, design, and innovation meet. Learn architecture with Oxford Royale from concept to construction through creative challenges, model-making, and technical drawing, building the skills to bring your ideas to life.

Be inspired by the beating heart of the UK, where modern innovation and historic architecture come together. From palaces to skyscrapers, your time on campus at Imperial College London, the city’s best university, will ignite your passion for architecture and help you master design.

## Step Into the World of Architecture

Explore the evolution of design from Ancient Greece to modern London while developing skills in technical drawing, mapping and model-making. Tackle a “City of the Future” project, learn from expert tutors, and discover your architectural interests.

Build a portfolio, collaborate with ambitious peers, and enjoy life in London, with its museums, culture and world-leading creative inspiration.

## An unforgettable summer

London offers a fast-paced mix of culture, style, and exploration. Students enjoy built-in city tours, luxury shopping opportunities, and the freedom to discover cafés, museums, and hidden corners of Kensington - the perfect base for embracing everything London has to offer.

- Guided tours through iconic London districts and landmarks
- Luxury shopping experiences in world-famous retail destinations
- Independent time to explore museums, cafés, and cultural hotspots
- Easy access to Kensington’s galleries, parks, and boutique streets
- A vibrant, contemporary city atmosphere at every turn

## Make connections to last a lifetime

The social scene in London is full of possibilities. Students can expect creative workshops, group outings, relaxed welcome events, and evenings exploring local culture - all set against the buzz of one of the world’s most exciting cities.

- Creative workshops that spark collaboration and fresh ideas
- Group outings to museums, galleries, and riverside destinations
- Welcome activities that help everyone connect from day one
- Debate society and grand final
- Fun social opportunities woven into city life

## Enjoy your favourites, or try something new

Sports in London centre on open-air fun and friendly competition. With Hyde Park as their playground, students can dive into football, badminton, classic lawn games, capture the flag, and our energetic park “Olympics,” all in one of London’s most iconic green spaces.

- Football matches and friendly tournaments in Hyde Park
- Badminton and other fast-paced mini-games
- “Olympics in the Park” with lively challenges and team spirit

## End your summer in style

The London graduation celebration reflects the energy of the programme - fun, upbeat, and memorable. Students gather in an exciting London venue for a ceremony, a special dinner, and a graduation party that sends everyone off with unforgettable city-night vibes.

- Graduation ceremony in a stylish contemporary London venue
- Recognition of achievements and programme milestones
- Celebration dinner with friends, mentors, and classmates
- End-of-programme party to mark the experience in true London style
- A lively, celebratory atmosphere to close an incredible summer
