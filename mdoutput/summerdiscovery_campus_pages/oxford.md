# Summer at Oxford

**Our courses in Oxford give high school students the opportunity to study and live in one of the world’s most celebrated academic cities.**

Oxford is renowned for centuries of scholarship, inspiring architecture, and a vibrant intellectual and cultural life. Our courses are hosted at historic colleges of the University of Oxford, providing students with a memorable setting in which to learn, explore, and connect with peers from around the world.

Beyond the classroom, students can discover the city of Oxford through a range of cultural activities and excursions. Depending on the program and itinerary, these may include visits to Oxford landmarks such as the Bodleian Library, Christ Church, and the Ashmolean Museum, as well as excursions to destinations such as London, Stratford-upon-Avon, the Cotswolds, historic castles, and Stonehenge. Activities and destinations vary by session and are subject to change.

Atlas Summer Courses is an independent organisation that contracts with colleges of the University of Oxford for the use of facilities. Atlas Summer Courses is not part of, affiliated with, or endorsed by the University of Oxford.

## Oxford Programs

Our programs for Middle and High School students provide an exceptional opportunity to challenge oneself to meet Oxford’s world-class academic standard while exploring the subjects that stir imagination and impassioned drive. Students will participate in cutting-edge programs such as global leadership, creative writing, philosophy, STEM innovation, and international law. They’ll also have the chance to hone critical thinking and problem-solving skills with guidance from Oxford's esteemed faculty and scholars. Courses include biology, medicine, history, business and entrepreneurship, English literature and law.

Oxford-style education is both intimate – not more than eight students per class – and personalized, as instructors have the latitude to dive more deeply into subjects that resonate with each individual study group, fostering a deeper connection to the material and inspiring full intellectual engagement.

This unique program in Oxford combines academic exploration with personal growth in a setting that is both worldly and world-class. Students will leave inspired — equipped with new knowledge and skills, and the confidence to apply them as they embark on a path of lifelong success. With flexible 2-week sessions offered throughout July and August, the program allows students to tailor their ideal summer experience, ensuring unforgettable memories and transformative learning opportunities.

## Atlas Summer Courses Academics

At Atlas Summer Courses, learning is **interactive, flexible and tailored to the students in the room**. Rather than following a fixed syllabus, tutors adapt course content to students’ ages, existing knowledge and interests. With class sizes averaging eight students, **seminars encourage discussion, questioning and active participation**, allowing students to explore their chosen subject alongside peers.

Alongside seminars, **tutorials provide opportunities for more focused academic discussion and feedback**, helping students examine ideas in greater depth and develop their own thinking. Teaching is adapted to the age and academic level of each group, with students encouraged to develop skills including **critical thinking, communication, problem-solving and independent learning** as they progress through the programme.

-

## Explore Courses

Atlas Summer Courses offers a broad range of subjects for students aged **13–24**, from Medicine, Law and Philosophy, Politics & Economics (PPE) to Computer Science, Business, Creative Writing and the Arts. Courses give students the opportunity to explore subjects they already enjoy or discover areas they may be considering for future study.

Course content is **adapted to students’ age and academic level**, allowing younger students to build their understanding while older students can engage with more complex ideas and questions. Whatever their subject, students are encouraged to **think critically, contribute their own perspectives and explore their academic interests in greater depth**.
