## Yale University Programs

Summer Discovery’s Institute for the Gifted courses at Yale University offer especially talented and creative students a proprietary academic curriculum that is challenging, interactive, and hands-on. The courses are designed to foster curiosity, promote personal growth, and inspire students to discover new interests and talents.

The program spans a variety of fields, including business, computer science & technology, law & government, medicine, among others, giving students the chance to explore diverse disciplines and deepen their understanding of subjects that spark curiosity. Our rigorous curriculum includes cutting-edge subjects such as biopsychology, medical microbiology, astrophysics, quantum mechanics, environmental science, engineering, and more, providing students with an understanding of these fields that is both deep and wide. Popular course titles include “Technical Design with VEX Robotics”, “Neuroscience: The Brain’s Physiology & Pathology”, and “Money Matters: Stocks, Bonds and Your Financial Future”.

Course curricula are curated to student interests, and one-to-one attention accommodates many different learning styles, ensuring each student’s academic goals are met. Full immersion encourages intellectual exploration and critical thinking, supported by world-class resources. Yale’s state-of-the-art laboratories and academic facilities provide students with access to cutting-edge tools that fuel innovation and discovery.
