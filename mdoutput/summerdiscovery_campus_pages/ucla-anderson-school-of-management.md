# Summer at UCLA Anderson School of Management

**UCLA and Summer Discovery have been partners in pre-college enrichment for over 35 years, providing students with an unparalleled academic experience, and the Summer Discovery UCLA Anderson School program builds on that tradition. Developed and hosted entirely by the UCLA Anderson School of Management Center for Media, Entertainment & Sports, every course has been created and is taught by distinguished faculty instructors specially selected by UCLA Anderson.**

**The collaboration between UCLA Anderson and Summer Discovery reflects a shared commitment to fostering the next generation of business leaders. Located in the heart of Los Angeles—a city known for its thriving entertainment, media, and sports industries—UCLA Anderson offers unparalleled real-world learning experiences and case studies, both on and off-campus. This dynamic environment fosters strategic thinking and encourages personal and professional growth.**

**The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors**

UCLA Anderson is designed for students who are passionate, driven, and eager to explore the world of business. The programs at Anderson attract individuals who want to challenge themselves, gain hands-on experience, and leverage cutting-edge strategies to make an impact in the marketplace. Students have the opportunity to connect with industry leaders and guest speakers who are an integral part of our program.

Situated in a global hub of entertainment, media, and professional sports—UCLA Anderson offers cutting-edge, real-world-relevant coursework, and beyond the classroom, students are immersed in the vibrant urban environment of Los Angeles, with endless opportunities to explore the city’s most famous attractions, neighborhoods, and experiences. From Rodeo Drive to Malibu Beach; Dodgers, Angels and Galaxy games to the Griffith Observatory and the iconic Hollywood sign, the energy and diversity of this world-class city provides a truly unforgettable and enriching summer.

## UCLA Anderson School of Management Programs

UCLA Anderson offers a variety of specialized academies for aspiring leaders, including the Digital Marketing & Social Media Academy; the Business of Entertainment, Media & Sports Academy; the Sports Business Academy; and the Music Business Academy. Students are exposed to industry leaders and taught by UCLA-selected expert instructors in the fields of business, leadership and entrepreneurship and sports management.

By integrating advanced academic insights with hands-on classroom experiences and field trips to support the curriculum, the program equips students with the strategic thinking and innovative mindset necessary to excel in their future endeavors, be they corporate or entrepreneurial.

**Students who complete the program will receive an official certificate of completion from UCLA Anderson, a prestigious addition to their academic portfolio.**
