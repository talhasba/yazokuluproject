# Summer at Dartmouth

**As a distinguished Ivy League University, Dartmouth stands as one of the premier academic institutions in the world, renowned for its commitment to excellence in education, research, and community engagement.**

**The Dartmouth Summer Scholars pre-college academic enrichment program is designed to give students a taste of the rigorous academic environment and vibrant intellectual community that defines Dartmouth. High school students are provided an extraordinary opportunity to choose from a multitude of diverse academies across multiple disciplines. Summer Discovery students benefit from small class sizes, personalized attention from distinguished faculty, and a rigorous curriculum that challenges and inspires.**

**The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors**

The students who thrive at our Dartmouth Summer Scholars pre-college program are academically driven, motivated and self-sufficient. They may be considering Dartmouth or other Ivy League schools and will flourish in a supportive environment with like-minded peers, as they balance their academic pursuits with social activities outside the classroom to achieve both academic and personal growth.

Founded in 1769, Dartmouth boasts a rich history and tradition of academic excellence. Located in the charming town of Hanover, New Hampshire, Dartmouth's stunning 269-acre campus is a quintessential representation of New England's natural beauty. The campus features a harmonious blend of historic and modern architecture, with iconic landmarks such as the Baker-Berry Library serving as symbols of the college's enduring legacy and forward-thinking vision.

Hanover's vibrant downtown area is just a short walk from campus and offers a delightful array of shops, restaurants, and cafes along Main Street. This bustling yet intimate community provides a welcoming atmosphere for students, faculty, and visitors alike. The local region also offers a wealth of outdoor activities and recreational opportunities, from hiking and biking on numerous trails to exploring nearby parks and engaging in water sports on the Connecticut River.

## Dartmouth Programs

The Dartmouth Summer Scholars pre-college academic enrichment programs are intensive academies designed by Dartmouth and taught by university faculty and expert instructors. They feature over five hours of class time each day, and some courses culminate in capstone presentations before a panel of selected peers and community members.

Diverse fields of study include entrepreneurship, business, data science, creative writing, psychology, medicine, engineering, design thinking, leadership and more. Highly sought-after courses include "Research and Lab Work Medicine”, “Business Foundations” and “Clean Energy Technologies”.

The Dartmouth Leadership Institute signature 2-week program combines intensive study, personal reflection, and leadership skills building on campus at Dartmouth combined with an off-campus outdoor experience. This signature program treats leadership as a practice: understand yourself, earn trust, make decisions under uncertainty, and help a team move forward.

**In addition to life-changing knowledge, self-confidence and maturity, students receive a Dartmouth-branded Certificate of Completion, as well as a letter of recognition for coursework completed, as tangible evidence of their time and accomplishments at Dartmouth.**

## Dartmouth Leadership Institute

Leadership is often treated as a title. The Dartmouth Leadership Institute treats it as a practice: understand yourself, earn trust, make decisions under uncertainty, and help a team move forward. Students begin on Dartmouth campus with leadership models, neuroscience, self-assessments, values work, case examples, and guided discussion that reveal how strengths, habits, and choices shape a group.

During the second week, students move to an outdoor lodge where they practice communication, teamwork, resilience, and judgment through collaborative challenges and write a personal This I Believe leadership essay. Hiking, orienteering, team initiatives, trail work, and increasingly complex outdoor challenges make leadership visible: students must decide when to step forward, when to listen, how to adapt, and how to support others. Reflection after each experience turns action into a personal framework for continued growth.
