# Summer at UC Berkeley M.E.T. Innovation Academy

**This UC Berkeley program, run by UC Berkeley's prestigious College of Engineering and the Haas School of Business, is a highly selective and elite learning experience that merges technology and entrepreneurship.**

**This immersive summer program is designed for rising high school juniors and seniors eager to explore the intersection of technology and entrepreneurship. Run by and hosted on the prestigious UC Berkeley campus, this program is a collaboration between the esteemed College of Engineering and the Haas School of Business, offering a unique academic experience that integrates technical innovation with strategic business principles.**

**The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors**

Tailored for exceptional students with strong aptitudes in science, technology, mathematics, and communication, M.E.T.ia provides a rigorous curriculum that fosters analytical thinking, creative problem-solving, and entrepreneurial leadership. Designed for those driven by curiosity and a passion for technological advancement, the program equips participants with the foundational skills necessary to navigate and shape the future of innovation.

Through hands-on projects, real-world case studies, and direct mentorship from Berkeley’s distinguished faculty, students gain invaluable insights into engineering, business strategy, and venture creation. Beyond the classroom, participants experience life at Berkeley, engage with like-minded peers, and build connections that extend beyond the program—preparing them to lead, innovate, and make meaningful contributions to the industries of tomorrow.

## UC Berkeley M.E.T. Innovation Academy Details

  - Immerse yourself in a rigorous but enriching learning experience at **Berkeley’s College of Engineering** and **Haas School of Business** that’ll enable you to expand your engineering skills and establish a solid business foundation.
  - Participate in a capstone project where you can develop and pitch a startup idea to seasoned venture investors together with your peers.
  - Develop prototypes at **Berkeley’s Jacobs Institute for Design Innovation** (pictured on the left).
  - Learn from industry titans, technologists, product managers, and marketers from leading tech companies in **Silicon Valley**, such as Ambi Robotics in Berkeley, Dolby Headquarters in San Francisco, and more.
  - Take advantage of our stellar career development guidance and resources – at the end of the week you will have created a standout resume and LinkedIn profile.
  - Interact with current M.E.T. students and alumni making waves in the tech and entrepreneurship space.
  - Engage in unique **Bay Area** outings to professional sporting events and cultural hotspots while building a lifelong community with your peers.

Hands-On Startup Innovation

Participate in a dynamic, hands-on startup innovation experience at Berkeley Met, where you'll collaborate with a diverse team of peers on a high-impact capstone project. From ideation to execution, you'll develop and refine a groundbreaking startup concept, navigating the challenges of market validation, business strategy, and product development. The program culminates in a high-stakes pitch to seasoned venture investors, providing you with invaluable real-world entrepreneurial skills, direct feedback from industry experts, and the opportunity to turn your vision into a viable venture.
