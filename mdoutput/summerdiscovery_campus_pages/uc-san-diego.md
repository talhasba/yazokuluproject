Located in La Jolla, one of California’s most prestigious coastal communities, UC San Diego offers an exceptional summer setting that blends academic rigor with natural beauty. Just minutes from downtown San Diego, the campus is surrounded by world-renowned beaches, a thriving biotech and innovation ecosystem, and a vibrant cultural scene.

The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors.

Outside the classroom, students can enjoy a wide range of on-campus and off-campus activities that take full advantage of San Diego’s unique location. From campus events like karaoke nights, trivia, paint nights, and scavenger hunts to weekend excursions to La Jolla Shores Beach, the San Diego Zoo, Padres baseball games, and the Little Italy Mercato Farmers Market, there’s no shortage of opportunities to explore and unwind.

With its year-round climate, coastal lifestyle, and diverse neighborhoods, San Diego provides an inspiring backdrop for both learning and connection. After a day of intellectually engaging coursework and collaboration with peers, students can relax along the Pacific coastline or explore the city—creating an unforgettable summer experience with Summer Discovery.

## UC San Diego Programs

The UC San Diego program will not be offered for Summer 2026, with future sessions currently in development. [**Join our waitlist**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfhVNGIs8rkm1kIOec-Kmpe-QnsztaSsw49mqbkFyKQIFdUpA/viewform?usp=header) to stay informed and be among the first to hear when it returns.

Summer Discovery at UC San Diego offers an immersive academic experience through the Leadership for Impact: Strengthfinding Academy, designed for students eager to explore their strengths and apply them to real-world challenges. The program emphasizes collaborative discovery, critical thinking, and purposeful action through interactive lectures, workshops, simulations, and design challenges.

Students engage with case studies of prominent leaders and changemakers who have driven meaningful social change, gaining insight into how values, empathy, and trust play a role in effective leadership. With a strong focus on self-reflection and problem-solving, participants learn how to identify real issues in their own communities and mobilize others toward positive impact.

Through hands-on coursework and collaborative projects, students build practical skills while developing a deeper understanding of how leadership can be used as a tool for change. The program is structured to encourage intellectual curiosity, personal growth, and meaningful engagement—supported by full access to UC San Diego’s university facilities for a truly immersive pre-college experience.
