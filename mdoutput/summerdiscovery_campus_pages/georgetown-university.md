# Summer at Georgetown University

**For over 30 years, Summer Discovery’s partnership with Georgetown University has represented the very best in academic excellence, with courses and academies for high school and middle school students that are designed and taught by Georgetown University expert instructors.**

**The Summer Discovery program at Georgetown University is designed to offer a comprehensive educational experience that extends beyond traditional classroom learning. Students engage in a diverse curriculum taught by distinguished faculty hand-selected by Georgetown, ensuring a high-quality education that fosters critical thinking and real-world application. The program’s emphasis on interdisciplinary studies allows students to explore various fields, develop a well-rounded academic foundation, and gain insights into potential future career paths.**

**The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors**

Georgetown University is the perfect fit for students who are seeking a balance between rigorous academics and dynamic extracurriculars. These students are motivated, academically driven, and want to experience Washington DC's rich culture and history.

Located in the heart of one of Washington, DC's oldest and most charming neighborhoods, Georgetown University provides an idyllic setting for students to learn and grow. The campus is surrounded by cobblestone streets, historic landmarks, and a picturesque waterfront, creating a vibrant and culturally rich environment. Students have access to countless cafes, shops, restaurants, and theaters, offering endless opportunities to explore and enjoy the community. This unique blend of academic rigor and cultural immersion makes the Summer Discovery program at Georgetown University an unparalleled experience for aspiring scholars.

Situated within a backdrop of stunning architectural beauty, Georgetown University is a warm and nurturing campus community ensconced in the heart of a global city. For nearly three decades, Summer Discovery has proudly partnered with Georgetown University, to deliver academics delivered by Georgetown University.

## Global Leadership Academy

This transformative two-week academy, developed by [Ideagen Global](https://www.ideagenglobal.com/2024globalpartnershipsummit), is crafted to empower the next generation of global leaders by exploring the intersection of real-world business practices and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Using the SDGs as a framework, students will engage in comprehensive case studies, dynamic interactive discussions, inspiring guest speaker sessions, and hands-on exercises to gain a deep understanding of how businesses can drive positive social and environmental impact while tackling global challenges.

The academy culminates in a capstone project where students take on the role of entrepreneurs, developing innovative and sustainable business plans that address real-world issues discussed throughout the course. This practical project not only reinforces the knowledge gained but also fosters critical thinking, creativity, and entrepreneurial skills.

Set in the vibrant heart of the nation's capital, the program includes tours of Washington, D.C., where students will examine the pivotal role of government in business relations. This unique combination of academic rigor and real-world application makes the Ideagen Global Academy an unparalleled opportunity. Apply today on Summer Discovery's website to secure your place in this impactful program.
