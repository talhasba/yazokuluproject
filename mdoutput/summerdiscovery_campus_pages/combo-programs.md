# Summer at Combo-Campus

From the vibrant energy of Los Angeles to the historic streets of Washington, D.C., and everything in between, Summer Discovery’s Combo Programs give students the chance to experience two incredible universities in one unforgettable summer. These programs combine academic exploration with travel, personal growth, and fun—perfect for students ready to see more of the world while preparing for college life. Mix and match your favorite university campuses to create the ultimate summer.

With over 50 years of pre-college expertise, Summer Discovery makes it easy to pair programs that fit your goals and schedule—like UCLA + University of Michigan, Yale University + Dartmouth College, Georgetown University + UC Berkeley, UC Berkeley + Dartmouth College, Real Madrid Graduate School + Dartmouth College, or Vanderbilt University + Georgetown University, and so many more. Each campus has its own character and community, giving students a chance to experience college life across the U.S. and Europe.

**Students who enroll in a Combo Program receive $750 off total tuition, plus an additional night of accommodation, meals, airport transfers, and one activity between campuses.** Our team takes care of every detail to ensure a smooth, supported transition between programs.

A Combo Program transforms one summer into two exciting chapters of learning, discovery, and connection—helping students build confidence, independence, and lifelong friendships along the way.

## Combo-Campus Programs

Summer Discovery’s Combo Programs let students experience life at two world-class universities in one unforgettable summer. It’s the perfect way to extend your learning, explore new destinations, and get a true feel for college life.

Students choose two programs with back-to-back dates—for example, beginning at UCLA and continuing at the University of Michigan, or starting at Yale University before heading to Dartmouth College. With dozens of campus options across the U.S. and Europe, there are endless ways to design your ideal summer experience.

Each campus offers a variety of courses—from business and law to medicine, technology, and the arts—so you can explore new interests or approach your favorite subject from a fresh perspective.

**Students who enroll in a Combo Program receive $750 off total tuition, plus accomodation between programs, meals, airport transfers, and one activity between campuses.** Our team takes care of every other detail to ensure a smooth, supported transition from one campus to the next. Flights aren’t included.

A Combo Program transforms one summer into two exciting chapters of learning and discovery—helping students grow academically, culturally, and personally along the way.

Explore some of our most popular pairings below, or [**view all program dates and tuition options**](https://www.summerdiscovery.com/offerings-dates-tuitions) to design your own perfect summer.
