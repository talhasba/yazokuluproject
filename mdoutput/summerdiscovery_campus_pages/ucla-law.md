# Summer at UCLA School of Law

UCLA and Summer Discovery have partnered in pre-college enrichment for over 35 years, providing students with an unparalleled academic experience, and the Summer Discovery UCLA Law program builds on that tradition. Through this exclusive two-week academic experience taught by distinguished UCLA and Loyola faculty, students are introduced to legal reasoning, courtroom advocacy, and trial law through immersive, real-world learning.

The collaboration between UCLA Law and Summer Discovery reflects a shared commitment to helping students explore the legal profession in a dynamic academic environment. Located in Los Angeles—a global center for entertainment, media, technology, and innovation—UCLA Law provides students with unique exposure to the industries and legal issues shaping today’s world. This setting encourages analytical thinking, persuasive communication, and hands-on learning both inside and outside the classroom.

### The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors

UCLA Law is designed for students who are intellectually curious, motivated, and eager to explore law, advocacy, and public speaking. The program attracts students interested in debate, justice, entertainment law, business, and communication, offering immersive academic experiences that strengthen critical thinking and real-world problem-solving skills.

Situated in one of the world’s leading centers for entertainment, business, and culture, UCLA Law offers students innovative, real-world-relevant coursework connected to the industries surrounding them. Beyond the classroom, students experience the energy of Los Angeles through exciting excursions, cultural landmarks, beaches, sporting events, and iconic attractions—from Santa Monica and Malibu to Universal Studios, the Hollywood Sign, and Dodger Stadium—creating a memorable and enriching summer experience.

## UCLA School of Law Programs

UCLA School of Law offers immersive courses for students interested in legal studies, advocacy, public speaking, and more. In the specialized program Trial Law & Advocacy, students are introduced to the foundations of legal reasoning through hands-on learning and real-world case studies.

Through interactive classroom experiences, mock trials, legal analysis, and discussions centered on current issues in entertainment, media, technology, and business, students develop critical thinking, persuasive communication, and problem-solving skills essential to the legal profession and beyond.

Students who complete the program will receive an official certificate of completion from UCLA School of Law, a distinguished addition to their academic portfolio.

-

## Trial Law & Advocacy

Step into the courtroom—no bench warrant required! Through lectures, hands-on practice, mock trials, and courtroom field trips, students learn how to craft persuasive opening statements, conduct direct and cross-examinations, handle objections and evidence, and deliver compelling closing arguments. Ideal for students interested in law, public speaking, or justice.

**Instructor:** John Henry — Director of Trial Advocacy, Loyola Law School

A Loyola Law graduate and former prosecutor at the LA County and Riverside County DA’s offices, John tried approximately 80 jury trials and rose to Chief Deputy District Attorney — the first African American on his office’s executive team. He built Loyola’s Byrne Trial Advocacy Team into a nationally ranked program, earning multiple regional and national championships, before transitioning to full-time faculty in 2025.
