# Summer at Management & Technology Summer Institute (M&TSI)

Run by the Jerome Fisher Program in Management & Technology in partnership with Penn Engineering and the Wharton School, M&TSI features classes taught by leading Penn faculty and successful entrepreneurs, an intensive team prototyping project, and other activities designed to give students the opportunity to learn about the principles and practice of technological innovation. Courses are taught by full-time Wharton and Penn Engineering faculty, and students explore Penn's business and engineering offerings, hearing guest lectures from prominent faculty recognized as leaders in their field.

Students will earn college credit while developing high-tech ventures and receiving feedback from Penn faculty and seasoned entrepreneurs. Connections with M&T Program and Penn Alumni, as well as industry leaders at firms like Google and Morgan Stanley, help students establish a network that extends beyond the classroom.

To learn more, please visit https://fisher.wharton.upenn.edu/management-technology-summer-institute/

## Included in Tuition

- Academic tuition
- One college credit unit from the School of Engineering and Applied Sciences
- Housing
- 2 meals per day from Sunday - Friday (no meals on Saturday due to excursions/activities)
- Extracurricular activities and excursions every afternoon, evening, and all weekend long
- Transportation for included activities
- Safety and supervision
-

Note: some courses and academies have additional academic fees

-

Contact a program specialist for more information at 516-414-5555

## Not Included in Tuition

- Application fee
- Airfare and airport transfers
- Supplemental course fees may apply
- Meals eaten off campus
- Pottruck Gym access
- Medical expenses
- Laundry
- Linens
- Spending money
-

For international students, the international registration fee is $379 — this fee covers a medical policy for the student and their airport transfers
