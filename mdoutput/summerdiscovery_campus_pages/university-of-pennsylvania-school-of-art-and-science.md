# Summer at University of Pennsylvania School of Arts and Sciences

Ready for a first-rate summer experience at America’s first university? Founded by Benjamin Franklin in 1740, Penn offers an authentic Ivy League experience in the heart of Philadelphia for academically gifted high school students. Penn flourishes in and is enriched by a vibrant urban ecosystem. Explore America’s history in Philly’s urban setting and feel the pulse of arts, culture, and innovation.

Engage with leading faculty and build intellectual connections while taking challenging courses selected from a variety of disciplines. The School of Arts and Sciences is widely known for its expertise and students can immerse themselves in a wide range of courses from across disciplines.

## University of Pennsylvania School of Arts and Sciences Programs

Penn Arts and Sciences High School Programs welcome bright and ambitious high school students from around the world to experience the Ivy League. Whether you’re ready for an immersive pre-collegiate experience, looking for advanced studies in a field that fascinates you, or planning to get a head start on college admissions by earning academic credit, we have a program for you.

## Included in Tuition

- Academic tuition
- Use of Penn academic facilities
- Housing
- Laundry
-

Most meals included on campus. No meals provided during excursion days

- Extracurricular activities and excursions every afternoon, evening, and all weekend long
- Transportation for included activities
-

Use of athletic and recreational facilities for students 15 years of age or older

- 1 ea fitted sheet & flat sheet - Twin XL, 1 pillow, 1 lightweight blanket
-

2 bath towels, 1 hand towel, 1 washcloth

- Safety and supervision

## Not Included in Tuition

- Application fee
-

Train transfers, airport transfers, and airfare

- Supplemental course fees may apply
- Meals eaten off campus
- Medical expenses
- Spending money
-

For students who reside outside of the USA, the international registration fee is $379, which students are responsible for covering

-

Cost of airport transfers

-

Note: the best airport to fly into for the University of Pennsylvania School of Arts and Sciences is Philadelphia International Airport (PHL)
