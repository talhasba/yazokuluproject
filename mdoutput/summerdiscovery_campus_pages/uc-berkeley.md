# Summer at UC Berkeley

**Summer Discovery at UC Berkeley enables students to immerse themselves in the university’s world-class academic environment through direct access to laboratories, maker spaces and a wealth of hands-on lab work, specimen dissections, medical research, and the tactile aspects of engineering and robotics.**

**Perennially ranked at the top of the nation’s best public universities, UC Berkeley is widely acclaimed for its distinguished programs in engineering, computer science, and business, maintaining a global reputation for excellence.**

**The Summer Discovery at UC Berkeley program offers a diverse array of courses and academies that present fascinating active-learning experiences. Students can participate in the highly selective Berkeley M.E.T. Innovation Academy, engaging in a hands-on curriculum that positions them at the forefront of technology and entrepreneurship.**

**The #1 Pre-College Enrichment Program Recommended by High School College Counselors**

UC Berkeley is the perfect fit for academically motivated students who are passionate about hands-on, project-based learning. They embrace the challenge of tackling in-depth, classroom-driven projects and excel at managing their time to meet the demands of a rigorous academic setting. Driven by curiosity and determination, these students are eager to push their boundaries and explore complex ideas while navigating a fast-paced, intellectually stimulating environment.

Situated on a sprawling 1,232-acre campus, this esteemed public institution fosters a robust start-up culture, with many successful tech companies originating from within its entrepreneurial ecosystem. Set amidst a vibrant urban setting, the campus offers stunning views of the San Francisco Bay. Nearby Telegraph Avenue features delightful bookstores, cafes, and street vendors, all steeped in a storied counter-cultural heritage. Beyond academics, UC Berkeley provides a plethora of recreational and cultural activities, ensuring a dynamic student experience both on and off campus.

## UC Berkeley Programs

Summer Discovery’s UC Berkeley program offers high school students an immersive, academy-style learning experience that blends hands-on activities, group projects, and real-world applications, allowing them to come out of the program with a solid understanding and knowledge base that will serve them well in college and beyond.

From the highly selective M.E.T. Innovation Academy run by UC Berkeley's College of Engineering and Haas Business School, to Summer Discovery programs in Psychiatry and Neuroscience, Research in Medicine, or Engineering and Robotics, students explore fields that ignite their passions and build a foundation for future careers. The Emergency Medicine and Health Sciences Academy, for example, offers students a direct, tactile opportunity to gain a comprehensive understanding of the daily responsibilities of emergency healthcare professionals, including dissections and site visits to local hospitals.

All courses are taught by passionate and accredited instructors in their field of study with students benefiting from exposure to eminent professors and researchers and a culture driven by technology and innovation and the influence of industry leaders from the Bay Area’s dynamic start-ups and corporate giants. Summer Discovery students at UC Berkeley have the unique opportunity to experience firsthand the university’s academic prestige with unparalleled access to labs, maker spaces, and other resources.

**At the conclusion of the program, students receive a resumé-boosting Certificate of Completion and a letter of recognition for each course taken.**

## Berkeley M.E.T. Innovation Academy

The Berkeley M.E.T. Innovation Academy (M.E.T.ia) is a premier residential summer program for rising high school juniors and seniors eager to lead and be at the forefront of tech and entrepreneurship. This highly selective program is hosted by UC Berkeley’s renowned College of Engineering and Haas School of Business. M.E.T.ia offers an immersive, hands-on academic experience. This program offers a unique opportunity for those who are top-tier in their high school and possess strong skills in science, technology, math, and communications. It’s perfect for students fueled by extraordinary ambition and an unwavering commitment to changing the world through technological innovation. This pre-collegiate summer program will launch those who possess an unwavering pursuit of hands-on applications of engineering and entrepreneurial knowledge to the next level.

Medicine & Health Sciences Academy

This comprehensive healthcare exploration program offers a transformative academic experience designed to deepen understanding and apply essential skills vital for success in health professions. Led by experts, action-based simulations provide hands-on exposure to the daily routines of healthcare professionals. Activities range from suturing and dissection to trauma response and clinical diagnostics, supplemented by engaging case studies and engaging class discussions.

Participants embark on on and off-campus site visits to state-of-the-art simulation labs and healthcare facilities, gaining invaluable insights from panels featuring professionals at various career stages. This immersive approach not only enhances technical proficiency but also fosters critical thinking and teamwork essential for thriving in healthcare environments. Whether aspiring to work in medicine, nursing, or allied health fields, this program equips students with practical knowledge and a firsthand glimpse into the fast-paced world of healthcare practice and advancement.

**Psychiatry & Neuroscience Academy**

In Psychiatry and Neuroscience at UC Berkeley, students will address topics such as the biology of mental illness, psychopharmacology, and the ethical considerations involved in treating patients with mental health disorders. Interactive learning techniques may involve role-playing exercises, case studies, and laboratory sessions that will help students refine skills in patient communication, critical thinking, and problem-solving.

With the Summer Discovery Psychiatry and Neuroscience Academy, you’ll understand the paths to and attributes of successful health professionals in the “brain sciences” (e.g. psychiatry, neurology, psychology, neuroscience), hear and learn from the lived experiences of current health professionals and students, and identify aspects of careers in healthcare to envision your own personal journey towards becoming a future “brain science” health professional.

![block image](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.summerdiscovery.com%2Fcampus%2F2%2FflexBlock%2Fimages%2F1732654657459_6.jpg&w=3840&q=75)


**Artificial Intelligence**

With this AI course, students will work on a mentor-led group project in a domain of their choice and present their work to peers and instructors.

The course will start with students collaborating in small groups to complete an AI project that sits at the intersection of AI and other domains, such as healthcare, art + entertainment, political science, astronomy, finance, sustainability, and more. Students are placed in project groups based on their responses to the project preference survey they receive before the course begins. Projects culminate in a presentation on the final day, in which students present their research findings and practice spoken scientific communication skills.

Engineering & Robotics Academy

This course explores the transformative impact of robotics across industries, covering its history, evolution, and the latest trends like AI-driven automation and soft robotics. Students will engage in hands-on projects to build and program robotic systems, learning key concepts such as circuitry, sensors, and mechanical design. The curriculum includes guest speakers from Silicon Valley, field trips to top tech companies, and a Capstone project where students develop innovative robotic solutions to real-world challenges. Designed for aspiring STEM students, this course offers an inspiring introduction to robotics and engineering, with no prior experience required.

![block image](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.summerdiscovery.com%2Fcampus%2F2%2FflexBlock%2Fimages%2F1732715772882_0.jpg&w=3840&q=75)


Research in Medicine

At UC Berkeley, Summer Discovery participants will embark on a transformative two-week journey into modern medicine, exploring cutting-edge techniques through a hands-on learning approach. The program combines interactive lectures from renowned medical experts with visits to state-of-the-art research facilities, offering students a comprehensive understanding of the medical research field. Participants will develop critical thinking and academic research skills as they create their own research project proposals, which are submitted for review on the final in-person day.

Following the on-campus experience, students join a virtual cohort, where they are paired 1:1 with a research mentor aligned with their interests. Over 10 virtual sessions, students work at their own pace to complete their projects, which can be submitted to prestigious journals, shared at symposiums, or transformed into creative outputs like podcasts. Conducted in partnership with Polygence, the premier online research academy, the program includes both the immersive on-campus experience and Polygence’s Core Research Program, ensuring dedicated mentorship from expert academics.
