# Summer at Bryn Mawr College

**Summer Discovery at Bryn Mawr College is tailored to gifted and talented commuter students in grades 1-8.**

Known for its historic sites and enchanting architecture reminiscent of Hogwarts, Bryn Mawr College has proudly hosted our gifted and talented program for more than 33 years. This picturesque and intellectually stimulating environment provides the perfect backdrop for our proprietary STEAM+ curriculum, meticulously designed for precocious young minds and accelerated learners.

Our program upholds a legacy of innovation, offering an array of programs that challenge students to stretch their abilities, expand their knowledge, and celebrate their unique talents.

## Bryn Mawr College Programs

Our proprietary STEAM+ curriculum focuses on applying creative thinking across multiple disciplines to solve real-world problems. Academic classes offer a variety of interdisciplinary studies in the fields of science, technology, engineering, mathematics, arts, humanities, fitness, and recreation with the goal of developing both the intellectual, social, and emotional needs of high-potential youth. The program fosters critical and creative thinking skills through hands-on multidisciplinary curriculum and challenges students to push the limits of their knowledge. Students are encouraged to embrace their individuality and to thrive in a community of inclusive and like-minded peers.

Each course is thoughtfully crafted to meet the intellectual, social, and emotional needs of precocious young learners, fostering an environment where students can thrive and excel. The curriculum is designed not only to enhance academic prowess but also to develop critical thinking, creativity, and collaborative skills.

Students engage in hands-on learning experiences, collaborative projects, and in-depth research, all under the guidance of distinguished faculty and industry experts. This immersive approach ensures that students are not just passive recipients of information but active participants in their educational journey. By embracing this holistic approach, Summer Discovery’s Institute for the Gifted program at Fairfield nurtures well-rounded individuals prepared to make meaningful contributions to their communities and the world.

**Upon successful completion of this program, students receive a branded Certificate of Completion and letter of recognition for each course taken—a prestigious addition to any academic portfolio.**

## Middle School

Summer Discovery's middle school program transcends the traditional classroom, offering a dynamic and personalized learning experience that empowers you to explore your passions and discover new talents. Choose the courses that spark your curiosity, from delving into the intricacies of law and government to uncovering the mysteries of forensic science.

Your summer experience will not only be filled with immersive academic courses, but it will also be balanced with opportunities to select from daily recreational activities in the arts, health, and sports, and embark on exciting off-campus excursions. Our middle school program is designed to ignite your passions and expand your horizons.
