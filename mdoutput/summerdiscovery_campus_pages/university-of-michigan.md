# Summer at University of Michigan

Summer Discover at the University of Michigan offers a comprehensive selection of courses taught by industry experts. Fields of Study include sports management, medicine, psychology, entrepreneurship, and various STEM options. Students engage with their peers through hands-on, project based learning in their areas of interest, with popular courses in psychology, personal finance & investment, business and entrepreneurship, careers in medicine, and mechanical engineering.

The curriculum is designed to challenge and inspire, catalyzing both intellectual and personal growth. Whether commuting or living on campus, weeklong courses enable students to start at the beginning of any week over the summer, so they can mix and match two or three unique courses in their personally designed two- or three-week session.

## University of Michigan Programs

Summer Discover at the University of Michigan offers a comprehensive array of courses taught by industry experts. Fields of Study include sports management, medicine, psychology, entrepreneurship, and STEM. Popular courses include social psychology, personal finance & investment, and mechanical engineering, among others. Timely and enticing course titles include “CSI: Forensic Science”, “Climate Change & Environmental Sustainability”, and “Business Disruptors: Tesla to Nvidia”.

The curriculum is designed to challenge and inspire, catalyzing both intellectual and personal growth. Whether commuting or living on campus, weeklong courses enable students to start at the beginning of any week over the summer, so they can mix and match two or three unique courses in their personally designed two- or three-week session.

**Upon successful completion of this program, students receive a Certificate of Completion and a letter of recognition for each course taken—a prestigious addition to any academic portfolio.**

## Featured Course: Day Trading in Today's Futures Market

Co-designed with NYSE icon Peter Tuchman and Wall Street Global Trading Academy co-founder David Green, this immersive one-week course guides student to think, speak, and act like professional futures traders. Through hands-on activities and simulations, students master the art of futures trading. The program culminates in the APEX Trader Funding evaluation challenge — giving students a tangible goal and a clear path to keep trading, and earning, when they return home.

## Included in Tuition

- Academic tuition
- Housing
- 2-3 meals per day, 7 days per week (varies by program)
- Extracurricular activities and excursions every afternoon, evening, and all weekend long
- Transportation for included activities
- Use of athletic and recreation facilities
- College admissions presentation
- Blanket and pillow
- Safety and supervision
- Note: some courses and academies have additional academic fees.
- Contact a program specialist for more information at +1 (516) 447-4907.

## Not Included in Tuition

- Application fee
- Airfare and airport transfers
- Supplemental course fees may apply
- Meals eaten off campus
- Medical expenses
- Laundry
- Linens
- Spending money
- For international students, the international registration fee is $379 (residential) OR $239 (commuter) – this fee covers a medical policy for the student and their airport transfers.
