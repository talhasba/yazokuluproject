# Summer at Culinary Institute of America

Discover the art and science of culinary excellence in one of the world's most presti﻿gious culinary settings - the Culinary Institute of America Culinary Academy. This immersive seven-day program at The Culinary Institute of America's Hyde Park campus brings curious high school students into the professional kitchens and teaching spaces where tomorrow's culinary leaders are trained.

Set on 170 stunning acres overlooking the Hudson River, CIA's Hyde Park campus offers far more than just a cooking class. This is an authentic culinary education experience, taught by the same renowned chefs and faculty who train professional culinary students year-round. The Hudson Valley's rich agritourism landscape, farm-to-table culture, and culinary heritage create the perfect backdrop for students exploring food as craft, culture, and potential career.

Students spend their days in CIA's professional teaching kitchens, building genuine skills through progressive, hands-on learning. They master core techniques—from precision knife skills and professional cooking methods to understanding flavor profiles, seasoning, and plating. The curriculum moves through global culinary traditions, exploring everything from Italian regional specialties to French bistro classics and dynamic Asian flavors, while also delving into the artistry of baking and pastry, from artisanal bread to contemporary plated desserts.

But this experience teaches more than recipes. Students learn how professional kitchens operate, how flavors are constructed, and how food connects creativity, culture, and commerce. They develop collaboration and time-management skills in fast-paced environments, building confidence as they take on increasing independence and complexity throughout the week.

The program culminates in a celebratory banquet dinner and official Toque Ceremony, where students receive their chef's hat—a meaningful symbol of their entry into the culinary world. Whether already passionate about food or simply curious to explore, students leave with authentic skills, expanded perspective, and a genuine understanding of what culinary arts and hospitality can offer.
