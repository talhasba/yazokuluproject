# Summer at The Academies by Harvard Student Agencies

The Academies by Harvard Student Agencies offer high school students the opportunity to experience academic life on one of the world’s most renowned university campuses. Hosted in Harvard classrooms throughout the summer, students are immersed in a collegiate learning environment while studying alongside motivated peers who share their academic interests.

Located in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the program places students at the heart of Harvard’s historic campus and just minutes from downtown Boston. Summer in Cambridge is warm and lively, with tree-lined paths, open green spaces, and a walkable campus that blends centuries-old architecture with a dynamic student atmosphere. The surrounding city offers a rich mix of history, innovation, and culture—creating an energizing backdrop for academic exploration.

Beyond the classroom, students connect with Harvard undergraduate mentors who provide academic support and insight into college life, while also enjoying a range of on-campus and local experiences. From campus tours and guest speakers to exploring Boston’s neighborhoods, museums, and landmarks, the setting and community combine to create an inspiring summer experience rooted in curiosity, connection, and discovery.

## The Academies by Harvard Student Agencies Programs

The Academies by Harvard Student Agencies are on-campus academic enrichment programs for high school students, hosted in Harvard classrooms throughout the summer. Designed for motivated learners, these week-long programs run between June and August and offer an immersive academic experience across **Business**, **Economics and Finance**, **Pre-Med**, **Government**, and **Coding**. Students engage deeply with their chosen subject area through full-day sessions in a structured, interactive university-style learning environment.

Each Academy includes **forty hours of programming**, combining engaging lectures, keynote talks from professionals in the field, field trips, and a collaborative final project or presentation. Courses are taught by Harvard undergraduate mentors, giving students the opportunity to learn from and connect with current Harvard students while gaining insight into academic pathways and college life. Through hands-on, discussion-based instruction, students build both subject knowledge and critical thinking skills.

Beyond the classroom, the Academies emphasize community and connection. Students participate in on-campus experiences such as tours of Harvard College and graduate schools, guest speaker sessions, and panels focused on the college application process. Select programs also include academic field trips—such as government-focused visits to the Massachusetts State House—adding real-world context to classroom learning.

Students can choose from **10 distinct Academies**, including **Pre-Med: Biology & Chemistry**, **Pre-Med: Anatomy & Physiology**, **Government: Pre-Law**, **Government: International Politics**, **Business: Entrepreneurship**, **Business: Management & Strategy**, **Coding: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence**, and **Coding: Introduction to Python**, among others. This range of offerings allows students to pursue their interests in depth while experiencing the rigor, mentorship, and collaborative spirit of university-level study.

## Pre-Med: Biology & Chemistry

The Pre-Med: Biology and Chemistry Academy provides an opportunity for students to learn about genetics, immunology, CRISPR, and organic chemistry. Students gain important medical background and take a deep dive into various diseases. The curriculum helps students better understand what being pre-med entails and culminates in a final presentation on a disease or genetic disorder, bringing together the skills learned throughout the program.

[Learn More](https://academies.hsa.net/)

-

## Business: Entrepreneurship

The Business: Entrepreneurship Academy teaches the fundamentals of launching a business while extending students’ financial literacy through hands-on exploration of the startup world. Students develop analytical thinking skills and gain practical experience building and refining ideas. Led by Harvard student instructors, the program culminates in a final pitch, where students present an innovative business concept to judges, simulating a startup presentation to investors.

[Learn More](https://academies.hsa.net/)

-

## Government: Pre-Law

The Government: Pre-Law Academy introduces students to the U.S. legal system, Supreme Court cases, and different types of law. Students learn how to analyze cases and legal documents while developing critical thinking and reading skills. The program culminates in a mock trial, where students take on the roles of lawyers, witnesses, or judges to simulate a real legal proceeding.

[Learn More](https://academies.hsa.net/)
