# Summer at University of Pennsylvania Carey Law School

High-achieving high school students with a passion for justice and a thirst for learning about law, government, and our legal system are invited to spend an intellectually stimulating summer at the University of Pennsylvania Carey Law School's Pre-College Academy. Students will have the unique opportunity to delve into the intricacies of the American legal system through an accelerated version of the first-year law school curriculum.

In this highly selective program, applicants are hand-selected by the University of Pennsylvania Carey Law School Legal Education Programs staff. Under the guidance of world-renowned faculty, thought leaders, seasoned practitioners, all faculty of this esteemed institution, you will engage with coursework similar to the J.D. students, gaining a comprehensive understanding of legal theory and practice.

Students in this program will have access to mock trial rooms, visit the James A. Byrne U.S. Federal Courthouse, and more curated visits designed to complement the in-classroom learning portion of the program. This exceptional program will not only challenge you intellectually but also provide you with invaluable insights into the diverse pathways available within the legal profession. After successfully completing this program, students will receive a Certificate of Completion from the Penn Law Academy.

- Housing
- Laundry
- 3 meals per day from Monday - Friday, two meals on Sunday (no meals on Saturday due to excursions/activities)
- Extracurricular activities and excursions every afternoon, evening, and all weekend long
- Transportation for included activities

- Application fee
- Airfare and airport transfers
- Supplemental course fees may apply
- Meals eaten off campus
- Medical expenses
- Linens
- Spending money
