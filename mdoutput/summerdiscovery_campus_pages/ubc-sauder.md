# Summer at UBC Sauder School of Business

Step into the world of business and discovery what it's like to study at one of the world's top 50 universities, as ranked by Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2025. UBC Sauder School of Business gives high-potential school students a taste of college life while exploring business, leadership, and entrepreneurship at one of Canada’s top universities. At the UBC Sauder Business Academy, students dive into engaging, interdisciplinary courses alongside peers from around the globe. They learn directly from world-renowned faculty who bring cutting-edge research and real-world industry expertise into the classroom.

Located on the Vancouver campus of the University of British Columbia - named the most beautiful university in Canada by Times Higher Education - the UBC Sauder Business Academy offers students the chance to learn in one of the most inspiring settings in North America, surrounded by ocean, mountains, and forest.

Outside the classroom, there’s so much to discover. From exploring downtown Vancouver or hiking nearby trails, students enjoy the perfect balance of academics and adventure.

This unforgettable experience builds confidence, independence, and lifelong skills — preparing students for success in college and beyond.

## UBC Sauder School of Business Programs

The UBC Sauder Business Academy run by the UBC Sauder School of Business gives high school students the chance to explore business, leadership, and entrepreneurship at one of Canada’s top universities. In rigorous courses taught by renowned faculty of UBC Sauder School of Business, students will build the foundation for a successful future in today's dynamic economy.

All students take the Business Fundamentals: Leadership for the Future course in the morning, followed by their choice of an afternoon elective, with options in Finance & Investing, Data Analytics, Marketing & Branding, and Entrepreneurship.

This program is perfect for students who want a head start on an undergraduate degree in business or may be considering a future career in business.

Students develop real-world skills through interactive lessons, collaborative projects, and hands-on activities that bring business concepts to life. Courses are designed to spark curiosity, creativity, and confidence while helping students think like future leaders and innovators.

Classes take place on the beautiful University of British Columbia campus, with access to outstanding facilities and a welcoming academic community.

Each course provides the perfect balance of challenge and fun — preparing students for success in high school, college, and beyond.

## UBC Sauder Business Academy

**Business shapes everything — learn the keys to success.**

Discover what it takes to lead in today’s fast-changing global economy. In this hands-on program at the world-renowned UBC Sauder School of Business, students explore the principles of business management, leadership, and entrepreneurship through real-world case studies and collaborative projects. Learn to think strategically, make data-driven decisions, and develop creative solutions to business challenges. Designed for motivated high school students, this program blends classroom learning with practical skill-building, preparing participants to excel in future academic and professional pursuits. Students will attend an all-group morning lecture followed by an afternoon elective track in their chosen area of focus.

**Morning Lecture - Business Fundamentals: Leadership for the Future**

For the morning lecture, students will step into the world of business and build the foundation for future success in today’s dynamic economy. In this engaging introduction to modern business and leadership, students will explore how organizations create value, make decisions, and compete in today's economy. As the core of the UBC Sauder Business Academy, it's where your summer starts — and where your business future begins.

## Investment Fundamentals: Finance, Valuation, and the Financial System

**Learn how money really works.** This afternoon elective course for students interested in finance breaks down how companies are valued, how investors think, and how the financial system operates — using real tools, real data, and real decisions. From reading financial statements to mastering time value of money and market multiples, every concept builds toward one goal: a live stock pitch where you analyze a publicly traded company and make your case. No finance background required — just the drive to figure it out.

-

## The Startup Mindset: New Venture Creation

**Think like a founder. Build like one too.** Experience the entrepreneurial process from idea generation to investor presentation and gain both the mindset and practical toolkit to launch and evaluate new ventures. This afternoon elective course drops high school students into the startup world from day one — covering how entrepreneurs identify opportunities, launch ventures, and attract investors. Master the tools that matter: customer discovery, business model design, market sizing, and go-to-market strategy. Learn how early-stage investors think so you can pitch with confidence. The course culminates in a live startup pitch and venture brief — your chance to prove your idea is ready for the real world.

-

## Business Data Analytics

**Data runs the world — learn to use it.** Develop essential analytical and digital skills for the emerging AI-driven economy and engage with the technologies shaping the future of business and society. This afternoon elective gives students a hands-on introduction to business analytics, from making sense of complex data to predicting trends and driving smarter decisions. Move through descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics using real tools and techniques — including machine learning and AI. Every concept connects directly to how today's top businesses operate. If you want to think sharper and work smarter, this is where it starts.

-

## Marketing and Brand Strategy

**Great brands don't happen by accident.** Gain hands-on experience developing campaigns, analyzing audience insights, and understanding how brands grow in a digital-first world. This afternoon elective gives students a practical introduction to marketing strategy and brand building in a digital-first world. Explore how companies identify customers, craft positioning, and design campaigns that drive real engagement. Dig into the psychology behind consumer behavior, the power of social media, and the data behind modern marketing. The course culminates in a collaborative brand campaign — your strategy, your creative, your pitch.
