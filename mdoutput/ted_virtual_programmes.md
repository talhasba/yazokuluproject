## How the virtual public speaking programme is structured

##### **Live online sessions**

Daily live classes bring the cohort together for interactive, practical learning. These sessions focus on discussion, practising delivery, and giving and receiving personalised feedback.

##### **Self-paced learning**

Students explore TED-curated content through a dedicated online platform and complete structured tasks that support the development of their talk step by step.

##### **Final showcase**

At the end of the two weeks, each student presents the TED-style talk they have developed and refined. It can also be added to their personal portfolio for any future applications.
