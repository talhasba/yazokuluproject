## Where you’ll stay

Barnard College, NY, United States

Students stay at Barnard College, part of Columbia University, located in the heart of Manhattan. This Ivy League setting offers a true New York academic experience - with world-class museums, Broadway theatres, and historic neighbourhoods a short walk or subway ride away. Accommodation includes modern rooms, Wi-Fi, shared facilities, and 24/7 security. Male and female students are housed separately, fostering a safe, welcoming community for learning and connection.

### Exciting activities in

### New York

#### Feel the energy of New York

New York is a city full of surprises. Every street has its own character, and every neighbourhood has a story. With our summer courses in New York, you’ll experience the energy of its culture, meet people from all walks of life, and enjoy its amazing food. It’s a city where every moment feels alive.

#### Discover Art and History

New York’s museums offer more than just exhibits – they’re stories waiting to be explored. At the Metropolitan Museum of Art, you’ll see masterpieces from around the globe. The American Museum of Natural History will bring science and history to life. Each visit offers something new to think about and enjoy.

#### The best of New York City

Walk through Central Park, stand beneath the towering Empire State Building, and take in the vibrant atmosphere of Times Square. You’ll visit places that make New York unforgettable while connecting your learning to the real world.
