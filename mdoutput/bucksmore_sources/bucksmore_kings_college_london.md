# Welcome to King's College London

Founded in 1829, King’s College London is one of the oldest university-level institutions in England.

Accommodation is located in the University's student residence across Waterloo Bridge and amongst iconic London landmarks such as, the London Eye and the Southbank Centre.

## Where you'll sleep

You will stay in fully supervised residential accommodation with comfortable single ensuite bedrooms.

The accommodation building offers warm, welcoming spaces providing both personal privacy and a strong feeling of community. The single dorm rooms, separated by gender on each floor, are equipped with an ensuite bathroom, a single bed, desk, storage space, and wardrobe. Towels and bedding are provided, with regular housekeeping services.

![A single bedroom in King's College London accommodation block.](<./Summer Camps at King's College London, UK _ Bucksmore_files/KCL-25.07.24-128-scaled-optimized.jpg>)

## The food

We can cater for students with allergies or dietary requirements notified to us. Our menus reflect the diversity of our students, and vegetarian and vegan options are available at all meals.

Breakfast includes fruit, cereal, toast and a cooked option, while dinner includes multiple choices of main course, dessert and a salad bar. On excursion days, a packed lunch is provided.

Please contact us if you would like to discuss your dietary needs.

## Travel and transfers to King's College London

Bucksmore offers a free airport transfer service for all students at King's College London arriving and departing within our complimentary transfer window at London Heathrow (LHR) and London Gatwick (LGW).

Students requesting private transfers outside of these destinations and times will be subject to additional charges. You can find out more about how arrivals and departures work.
