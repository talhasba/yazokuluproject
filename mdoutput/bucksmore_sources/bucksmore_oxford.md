## A picturesque and scholarly city.

Oxford is most known for its University, one of the oldest in the world!

With picturesque streets, beautiful parks, and a rich academic tradition, it attracts visitors and scholars from around the world.

## Oxford Christchurch and the Museum of Oxford

During your studies, you will have the opportunity to explore the historic sights of Oxford, learning about what life can be like in one of the most-visited cities in the United Kingdom!

### Christchurch College

One of Oxford's most famous spots is Christchurch College, part of the University of Oxford. It has a long tradition of academic excellence and has produced many prominent figures, including prime ministers, writers, and scholars.

### Museum of Oxford

We'll also take you to the Museum of Oxford which showcases the city's history, culture, and community through engaging exhibits, artifacts, and interactive displays.

## Explore our venues in Oxford

We have two charming locations in Oxford over the summer.

You can choose from the enchanting St. Hilda's College, part of the **University of Oxford**. This is a quaint university campus, perfect for your Pre-University studies.

Alternatively, experience a remarkable independent school in Oxford. **d'Overbroeck's** provides contemporary classrooms and relaxed hangout spaces.

We are in Oxford city, but we also have our own space to relax, play sports, and focus on learning in a beautiful place with the River Cherwell flowing through and surrounded by the university's historic architecture.

[![Featured image for “Oxford”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/dOverbroecks-10-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### d'Overbroeck's Oxford](http://www.bucksmore.com/english-learning-locations/oxford/d-overbroecks-school/)

d'Overbroeck's is located in central Oxford and is a stunning combination of contemporary and traditional architecture. d'Overbroeck's is home to some of our challenging Young Professionals programmes.

St Hilda’s, part of the world-renowned University of Oxford, is a prestigious setting for a Bucksmore learning experience. Just being here will boost your confidence as you improve your English or prepare for university.
