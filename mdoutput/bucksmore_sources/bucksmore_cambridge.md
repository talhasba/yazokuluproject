# Welcome to Cambridge! Explore our Summer Pre-University Courses

Cambridge is one of the most historic and inspiring cities in the UK. It’s renowned for its world-famous university, stunning architecture, and riverside charm. Although you’ll be staying on campus, you’ll get the chance to experience the best of Cambridge on our carefully planned trips.

## A city full of history.

Cambridge is brimming with things to discover. From its historic colleges to the winding River Cam, it’s a city full of character, perfect for summer adventures.

With grand architecture all around and peaceful green spaces to relax in, there’s always something new to explore.

## Cambridge's Riverside History

One of Cambridge's most famous attractions is the River Cam. Known for its picturesque punting tours, this is a classic Cambridge experience where you can glide past historic colleges and beautiful bridges, taking in the city from the water.

### **King’s College Chapel**

A masterpiece of Gothic architecture and one of the most famous landmarks in Cambridge, known for its stunning stained glass and choral music.

### Cambridge University Botanic Garden

A peaceful place to explore, with over 8,000 plant species, glasshouses, and plenty of green space to relax.

## Your home from home

You'll be staying in St Catharine's College, a welcoming campus which is part of the University of Cambridge, located in the heart of the historic city centre. The college's traditional courts and tranquil gardens provide a picturesque backdrop for studying, with modern facilities and easy access to Cambridge’s shops, cafés, and iconic sights.
