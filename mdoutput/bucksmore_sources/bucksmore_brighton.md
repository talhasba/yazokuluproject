## A city full of fun.

Brighton is buzzing with cool things to do. From the pebbly beach to the lively pier, it’s a place full of energy, perfect for summer adventures.

With the sea on one side and beautiful green hills on the other, there’s plenty to see and explore.

## Brighton Pier and the seafront

One of Brighton’s most famous spots is the Brighton Pier. It’s literally a fun fair on stilts! With classic rides, fun arcades, and tasty snacks, it’s a must-see when we take trips into the city.

### Brighton Beach

Brighton’s beach is made of shiny pebbles, perfect for chilling out with friends or dipping your toes in the water. On a sunny day, there’s nothing better than hanging out on the beach after a day of exploring!

### The i360

We'll also take you on the i360, a futuristic-looking glass pod which rises to 162 metres above the beach. It's the world's tallest moving observation tower!

## Your home from home

You'll be staying 10 minutes' coach ride from Brighton in the peaceful, safe surroundings of our own private boarding school campus at OIC Brighton.

We think it's the best of both worlds.

We are so close to Brighton that it makes it very easy to have a half-day excursion there, but we also have our own (huge) space to relax, play sports, and focus on learning in a beautiful place surrounded by hills and woodland.
