# TED
Summer School

A transformative two-week summer programme for motivated students aged 15-18, built around the art of communication, storytelling, and public speaking.

[Enroll now](https://www.immerse.education/select-programme/filter/educational-model:1148/)

## Immersive residential experience

Through a blend of theory and practice, small-group collaboration, and 1:1 coaching, participants learn to shape powerful ideas and deliver them with clarity, confidence, and presence. The experience culminates in each student presenting their own TED-style talk to a live audience, captured on video.

## How the public speaking programme is structured

##### Core Theory

Daily interactive sessions, where students discover key frameworks for idea development, storytelling, communication, and presentation.

##### Practical Application

Students work in small groups to test their ideas, give and receive feedback, and refine their TED-style talk in a supportive and creative environment.

##### 1:1 Coaching

Throughout the programme, students receive personalised guidance and feedback from experienced tutors who help them navigate every stage of their talk.

##### Guest Speaker Sessions

Students will hear from inspiring guest speakers, including professionals, entrepreneurs, and storytellers who have presented their ideas on prominent stages.

##### Final Showcase

The programme culminates in a final showcase, where each student presents their TED-style talk to a live audience of peers, tutors, and invited guests.
