###  Explore International Relations with Cambridge summer programs

Gain a deeper understanding of global politics and diplomacy in the academic city of Cambridge. Immerse Education’s Cambridge international relations summer school offers two courses: one for students aged 13-15, introducing the basics of global politics, and another for students aged 16-18, focusing on advanced issues like international law and conflict resolution. Both international relations summer programmes in Cambridge equip students with analytical and communication skills, preparing them for further studies in international relations, politics, or diplomacy. Learn more below to explore which programme suits your academic interests.

##  Meet our International Relations tutors

 Our tutors have practical backgrounds in international organisations, politics, and diplomacy. They show global challenges using case studies. They connect theory with actual occurrences. Their objective is to support students' critical thinking and the development of compelling policy concepts. To help students in their studies, each tutor offers one-on-one counseling, including suggestions and criticism.

 Through discussions and practical exercises, they encourage students to explore their interests and build confidence in their knowledge. For those learning English as a second language, tutors offer extra help with diplomatic terms and key concepts. Lessons are clear, engaging, and designed to make complex topics easier to understand. Whether you are new to international relations or looking to expand your knowledge, our tutors are here to support you. They help students grow, ask important questions, and gain skills for the future.

##  Experience the summer of your life

####  Live and learn in a historic college

 At Immerse Education’s international relations summer course in Cambridge, students stay in one of Cambridge’s beautiful colleges, fully immersing themselves in university life. A few weeks before arrival, participants are assigned accommodation based on their subject and dates. If you're attending with a friend or sibling, let us know - we’ll do our best to place you together. Living on campus means you’ll experience the same traditions and surroundings as Cambridge students, making every moment of your stay feel special.

####  A taste of tradition

 Twice during the programme, you’ll take part in formal dining experiences, a cherished tradition at Cambridge. These three-course meals are held in the grand Formal Hall, where centuries of scholars have gathered before you. The first dinner welcomes you to the programme, and the second is a special celebration following your graduation.

####  Celebrate your achievements

 Your studying at Cambridge international relations summer school ends with a grand graduation ceremony in a historic hall. This special event brings together students, faculty, and guests to recognise your accomplishments. After receiving your certificate, you’ll enjoy one final formal dinner, reflecting on your experiences and celebrating with new friends.

####  Have a day out in London

 Step beyond Oxford with a full-day trip to London! Experience London’s famous landmarks and enjoy free time to explore the city at your own pace.

####  Afternoons and evenings full of activities

 There's always something going on outside of classes. Play zorb football, wager on the River Cam, or work through an exciting murder mystery at the college throughout the afternoon. Attend quiz nights, talent showcases, or guest speaker events in the evenings to unwind with your pals. These activities provide even more specialness to your summer, whether you're painting, participating in sports, or having a stimulating conversation.
