###  Explore the Engineering programme in Oxford

Explore the world of engineering in Oxford, a city known for innovation and excellence in science and technology. Immerse Education’s Engineering summer programme offers two courses: one for students aged 13-15, introducing fundamental engineering concepts, and another for students aged 16-18, focusing on advanced engineering topics like mechanics, electronics, and structural design. Both courses give students the opportunity to work on practical projects and develop problem-solving skills, preparing them for future academic and career pursuits in engineering. Learn more below to find out which programme supports your engineering goals.

##  Experience the summer of your life

####  Live on campus

 As part of this immersive experience, students will reside at one of Oxford’s colleges, assigned a few weeks prior to arrival based on their subject choice. The residential experience, which includes accommodations at an engineering summer school, fosters friendship among students and provides a glimpse into life as a student of Oxford engineering.

####  Fine dine in formal hall

 Formal dining is a highlight of this experience, with two traditional Oxford College dinners included. The first dinner welcomes students in their initial week, and the second follows the graduation ceremony. Smart attire is encouraged to make the most of these occasions, and it is an integral part of engineering summer schools in Oxford.

####  Graduation ceremony

 Celebrate your achievements in a historic Oxford hall, where students are recognised for their participation in Oxford engineering courses. The ceremony features motivational speeches and the awarding of certificates, offering a memorable conclusion to the programme. Following the ceremony, a formal dinner brings the Immerse Education programme to an elegant close.

####  Have a day out in London

 Step beyond Oxford with a full-day trip to London! Experience London’s famous landmarks and enjoy free time to explore the city at your own pace.

####  Sports, games & leisure

 Afternoons at the are filled with engaging activities like punting on the River Cherwell, zorb football, and a murder mystery night. These activities balance learning with relaxation, giving students a complete Oxford engineering summer school experience.

####  Evening events

 Evenings are dedicated to social events such as guest speakers, quiz nights, and sports tournaments, providing an opportunity to relax after a day. These events foster a sense of community within the engineering summer schools.
