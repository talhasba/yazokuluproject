## Explore the Criminology summer school in Oxford

 We empower participants with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to thrive in their chosen paths.

###  Explore the Criminology programme in Oxford

Study criminal behaviour, justice systems, and social impact at the Criminology Summer School in Oxford, designed for students aged 16-18. Explore how society defines crime and how criminal justice systems operate across the world. Examine real-life case studies and learn to analyse criminal motives, social inequality, and legal responses. Our criminology summer programs for high school students provide academic guidance from expert tutors, allowing them to explore complex criminological theory, forensic psychology, and legal frameworks through debate and structured learning. Oxford’s museums, landmarks and libraries offer thought-provoking settings in which to develop your critical thinking and research skills – ideal preparation for further study in law, sociology, or politics.

##  Meet some of our Criminology tutors

 Immerse Education’s criminology summer programs for high school students are taught by tutors with academic and applied experience in criminal justice, sociology, and forensic psychology. These experts bring deep insight into how crime is studied, how justice systems function, and how societal inequalities influence legal outcomes. Tutors in Oxford explore theoretical frameworks alongside real-world case studies, giving students a clear, structured understanding of criminological debates.

 Through expertly moderated discussion and workshops designed around each student's interest areas, tutors foster a strong culture of criticism and analysis. Their aim is not only to share knowledge but to encourage independent thinking and curiosity about the complexity of crime and justice in a global context. They’re also sensitive to students who may be new to this field, offering clear explanations and personalised support. Whether your interest lies in law, social sciences, or public policy, our tutors ensure every student leaves with new confidence.

## Where you will be staying

Participants will be based at Oxford University colleges, living and learning in the same historic surroundings as Oxford students. This unique residential experience allows you to fully immerse yourself in university life, form lasting friendships with peers from around the world, and gain authentic insight into life as an Oxford student. The experience of residing within the colleges at Oxford University is unparalleled. Each college possesses its own distinct history and architectural flair. Male and female students will have separate accommodation, and Wi-Fi is available at every college.

##  Experience the summer of your life

####  Live on campus

 The Oxford Creative Writing summer school offers a unique opportunity to live on campus in one of Oxford University’s historic colleges. Accommodation is arranged a few weeks before arrival, based on subject choice and programme dates, including those enrolled in creative writing. Special requests can be made if participants wish to stay with friends or siblings. Living within the university's halls adds a layer of authenticity to what is considered one of the best creative writing courses for teenagers.

####  Fine dine in formal hall

 Dining is a programme highlight, with two formal Oxford College dinners giving participants a true taste of Oxford tradition. These three-course meals in stunning dining halls allow Oxford Creative Writing students to bond and celebrate their shared passion. The first dinner welcomes participants during their first week, while the second coincides with the graduation ceremony, guaranteeing a memorable farewell.

####  Graduation ceremony

 The graduation ceremony celebrates students' achievements attending the Oxford Creative Writing summer school. Hosted in a historic Oxford hall, the event features inspirational speeches and the presentation of certificates. Often considered a standout feature of one of the best creative writing courses, this ceremony concludes with a formal dinner, creating an unforgettable ending to the Immerse Education programme.

####  Have a day out in London

 Step beyond Oxford with a full-day trip to London! Experience London’s famous landmarks and enjoy free time to explore the city at your own pace.

####  Sports, games & leisure

 Social activities at the Oxford Creative Writing school bring energy and fun to the programme. What can students look forward to? Outside the classroom, there’s punting on the River Cherwell, zorb football, and even a thrilling murder mystery night. Why are these activities important? They give students a chance to relax, have fun, and connect with others who share their passion for writing.

####  Evening events

 Evenings at the Oxford Creative Writing summer school are full of fun and creativity. What can students do? They can enjoy activities like guest speaker sessions, talent shows, cake painting, and quiz nights. Why are these events special? Oxford Creative Writing events give students a chance to relax and unwind after a busy day of learning. It’s a perfect balance of creativity and fun, allowing participants to enjoy their creative writing courses for teenagers while building connections with others.
