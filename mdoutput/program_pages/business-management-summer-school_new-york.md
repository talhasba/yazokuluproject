## Business Management summer school in New York

 We empower participants with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to thrive in their chosen paths.

###  Explore the Business Management programme in New York

Explore the vibrant world of business at our Business Management summer school in New York, a two-week programme designed for students aged 15–18. With its dynamic mix of theory and practical learning, this summer school offers participants an exciting opportunity to explore the principles of business management while experiencing the energy of one of the world’s most influential cities.

Whether you’re new to the subject or eager to deepen your knowledge, this programme caters to a range of experience levels. From foundational concepts to real-world applications, students will develop the confidence and skills to thrive in the competitive world of business. Immerse Education offers a wide range of business summer programmes for high school students across various locations. Check and find a programme that suits your needs and goals.

## Where you will be staying

Participants will stay in Barnard College, a part of Columbia University, situated in the heart of Manhattan. This prestigious location offers students an authentic Ivy League experience, surrounded by the cultural and intellectual energy of New York City. From iconic landmarks to vibrant neighbourhoods, students will enjoy access to world-class museums, theatres, and historic sites, just steps from their residence. Accommodation includes modern, comfortable rooms with shared facilities, Wi-Fi access, and 24/7 on-site security. Male and female students are housed separately. The residential setting fosters a strong sense of community, encouraging friendships and collaboration.

##  Experience the summer of your life

####  Study in a global financial hub

 New York City, home to iconic landmarks and the headquarters of leading global businesses, serves as the ideal backdrop for this transformative experience. During this business summer school in New York, students will explore the city’s bustling financial district, including Wall Street and the New York Stock Exchange, gaining first-hand insights into the corporate world.

####  Expand your perspective

 Meet students from across the globe who share your interest in business and entrepreneurship. Through interactive group projects and collaborative workshops, you’ll have the chance to exchange ideas, learn from diverse perspectives, and build a network that spans continents. These connections often extend far beyond the summer programme, creating lasting friendships.

####  Learn practical leadership skills

 Leadership is at the heart of this business summer programme. Through hands-on activities and simulations, you’ll develop key skills such as decision-making, communication, and teamwork. Whether pitching a business idea or solving a management challenge, these experiences will prepare you for future academic and professional pursuits.

####  Explore the city that never sleeps

 Beyond academics, this programme offers the chance to experience the cultural richness of New York. Guided visits to world-famous attractions, from Central Park to Times Square, provide an exciting glimpse into life in this bustling metropolis. The memories you make here will last a lifetime.

####  Graduation ceremony and dinner

 The Graduation Ceremony marks the completion of your journey at our Business Management Summer School in New York. This special event is a celebration of your achievements, set in an inspiring venue where faculty, peers, and industry guests come together to recognise your progress. Thought-provoking speeches, certificate presentations, and a refined dinner create an ideal setting for reflection and networking.
