## Oxford Medicine summer school

 We empower participants with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to thrive in their chosen paths.

###  Explore the Medicine programme in London

Experience the world of modern medicine at the Medicine Summer School in London, crafted for students aged 15-18 preparing for future medical studies. Explore anatomy, diagnostics, ethics, and clinical decision-making through lectures, case discussions, and hands-on workshops. Learn to assess symptoms, interpret test results, and understand the complexities of patient care. With expert academic mentors, you’ll explore the skills and knowledge needed for success in university-level medicine. London’s status as a global leader in healthcare makes it the ideal location to begin your academic medical journey. Expand your understanding of modern medical challenges and develop insight into a field that shapes lives worldwide.

##  Meet your Medicine tutors

 Immerse Education’s Medicine Summer School in London is led by experienced educators and clinicians who bring academic rigour and real-world insight into the classroom. Students aged 16-18 are guided through core concepts in anatomy, clinical reasoning, diagnostics, and medical ethics using a mix of lectures, hands-on workshops, and structured case studies. Tutors, many of whom have worked or trained in leading hospitals and research institutions, help students understand how medical theory applies in practice.

 With a focus on clarity, precision, and compassion, they adapt their teaching to suit students with varied levels of scientific background. Beyond academic knowledge, tutors offer advice on medical school applications, interview preparation, and what to expect from a career in healthcare. They also share their personal journeys in medicine, giving students real insight into the dedication and curiosity the field requires. With their support, students leave the programme with stronger knowledge, sharper critical thinking, and a clear understanding of how to pursue their medical ambitions.

## Where you will be staying

Participants in our London programmes stay in various centrally-located accommodations at University College London (UCL), including Astor College and John Dodgson House. Just a short walk from world-famous landmarks such as the British Museum, the British Library, and Russell Square, these residences offer an authentic taste of city-based student life. Accommodation, meals, and extracurricular activities are all included in the programme, providing a safe, comfortable, and enriching environment. Students live and learn in modern facilities with easy access to the academic venues and cultural highlights that London is known for. Male and female students are housed separately, and all buildings are equipped with Wi-Fi.

##  The London Medicine summer school – a summer like no other

####  Stay on UCL's Campus

 Academic Insights participants in London will stay at University College London accommodation. These award winning facilities are ideally located with iconic sights in every direction. The accommodation boasts modern communal lounges, a furnished outdoor courtyard, and attractive open-plan dining areas: this will be your home away from home as you live and learn at one of London's premier universities.

####  Graduation Ceremony and Dinner

 The Graduation Ceremony marks the completion of your Academic Insights course. This event will be an lively celebration of your achievements. With heartfelt speeches, award presentations, and a delicious celebratory meal, it offers a perfect opportunity to unwind and reflect on the course experience. This event will be a memorable conclusion to a transformative journey.

####  Learn in the Heart of London

 Our perfectly situated accommodation means students have access to world-famous universities and their cutting-edge facilities, iconic landmarks, and enriching museums - taking the learning experience outside of the classroom.

####  Sports, Games & Leisure

 Participants on our London summer schools can enjoy a variety of cultural activities, excursions, and sports. Expect visits to landmarks like the British Museum and Tate Modern, boat rides on the Thames, and trips to the Tower of London. Sports enthusiasts can engage in team games at local parks, fitness sessions, and traditional English cricket matches, all fostering camaraderie and networking.

####  Evening Events

 Situated between Bloomsbury and Kings Cross, our prime location allows us to enjoy the city's exceptional food, culture, and history. Students will benefit from guest talks by inspirational speakers and experience a show in the West End.
