######  Chocolate Films

Students rotated through directing, camera operating and presenting roles while creating a short film. The workshop also included a 30-minute Q&A session with filmmakers about careers in the industry.

######  Mediatracks

Discover how music brought moving images to life. In this practical workshop, students teamed up with MediaTracks music producers, explored professional TYX studios, and wrote original music for a film trailer or their own project.

######  John Yorke Story

This half-day session equipped film students with practical character-crafting skills for their portfolios. Participants gained industry context while boosting their confidence in developing, sharing and critiquing creative ideas within the wider sector.

######  Cinema Museum

Students visited London’s Cinema Museum, where they explored the history of cinemagoing through a unique collection of artefacts, memorabilia and equipment, housed in the former Lambeth Workhouse closely associated with Charlie Chaplin.

## Film Studies summer school in London

 We empower participants with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to thrive in their chosen paths.

###  Explore the Film Studies programme in London

Delve into the world of film in London, a city celebrated for its contributions to the global film industry. Immerse Education’s film summer course in London for students aged 15-18 explores the art of filmmaking, including directing, cinematography, and scriptwriting. With London as the backdrop, students will have the chance to study both classic and contemporary cinema, gaining a deeper understanding of the film industry and preparing for future studies or careers in media and film. Learn more below about how London can be your gateway into the world of film.

##  Meet some of our Film Studies tutors

 The film studies tutors at Immerse Education enrich each course with their love for movies. They help students understand movies in fresh and engaging ways because they have backgrounds in both academia and the film business. In addition to teaching, they inspire students to reflect, inquire, and investigate. Tutors help students see films from a deeper perspective by having conversations about various genres, topics, and filmmaking processes.

 They analyse patterns, provide context for the director's decisions, and start discussions that raise films above just amusement. In cinema analysis, each student is encouraged to express their ideas and find their unique voice. Tutors take extra time to thoroughly explain important film concepts to students studying English. This gives students the confidence they need to write and discuss movies. Our tutors add interest to every class with their expertise and passion.

 Louis

 Louis holds a BA in Film Studies from King's College London and an MSc in Cognitive and Evolutionary Anthropology from the University of Oxford.

 Jonathan

 Jonathan is an award-winning screenwriter and script advisor. His NFTS graduation film premiered at Cannes, and he’s since worked on acclaimed shorts, documentaries, and Mulaqat (Venice, Sundance 2022).

## Where you will be staying

Participants in our London programmes stay in various centrally-located accommodations at University College London (UCL), including Astor College and John Dodgson House. Just a short walk from world-famous landmarks such as the British Museum, the British Library, and Russell Square, these residences offer an authentic taste of city-based student life. Accommodation, meals, and extracurricular activities are all included in the programme, providing a safe, comfortable, and enriching environment. Students live and learn in modern facilities with easy access to the academic venues and cultural highlights that London is known for. Male and female students are housed separately, and all buildings are equipped with Wi-Fi.

##  Experience the summer of your life

####  New experience

 Open the world of film in one of the most exciting cities for cinema. Our school offers a digital film summer programme in London where students explore storytelling, filmmaking, and industry insights in the heart of London. Whether you dream of directing, writing, or analysing films, this experience will bring you closer to the world of cinema.

####  Live on campus

 Students will stay at UCL’s award-winning accommodation, perfectly located to explore the city. Modern lounges, outdoor courtyards, and open-plan dining areas create a comfortable and welcoming environment. This will be your home away from home while you dive into film studies and connect with like-minded peers.

####  Celebrate your achievements

 Immerse Education’s film summer course in London ends with a special Graduation Ceremony and Dinner. This is a chance to reflect on everything you’ve learned, celebrate your growth, and share the experience with friends and mentors. With inspiring speeches, awards, and a memorable meal, it’s the perfect way to close an exciting summer with our film studies summer school in London.

####  Discover London

 London is a city filled with film history and creative inspiration. Stay in the heart of it all, surrounded by iconic locations, industry hubs, and top universities. Explore landmarks like the British Museum, Tate Modern, and the Tower of London. Take a boat ride on the Thames or stroll through the streets where legendary films were made.

####  Activities and evening events

 Beyond the classroom, our London film school summer course offers plenty of ways to explore and connect. Join team games in local parks, try a fitness session, or experience a traditional English cricket match. In the evenings, attend talks by industry professionals, enjoy West End theatre, and immerse yourself in London’s culture. This summer is more than just learning about film—it’s about experiencing it in a city that lives and breathes cinema.
