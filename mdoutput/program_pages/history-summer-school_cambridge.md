###  Explore the History Cambridge summer program

Explore the past in the historic city of Cambridge with Immerse Education’s Cambridge history summer school, designed for students aged 16-18. This Cambridge history programme covers key historical events, figures, and movements, offering students the chance to dive deep into historical analysis and critical thinking.

Students will leave with enhanced research skills and a greater appreciation for history, preparing them for further studies in history or related disciplines. Learn more below to discover how Cambridge’s rich historical backdrop can inspire your studies.

##  Meet some of our History tutors

 Our history tutors love bringing the past to life. They make complex events easy to understand and encourage students to ask questions. With expertise in different time periods, they show how history connects to today’s world. Tutors help with essays, research, and document analysis. They teach students how to think critically and form strong arguments. Lessons are interactive, with discussions that keep history engaging and relevant.
 For students learning English, tutors explain historical terms in a simple way. They provide extra support to make sure every student feels confident. Whether exploring ancient empires or modern conflicts, students gain a deeper understanding. Tutors share their own insights and knowledge, making history feel real and meaningful. Their goal is simple—to build confidence and a love for learning. With their support, students improve their research and writing skills, preparing for future studies.

##  Make the most of your Cambridge summer school experience

####  Stay in a Cambridge college

 A few weeks before arrival to our history summer school, you’ll be assigned to one of Cambridge’s historic colleges based on your subject and dates. Want to stay with a friend or sibling? Let us know, and we’ll do our best to make it happen.

####  Dine in traditional style

 Twice during Cambridge history summer school, you’ll take part in a formal dinner—a special Cambridge tradition. The first happens in your first week, and the second follows the graduation ceremony. These three-course meals are served in grand dining halls, just like they have been for centuries. Smart clothes are recommended to soak in the atmosphere.

####  Celebrate at a graduation ceremony

 Your achievements will be recognised at a special ceremony in a historic Cambridge hall. Faculty members and fellow students will gather to celebrate, and you’ll receive your certificate. The event wraps up with one final formal dinner—an unforgettable way to end your time here.

####  Have a day out in London

 Step beyond Oxford with a full-day trip to London! Experience London’s famous landmarks and enjoy free time to explore the city at your own pace.

####  Sports & games

 Afternoons during our Cambridge history course are packed with activities run by our friendly mentors. You can ty punting on the River Cam, play Zorb football, or take part in an exciting murder mystery night. There’s always something fun to do.

####  Enjoy evenings with friends

 Evenings are for relaxing and socialising. Join a quiz night, enjoy a talent show, listen to guest speakers, or get creative with cake and canvas painting. Sports tournaments and team games also bring everyone together. After a busy day, it’s a great way to unwind and make new friends.
