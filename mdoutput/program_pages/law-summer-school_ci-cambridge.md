## Law: Cambridge summer school for under 18

 We empower participants with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to thrive in their chosen paths.

###  Explore our Cambridge Summer Program in Law

Step into Cambridge – a city shaped by centuries of legal tradition and practice. Our Law summer programme is for students aged 15-18 who are fascinated by justice, advocacy, and how the law shapes our society. You’ll learn about key areas including criminal law, human rights, and international law, while gaining practical experience through law firm visits, case analysis, debate, and simulated legal proceedings.

Through workshops and discussions led by legal scholars and practitioners, you’ll build a foundation in legal reasoning and argumentation, as well as insight into careers within legal bodies ranging from governmental organisations to commercial firms. Whether you’re considering a future as a barrister, solicitor, policymaker, or simply want to explore the world of law more deeply, a law summer programme in Cambridge helps you take your first step into the legal world with clarity and confidence.

##  Meet some of our Law tutors

 At the Law summer programme in Cambridge, your tutors will bring centuries of legal tradition to life through modern lenses. These experienced professionals - from legal scholars to practising lawyers - will guide you through core topics in legal theory, courtroom skills, and ethical reasoning. They’ll share case studies, facilitate debates, and offer insight into how laws are argued, interpreted, and reformed. Through mock trials and group discussions, you’ll gain confidence in presenting arguments and understanding legal frameworks.

 You’ll also hear about the real-life paths your tutors took, from studying law at top universities to building careers in advocacy, policy, and commercial law. They’ll help you refine your reasoning, structure your arguments, and sharpen your public speaking. In Cambridge, where legal thought has evolved for centuries, you’ll be inspired to imagine how you might contribute to that legacy. Whether law school is your goal or you’re just starting to explore the field, your mentors will support your journey.

## Where you will be staying

Participants are typically accommodated in historic colleges at Cambridge University, providing an authentic university experience. Accommodation, meals, and extracurricular activities are included in the programme package. The experience of staying at Cambridge colleges is like no other. Each of the colleges has its own individual history and architectural style, and staying within close quarters allows programme participants to forge lifelong bonds. Male and female students will have separate accommodations, and Wi-Fi is available at every college.

##  Experience the summer of your life

####  Experience a Forward-Thinking City

 In Cambridge, students experience professional life in a city that has always been one step ahead. The University has been pioneering since its foundation, and now supports a thriving startup ecosystem in "Silicon Fen" as well as world-renowned law and business schools.

####  Tradition Amongst Innovation

 Students participate in formal dining experiences twice during the programme, which is a treasured custom at Cambridge. Students congregate in opulent Formal Halls with towering ceilings and chandeliers for three-course meals, providing ample opportunities to network and reflect on industry visits.

####  Celebrate Your Achievements

 Our Career Insights programmes in Cambridge conclude with graduation ceremonies in a world famous setting. Students, tutors, and mentors come together to celebrate achievements, share experiences, and mark the end of an enriching journey. Certificates are awarded, a celebratory meal is shared, and plans are made for your next career steps.

####  Have a day out in London

 Step beyond Oxford with a full-day trip to London! Experience London’s famous landmarks and enjoy free time to explore the city at your own pace.

####  The Perfect Work-Life Balance

 There are plenty of fascinating things to do in the afternoons to make the most of your stay in Cambridge. Enjoy an exciting murder mystery night, play Zorb football on the college campus, or take a leisurely punt along the River Cam. Every activity aims to unite students while enhancing the program's excitement and diversity.

####  Evenings to Remember

 Immerse Education’s career experiences in Cambridge brings something different every day. Listen to inspiring industry guest speakers, take part in a lively quiz night, or showcase your creativity at a cake and canvas session. Whether socialising with friends, attending a talent show, or engaging in a thought-provoking discussion, these moments make your summer even more memorable.
