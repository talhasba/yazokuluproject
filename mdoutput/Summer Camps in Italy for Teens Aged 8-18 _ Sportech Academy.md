# Summer Programs for students aged 8 to 18

These summer camps in Italy welcome international students aged 8 to 18, with English-taught classes and full supervision. One or two weeks inside the Italian companies and universities that define each field. Real projects, real mentors, taught in English.

1 or 2 weeksMilan, Parma, Rome, Venice24/7 supervision

**✓** Insurance included**✓** 24-hour supervision**✓** House parents on site**✓** Meals included**✓** Taught in English**✓** Certificate of completion
