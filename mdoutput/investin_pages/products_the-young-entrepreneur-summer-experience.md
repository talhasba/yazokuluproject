Please find below some of our most frequently asked questions. If you have any other questions you'll be able to find our full [FAQ list](https://investin.org/pages/faq) and you are also welcome to reach out to us at info@investin.org and we'll be happy to help!

 When is the deadline for registering for an experience?
Places on our programmes are allocated on a first-come, first-serve basis so we will close registration as soon as we reach capacity, because of this we would always recommend booking as soon as possible to ensure you do not miss the opportunity to attend.

 Do I get a certificate?
All students are invited to complete a post-programme online assessment and upon completion, they will receive a digital, personalised certificate. Students attending 2-week programmes will also receive a physical certificate and an industry reference letter.

 Who are your safeguarding team?
On each Summer Experience, we have safeguarding team members on-site 24/7 to promote the safeguarding and pastoral welfare of our students. Our team are security-vetted (enhanced DBS-checked) professionals who work in education full time and our team is made up of Academic Professionals / LSA, Safeguarding Officers, Nursing Professionals, and Mental Health Practitioners. Students will therefore always be able to easily contact a guardian, should they need any support.

 Will there be any opportunities for rest days?
Programme hours take place Monday-Friday, with a break over the weekend. If you select the 2-Week Premium option, the weekend will feature exclusive professional development opportunities outlined in the timetable.

 What type of work experience do you need to be an entrepreneur?
There isn't any particular experience you'll need to become an entrepreneur, as there are many different routes to success. Even some of the most famous entrepreneurs started off with little experience. However, there are some skills and characteristics that are key to success: - An idea or vision: An idea or vision is essential as something to work towards. This can a brand-new idea or an existing idea that you can build on - Creativity: You'll need creative skills to solve problems and bring your vision to life - Communication skills: A key part of the job will be communicating your vision to your team and potential investors, so good communication skills are vital - Focus: Entrepreneurship is challenging, involving lots of hard work and long hours with a singular focus - Eagerness to learn: Minds that are curious and eager to learn more are better at solving problems and coming up with new ideas - Determination: Setbacks are an inevitable part of the role; those who are determined and resolute will be better suited to face challenges - Organisation skills: From prioritising tasks and managing time to using resources efficiently and mastering planning, organisational skills will be vital throughout your entrepreneurial career Self-belief and passion: Firm self-belief and a passion for your vision will be the driving forces behind a successful career, particularly when challenges arise

 How can I build skills that will help me become an entrepreneur?
Ways to build entrepreneur skills and experience include: - Business mentorships: Business mentors have relevant knowledge and experience that you can benefit from, so seek out opportunities to learn from other entrepreneurs. Activities like networking, entrepreneurial events and organised mentorship programmes are all great opportunities. - Roles in related fields: Jobs in sales and marketing will give you insights into how to promote your products and services effectively, while roles in legal or regulatory fields will help with things like compliance. Consider areas that you'll need knowledge in and seek out opportunities to learn. - Summer schools: Entrepreneurship summer schools and internships are a great way to learn from experts and meet like-minded people. Our summer programme is a unique opportunity to get entrepreneurial work experience. - Stay informed: Read relevant books and publications to keep up-to-date with the latest news in the industry and learn from successful entrepreneurs. - Practise creativity: Look for innovative solutions to problems and build a practice of creative thinking.

 Is it hard to become an entrepreneur?
Entrepreneurship is challenging, requiring lots of work, focus and sacrifice. However, it's incredibly fulfilling, and its potential rewards are higher than almost any other field.

 How do you include entrepreneurial experience on a CV?
You can include entrepreneurial experience on a CV the same way you would include any other experience. Simply list it under a title like 'Entrepreneur’, 'Self-Employed' or 'Business Owner' and add in the relevant points below. Employers will value any entrepreneurial skills that you've developed through your own hard work, so this may set you apart from other applicants.
