### Overview

**This programme is now fully booked for summer 2026. Join [our mailing list](https://investin.org/pages/join-our-mailing-list) to be the first to hear about summer 2027.**

InvestIN and Summer Boarding Courses have joined forces to deliver an inspiring two-week summer programme designed for curious minds aged 12–14.

Students will explore a range of competitive and desirable careers, from medical sciences and engineering to finance and technology, through hands-on workshops, real-world simulations and English language learning. Each day, students will have the opportunity to experience life on the historic Dulwich College campus, visit the leading University College London, and explore the top London sites.

**Register now for a life-changing experience that will set you up for your future career, or [contact us](https://investin.org/pages/contact-us) to find out more.**

 Coming from abroad?

- To date, we have welcomed students onto InvestIN and SBC programmes from over 100 different countries. They often say that a huge motivation is to meet and befriend students from around the world, whilst taking their English language skills to the next level.
- Attending a summer programme is a fantastic way to experience London, one of the world’s most renowned capital cities for academic excellence, global commerce and vibrant culture. You can also select our residential package to truly experience life on-campus at a top UK school (Dulwich College). We can offer everything you need – from airport transfers to meals and a fantastic evening social programme.
- For students learning [English as a Foreign Language (EFL)](https://investin.org/collections/english-as-a-foreign-language), attending an InvestIN programme facilitates real-world application of English. Students will be immersed in the language 24/7, whether that’s working in a team, writing a project proposal, presenting to a group, networking with professionals or socialising with peers. Our programmes are delivered and facilitated by native English speakers, whilst a significant number of students will be attending from UK based schools, providing international students with the perfect opportunity to enhance their oral and written skills in a professional and social context. Find out more about the benefits for EFL students in our [work experience for EFL students blog post](https://investin.org/blogs/news/elevate-your-english-the-benefits-of-uk-work-experience-for-efl-students).
- For information about visas and language requirements, please see our [FAQs page](https://investin.org/pages/faq).

### **Immersive Experiences Week 1**

### Medical Sciences Career Discovery

- Learn CPR on industry standard models
- Practice suturing on a skin-pad
- Use state of the art software to respond to a medical emergency

### Engineering Career Discovery

- Create a civil engineering structure and test this in groups
- Design your own rocket and test it in groups
- Understand ballast and create a balanced airplane design

### UCL Bloomsbury Visit

- Practice questions for school, university and job interviews and receive feedback
- Build your own mini CV and skills profile
- Begin planning your career pathway and reflect on your first week

### **Immersive Experiences Week 2**

### Business and Finance Career Discovery

- Work in groups and create your own business
- Work in pairs and create your own portfolio
- Trade a simulated market for a week

### Technology and AI Career Discovery

- Build your own functioning lines of code with experts
- Programme a robot using scratch or python
- Create a game mechanic using Unity

### London Cultural Excursion

- Full day cultural excursion into central London to experience the city
- Experience one of London’s most well known museums
- Supervised free time in a famous London retail neighbourhood

### Career Assessment Centre and Graduation

- Work through a series of challenging tasks to consolidate your learning
- Graduate from your career discovery programme at one of UCL’s premier lecture theatres used for graduation ceremonies
- Receive your personalised certificate and course feedback

Please find below some of our most frequently asked questions. If you have any other questions you'll be able to find our full [FAQ list](https://investin.org/pages/faq) and you are also welcome to reach out to us at info@investin.org and we'll be happy to help!

 Who is this programme for?
Students aged 12–14 who want to explore future career pathways while developing their English and academic confidence

 What English level do I need?
A minimum of B1 English level. Advanced English speakers will follow the Career Discovery timetable.

 What’s included in the residential package?
Tuition, accommodation, all meals, evening social activities, weekend excursions and 24-hour safeguarding.

 What rooms are available?
Single ensuite and shared rooms are available to book.

 Is there a social programme?
Outside of the academic programme, residential students will be able to enjoy a dynamic and exciting programme of evening activities including quiz nights, board game nights, sports activities, craft activities and more. Students will be given free time within the Dulwich College campus.

 Can I attend as a day student?
Yes, non-residential students attend Monday to Friday and can opt into the London excursion days.

 Are airport transfers available?
Yes, transfers are available from Heathrow, Gatwick, and St Pancras between 8am–6pm on arrival and departure days.

 Will students be supervised?
Yes, students are supervised 24 hours a day, with a maximum 1:15 staff-to-student ratio on campus and 1:10 on excursions.

### Information for international & EFL students

For The Young Career Discovery Summer Experience, we have a minimum English language requirement of B1 (intermediate, CEFR). Students will join one of two timetables depending on their English language level: one stream for B1 learners, and another for B2+, fluent and native speakers. Our programmes are delivered and facilitated by native English speakers, whilst over 50% of our students are from the UK and the US, providing international students with the perfect opportunity to enhance their oral and written skills in a professional and social context. Find out more about the benefits for EFL students in our blog post [here](https://investin.org/blogs/news/elevate-your-english-the-benefits-of-uk-work-experience-for-efl-students).
