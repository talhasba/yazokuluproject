### Overview

**Are you an aspiring engineer? Join our 2027 engineering summer school in London, designed for students aged 15-18.**

Over the course of 2 weeks, students will get to step into the shoes of an engineer working in London, through a range of immersive career simulations and exclusive professional site visits. Plus, gain a qualification in work experience to boost university applications!

Register now for a life-changing experience that will set you up for a future as a successful engineer, or [contact us](https://investin.org/pages/contact-us) to find out more.

### Immersive Experience Week 1

### Civil and Structural Engineering

- Use core concepts and real-world constraints to design a civil engineering masterpiece
- Build a structure to carry weight across two points in a graduate recruitment-style group task
- Compete against other attendees to impress the judges and become the most successful engineer

### Aeronautical and Aerospace

- Understand the physics of flight when manufacturing a high-performing aerofoil
- Work with a Rolls Royce engineer to improve and test 3D-printed aerofoil designs
- Find the optimum conditions for flight using a wind tunnel
- Discuss your results with top material and aerospace engineers and propose solutions to achieve the most aerodynamic design
- Get a taste of university-level applied physics and maths to deepen your understanding of engineering and how these subjects power real-world innovations
- Take part in a mission to mars activity led by an leading systems engineer to design your transportation architecture

### The Boeing Experience

- Visit an airfield and explore a range of commercial aircraft with pilots and aerospace engineers
- Learn how the design of different aircraft and gliders varies depending on function
- Rig and derig a glass fibre glider with traditional controls, working as a team to lift the wings and secure the main rigging pins
- Work alongside electrical engineers and pilots to programme a fly-by-wire control panel

### Materials Engineering

- Explore the Field of Materials Engineering: Gain an insightful introduction to the vital role of materials engineers in shaping the future of technology and innovation
- Graphene Isolation and manipulation: Participate in the isolation of graphene, a ground breaking material, and apply it to create functional electrical circuits
- Metallurgic Failure Analysis: Learn the principles behind metallurgic failure analysis and understand its importance in assessing material integrity and performance
- Act as a Professional Engineer: Take on the role of a materials engineer by conducting your own failure analysis, enhancing your problem-solving and analytical capabilities
- Produce your own professional metallurgic failure report and receive expert feedback from an industry professional on the quality and accuracy of your analysis

### How to Become a Successful Engineer

- Your path to success: understanding the key decisions ahead and how to maximise your chances of success
- Making the right subject choices: A-level/IB/university and beyond
- Securing work experience and designing an impactful CV/resume
- Powerful interview techniques
- Coaching from engineers on what you can do now to stand out in the future

Please find below some of our most frequently asked questions. If you have any other questions you'll be able to find our full [FAQ list](https://investin.org/pages/faq) and you are also welcome to reach out to us at info@investin.org and we'll be happy to help!

 When is the deadline for registering for an experience?
Places on our programmes are allocated on a first-come, first-serve basis so we will close registration as soon as we reach capacity, because of this we would always recommend booking as soon as possible to ensure you do not miss the opportunity to attend.

 Do I get a certificate?
All students are invited to complete a post-programme online assessment and upon completion, they will receive a digital, personalised certificate. Students attending 2-week programmes will also receive a physical certificate and an industry reference letter.

 Who are your safeguarding team?
On each Summer Experience, we have safeguarding team members on-site 24/7 to promote the safeguarding and pastoral welfare of our students. Our team are security-vetted (enhanced DBS-checked) professionals who work in education full time and our team is made up of Academic Professionals / LSA, Safeguarding Officers, Nursing Professionals, and Mental Health Practitioners. Students will therefore always be able to easily contact a guardian, should they need any support.

 Will there be any opportunities for rest days?
Programme hours take place Monday-Friday, with a break over the weekend. If you select the 2-Week Premium option, the weekend will feature exclusive professional development opportunities outlined in the timetable.

 What kind of work experience is beneficial for aspiring engineers?
Outside the industry, roles in sales, project management, or technical roles can help you build the necessary communication, collaboration and leadership skills that will support a career in engineering. More industry-related work experience opportunities include internships and apprenticeships, mentorships programmes, summer schools and freelance work. InvestIN’s summer school programme is a fantastic work experience opportunity for students aged 15 to 18 looking to pursue a career in engineering.

  Do you need work experience to become an engineer?
To become an engineer, you'll require the appropriate qualifications and work experience. Recruiters value work experience, so a relevant placement year or summer internship will impress your potential employers and set you apart from other applicants.

 Is it hard to get into engineering?
Engineering is a highly distinguished industry, so positions in the field can be competitive. However, with hard work, the right qualifications and good work experience, students can land their dream engineering job.

 What skills are important for a career in engineering?
Key skills that will help engineering students succeed include: - Relevant technical knowledge: Keeping up-to-date with industry news and getting certified in new technologies will help you when submitting job applications - Communication skills: All projects will involve discussions between various parties, so the ability to listen and communicate clearly is essential - Leadership skills: To lead on projects, you'll have to demonstrate your ability to take responsibility and guide your team towards completion - Creativity: When problems arise, you'll need to be able to think outside the box and come up with creative solutions - Innovation: Engineering is an industry that's constantly looking for innovative ways to improve, so innovation skills will be a huge bonus - Conscientiousness: Diligence and attention to detail are essential in such a specialised field - Tenacity and resilience: You'll face lots of challenges as an engineer, so your ability to stay focused and determined will help you succeed - Interpersonal skills: Teamwork is a vital part of engineering roles, so interpersonal skills will help you build successful working relationships

 What qualifications do I need to be an engineer?
To become an engineer, you'll need to complete either a relevant degree or apprenticeship. Students completing an engineering degree will attain a Level 5 or Level 6 qualification in a chemical, civil, mechanical, general, or electronic engineering specialism. Completing an apprenticeship will give you a Level 3 qualification and lots of work experience in the field. Up to 80% of your apprenticeship will be spent working with a relevant company in the engineering sector.
