## Where you will be staying

## Live and study on UCL’s central London campus

Participants in our London programmes live in University College London accommodation across Bloomsbury and neighbouring Fitzrovia, including John Adams Hall and Astor College. Just a short walk from world-famous landmarks such as the British Museum, the British Library, and Russell Square, these residences offer an authentic taste of city-based student life. Accommodation, meals, and extracurricular activities are all included in the programme, providing a safe, comfortable, and enriching environment. Students live and learn in modern facilities with easy access to the academic venues and cultural highlights that London is known for. Male and female students are housed separately, and all buildings are equipped with Wi-Fi.

 **UCL, Gower St, London WC1E 6BT, United Kingdom**

##  What makes our London summer school stand out?

####  What makes our London summer school stand out?

Immerse Education’s London summer programme places you at the heart of the action. Based at University College London (UCL), students live and study in central London, experiencing the independence and pace of campus life in one of the world’s most influential cities. Designed for ambitious students aged 15–18, the programme allows you to choose between two distinct pathways: Career Insights, for those eager to explore professional environments, or the TED Summer School, for students ready to develop their voice and communicate ideas with clarity and purpose.

Our summer course in London is structured around small-group learning, practical workshops, and immersive experiences that reflect the city itself. Whether you’re visiting a world-famous start-up or perfecting a persuasive speech with friends, London becomes part of the learning experience – a city shaped by ideas, influence, and opportunity.

Students are supported throughout by dedicated mentors, current or recent university students who live on campus and guide daily life, helping participants settle in and build independence. Accommodation within UCL’s central London residences places students at the heart of the city’s academic quarter, providing both immersion and reassurance. With small cohort sizes, personalised feedback, and a carefully structured experiential learning framework, the London Summer School ensures that whichever pathway you choose, you leave with greater confidence, practical skills, and tangible work to support your next steps.

##  Exciting activities in London

####  Explore iconic landmarks

 As a participant in a summer school London programme, you’ll have the chance to visit famous landmarks like Big Ben, Buckingham Palace, and the Tower of London. These places offer more than just a beautiful view—they provide insight into the rich history and culture of the UK. When you’re not in class, you can take a stroll around these iconic spots and feel the history come to life.

####  Relax in Historic Neighbourhoods

 One of the advantages of summer programs in London for high school students is the chance to explore the city beyond campus. Just a short walk from UCL, Covent Garden is one of London’s most historic districts. Today, it blends heritage architecture with independent boutiques, well-known brands, theatres, cafés, and restaurants. Students can wander through the piazza, browse a wide range of shops, discover artisan stalls, or pause to watch street performers beneath the colonnades. It’s an ideal place to unwind after a day of learning, offering a memorable glimpse of central London life during your London summer school experience.

####  Southbank Museums & River Cruise

 During your time on our London summer programmes, a visit to the Southbank is a must. This lively stretch along the River Thames is home to some of the city’s best museums and galleries, including the Tate Modern and the Southbank Centre. After exploring, you can unwind with a river cruise and take in the city from a whole new angle, gliding past the skyline’s most famous silhouettes like the London Eye, St Paul’s Cathedral, and Tower Bridge.

####  Experience West End Theatre

 London’s West End is famous for its theatre productions. During your time at summer school in London, you’ll have the chance to enjoy some of the best plays and musicals in the world. From The Lion King to Les Misérables, these shows are an unforgettable part of your London adventure. The West End offers top-quality entertainment that you won’t want to miss.

####  Learn in a global city

 The great thing about summer programmes in London for high school students is that you’re not just learning from textbooks. You’re learning in one of the most diverse cities in the world. London is a place where cultures, ideas, and perspectives come together, giving you a unique experience that goes beyond your typical classroom. This is why London summer school programs are a fantastic way to build skills and gain knowledge while also exploring new opportunities.
