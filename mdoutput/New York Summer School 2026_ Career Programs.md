## Where you will be staying

## Live and study on Columbia University’s Upper Manhattan campus

Participants will stay in Barnard College, a part of Columbia University, situated in the heart of Manhattan. This prestigious location offers students an authentic Ivy League experience, surrounded by the cultural and intellectual energy of New York City. From iconic landmarks to vibrant neighbourhoods, students will enjoy access to world-class museums, theatres, and historic sites, just steps from their residence. Accommodation includes modern, comfortable rooms with shared facilities, Wi-Fi access, and 24/7 on-site security. Male and female students are housed separately. The residential setting fosters a strong sense of community, encouraging friendships and collaboration.

##  Career Insights: New York is your classroom

 Immerse yourself in the legendary districts that built modern industry. Celebrating centennials like NBC, students go behind the scenes of Broadway, Wall Street, and the Garment District – iconic locations that have defined global media, finance, and fashion for over a century.

######  NBC

At NBC Studios in Rockefeller Center, where broadcast television was born, students go behind the scenes of one of New York’s most iconic media institutions, exploring production, audience engagement and the fast-paced world of broadcasting.

######  Broadway

In the heart of New York’s Theatre District, students explore Broadway from both creative and commercial perspectives, gaining behind-the-scenes insight into theatre marketing, backstage production, and the financial strategies that bring world-class shows to life.

######  Wall Street

In Lower Manhattan, where the NYSE traces its origins to 1792, students experience Wall Street through immersive simulations and workshops, gaining practical insight into trading, risk management and the fast-moving financial decisions shaping global markets.

######  Garment District Tour

From historic studios to contemporary design spaces, the Garment District gives students an immersive introduction to New York fashion, revealing the creativity, craftsmanship and industry innovation behind one of the city’s most iconic sectors.

##  Why choose Immerse Education’s New York summer school?

####  Why choose Immerse Education’s New York summer school?

The New York summer school is not just about classes – it’s about experiencing the city as a place where industries are built and ideas take centre stage. This programme is rooted in New York’s global legacy as a hub for business, media, law, technology, and creative influence. At the heart of our NYC summer school are career-focused pathways and the TED programme, designed to help 15- to 18-year-old students explore how ideas become action, impact, and influence. Each New York summer course is hands-on and immersive, challenging students to think practically, communicate clearly, and engage with the real world in meaningful ways.

New York itself becomes central to the experience. Through carefully curated industry visits, students step inside professional environments and see how sectors operate at the highest level, using the city as a living backdrop for learning. Students explore the bustling energy of New York with complete peace of mind, living in accommodation steps away from Central Park and supported throughout by trained mentors who guide day-to-day life, lead activities, and help students make the most of every opportunity. The NYC summer programme also brings together students from around the world, creating space for collaboration, discussion, and shared ambition.

By the end of the summer course in New York, students leave with more than memories. They gain practical insight into careers, confidence in expressing ideas, and a deeper understanding of how New York shapes industries and conversations on a global stage – all while building independence, friendships, and momentum for what comes next.
