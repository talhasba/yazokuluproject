## What is an Intensive English Summer Camp?

An Intensive English summer camp focuses on rapid English development through immersive lessons and real-life practice.

At Bucksmore’s London camp, students engage in daily classes that enhance grammar, vocabulary, and communication skills.

Outside the classroom, students participate in activities like sports, cultural excursions, and social events, providing even more chances to hear and use English. These experiences, combined with group projects and teamwork, create a dynamic environment where English is constantly spoken, ensuring both language improvement and memorable summer experiences.

Download our [Course Overview](http://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/BM-Intensive-English_London_Course-Overview-1.pdf) for more details.

## Our very special English Summer Camp

Bucksmore Intensive English course aims to build**strong fluency** **and enhance English oral and written communication skills** through a fun and dynamic language curriculum.

Students study **25 hours of Intensive English per week** to develop their English skills through a variety of engaging sessions, including academic tutorials, group projects, conversation classes, and hands-on excursions that take learning outside the classroom.

Our course also builds strong interpersonal skills such as teamwork, creativity, and confidence through **project-based learning** and mentorship.

Student progress is tracked and assessed at the end of each course by **Bucksmore Assessment**, an evaluation tool developed in alignment with the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) scale.

## Excursions

The Bucksmore Intensive English course in London includes 2 full-day and 1 half-day excursion per week. You can see a selection of excursions options below and an [example two-week programme](http://www.bucksmore.com/summer-courses/english-courses/intensive-english-course-london/#excursions) below.

![Featured image for “London: Natural History Museum & Kensington Palace”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/natural-history-museum-1-optimized.webp)

### London: Natural History Museum & Kensington Palace

The world-famous Natural History Museum is a centre for research and exploration into a vast range of specimens from various segments of natural history.

You will also visit Kensington Palace, a royal residence since the 17th century. Explore the state rooms open to the public in this historic building.

![Featured image for “Brighton”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Brighton-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Brighton

Explore the vibrant seaside city of Brighton. Stop off at the typical English seaside pier with fairground attractions, souvenir and novelty shops.

Next enjoy the view from high above on the Brighton i360 which is a giant observation pod that gives 360-degree view of the city and sea.

![Featured image for “Windsor”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Windsor-optimized.webp)

### Windsor

Visit the oldest and largest occupied castle in the world, home to 1,000 years of royal history.

Then explore the rich history of Windsor including visits to the Theatre Royal, Eton College, and time for shopping.

![Featured image for “London Sealife Aquarium”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/London-Aquarium-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### London Sealife Aquarium

Experience an amazing underwater world at Sea Life London, home to over 500 species in 14 themed zones and right in the centre of London!

![Featured image for “London: St Paul’s Cathedral”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/St-Pauls-Cathedral-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### London: St Paul’s Cathedral

Stand in awe at this Grade I listed English Baroque style building designed by Sir Christopher Wren.

This architectural feat is an icon of London’s skyline!

![Featured image for “Thorpe Park”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thorpe-park-optimized.webp)

### Thorpe Park

Thorpe Park is a theme park located in Surrey, England with over 30 rides, attractions and live events.

You will be able to experience record-breaking roller coasters, water rides, and seasonal events alongside your new friends!

![Featured image for “London Eye”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/London-Eye-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### London Eye

Get closer views of London’s most spectacular landmarks from Big Ben to The Houses of Parliament, and even Windsor Castle on a clear day!

![Featured image for “Cambridge”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/cam-kings-optimized.jpg)

### Cambridge

Visit Cambridge and experience its historic waterways via a punting trip on the River Cam.

You will also have time to explore one of the 31 Colleges that make up the University of Cambridge – King’s College – founded by King Henry VI in 1441.

The moment you touch down in the UK, you’ll be looked after by Bucksmore staff. At the arrivals gate you’ll be met by one of our representatives and be escorted to your complimentary transfer or exclusive airport lounge (Heathrow only) to wait for your transfer.
