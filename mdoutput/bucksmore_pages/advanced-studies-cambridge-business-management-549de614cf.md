## What is a Business Management course?

Bucksmore’s Pre-University Business Management course in Cambridge gives your child the opportunity to study key business concepts in a city renowned for both tradition and innovation. Students develop academic readiness for university while exploring disciplines including marketing, finance, strategy, operations, and human resources. Alongside subject knowledge, they build essential skills in teamwork, problem-solving, communication, and digital literacy.

Your child will live on campus in one of Cambridge’s historic colleges, set against the city's entrepreneurial ecosystem. The course combines classroom learning with excursions around the city and visits to some of the UK’s most exciting destinations, blending academic theory with practical, real-world insights.

### This course is for you if:

- You have an advanced or native level of English
- You want to study at a UK university and learn about undergraduate life
- You are motivated with a keen interest in Business Management
- You want to support your application into the top UK universities

## The Course in Detail

In this pre-university summer course, your child will examine how companies are structured, how leaders shape strategy, and how resources are organised to meet business objectives. The programme combines theory and applied learning, encouraging students to connect management concepts to current global issues.

### Course Features

-

**Business Operations and Strategy:** Learn how organisations grow, compete, and innovate.

-

**Financial Management:** Understand basic accounting, budgeting, and financial analysis to inform business decisions.

-

**Marketing Essentials:** Examine branding, promotion, and customer engagement strategies.

-

**People Management and Leadership:** Explore leadership, organisational culture, and employee motivation.

-

**Global Business and Trade:** Discuss the challenges of internationalisation and cross-cultural management.

### Example Lessons

- **Case Study:** Analyse the strategy of multinational organisations when preparing to engage with a new market.
-

**Business Simulation:** Collaborate to launch a mock company and present your pitch.

-

**Marketing Workshop:** Design a promotional strategy tailored for an international audience.

-

**HR Workshop:** Engage in leadership scenarios to practise effective communication.

-

**Globalisation Debate:** Consider the future of international trade and sustainable business.

### Final Outcome

By the end of the course, students will:

-

Gain a thorough foundation in business management disciplines.

-

Apply theoretical learning through debates, workshops, and hands-on projects.

-

Strengthen skills in leadership, collaboration, and analysis.

-

Immerse themselves in Cambridge’s dynamic academic and cultural life while preparing for university studies in business and related subjects.

These sessions help build essential skills for successful entrance to universities. You will participate in masterclasses designed to connect your skills, strengths and interests to potential careers.

You will gain insights into the various types of universities and acquire a comprehensive understanding of the full application cycle for UK universities, including detailed guidance on structuring a personal statement

![Featured image for “Small Group Tutorials”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Bucksmore.Photo_.Brasenose-4-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Small Group Tutorials

Teaching on the Pre-University Programme mirrors Oxbridge university learning in many ways, including your participation in small group tutorials.

The tutorials are a key educational strategy that reflects the active, student-centered learning model of Bucksmore Education. This method encourages active exploration and autonomous learning whilst facilitating peer collaboration and team building.

![Featured image for “Debate Society”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/KIng-Edward0988a-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Debate Society

Debate Societies are an integral part of university life. Debate sessions give you a chance to explore and discuss a range of topics you are passionate about, outside of the subjects you are studying.

Debating encourages you to develop strong critical thinking and analytical skills, plus building teamwork, leadership and communication skills.

## Excursions

The Bucksmore Pre-University Preparation in Cambridge includes 2 full-day and 1 half-day excursion per week. You can see a selection of excursions options below and an example two-week programme.

![Featured image for “London: Natural History Museum & Kensington Palace”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/natural-history-museum-1-optimized.webp)

### London: Natural History Museum & Kensington Palace

The world-famous Natural History Museum is a centre for research and exploration into a vast range of specimens from various segments of natural history.

You will also visit Kensington Palace, a royal residence since the 17th century. Explore the state rooms open to the public in this historic building.

![Featured image for “Windsor”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Windsor-optimized.webp)

### Windsor

Visit the oldest and largest occupied castle in the world, home to 1,000 years of royal history.

Then explore the rich history of Windsor including visits to the Theatre Royal, Eton College, and time for shopping.

![Featured image for “Oxford: Museum of Oxford”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Museum-of-Oxford-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Oxford: Museum of Oxford

On this half-day excursion you will visit the only museum all about Oxford. The Museum tells the story of Oxford and its people through a range of social history objects and interactive displays.

![Featured image for “Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thames-river-cruise-optimized.webp)

### Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich

Step aboard a cruise to Greenwich via the River Thames, taking in some of London’s world-famous sights along the way.

After lunch, you will explore the Cutty Sark, a historic sailing ship which was the fastest of its time!

![Featured image for “Thorpe Park”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thorpe-park-optimized.webp)

### Thorpe Park

Thorpe Park is a theme park located in Surrey, England with over 30 rides, attractions and live events.

You will be able to experience record-breaking roller coasters, water rides, and seasonal events alongside your new friends!

![Featured image for “Cambridge”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/cam-kings-optimized.jpg)

### Cambridge

Visit Cambridge and experience its historic waterways via a punting trip on the River Cam.

You will also have time to explore one of the 31 Colleges that make up the University of Cambridge – King’s College – founded by King Henry VI in 1441.
