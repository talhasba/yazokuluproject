## What is English plus Creative Arts Summer Camp?

An English Plus Creative Arts summer camp combines language learning with creative expression. At Bucksmore, students strengthen their English while exploring a wide range of artistic disciplines, including visual arts, drama, dance, and music.

This unique programme blends creative workshops with English lessons, helping students build artistic skills, confidence, and language proficiency.

It’s the perfect environment for young learners to express themselves both creatively and academically, and achieve their potential all within a supportive and inspiring setting.

Download our [Course Overview](http://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/BM-English-Plus-Arts_Course-Overview-1.pdf) for more details.

## Our very special English Summer Camp

This course is designed for motivated students who want more than just English language tuition from their summer. With a focus on the creative arts, students have the opportunity to develop their skills through practical workshops that encourage imagination, expressions, and collaboration.

The creative arts workshops play a significant role in developing a child's creativity, confidence, coordination, language and communication skills - helping students to thrive not only in education but also in their future endeavours. Alongside the arts programme, students improve their English through academic tutorials, group projects, conversations classes, and cultural excursions that bring learning to life.

Each week offers a new specialism, taught by experienced practitioners, for example:

- **Art** - drawing, painting, sculpting, and mixed media, showcased in a final exhibition
- **Dance** - contemporary, hip-hop, ballet, and jazz, performed in a live showcase
- **Drama** - acting, improvisation, and stagecraft, culminating in a theatre performance
- **Music** - instrumental skills, contemporary styles, and band collaboration, ending with a concert

Beyond arts and language skills, the course builds interpersonal abilities such as teamwork, creativity, and confidence through project-based learning and mentorship.

Student progress is carefully tracked and assessed at the end of each course by the **Bucksmore Assessment**, an bespoke evaluation tool aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) scale.

## Excursions

The Bucksmore English Plus courses in Brighton includes 2 full-day and 1 half-day excursion per week. You can see a selection of excursions options below and an example two-week programme below.

![Featured image for “Brighton Walking Tour”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/orry-verducci-mbLe_D3HO1o-unsplash-1-optimized.webp)

### Brighton Walking Tour

On this half-day excursion you will explore the coastal sights of Brighton, including Brighton Pier, the Royal Pavilion and The Lanes, while learning about the history of the city.

![Featured image for “Portsmouth”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Portsmouth-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Portsmouth

On this full-day excursion you will visit the Portsmouth Historic Dockyard and visit world-famous historic ships at the home of the Royal Navy.

Afterwards, you will have a chance to shop at Gunwharf Quays, the South coast’s leading designer shopping outlet.

![Featured image for “Canterbury”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/pexels-spencphoto-28586330-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Canterbury

Visit the beautiful cathedral city of Canterbury. The day will begin with a punting tour of this historic city through its waterways.

Following that, you will visit Canterbury Cathedral, often described as ‘England in stone’ and discover why it’s rich history is intrinsically linked to the nation’s story.

![Featured image for “Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thames-river-cruise-optimized.webp)

### Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich

Step aboard a cruise to Greenwich via the River Thames, taking in some of London’s world-famous sights along the way.

After lunch, you will explore the Cutty Sark, a historic sailing ship which was the fastest of its time!

![Featured image for “Thorpe Park”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thorpe-park-optimized.webp)

### Thorpe Park

Thorpe Park is a theme park located in Surrey, England with over 30 rides, attractions and live events.

You will be able to experience record-breaking roller coasters, water rides, and seasonal events alongside your new friends!

![Featured image for “London: Buckingham Palace & Politics Walking Tour”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/buckingham-palace-optimized.webp)

### London: Buckingham Palace & Politics Walking Tour

You will embark on a walking tour of key locations relating to London’s rich political and royal history.

You’ll also visit the historic vibrant market area of Covent Garden. Following lunch you will enjoy a tour of the magnificent State Rooms of Buckingham Palace.
