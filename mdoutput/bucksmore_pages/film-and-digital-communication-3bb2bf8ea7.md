Summer Camp in London, UK

Leave with a short film of your own, real-world experience, and valuable preparation for future study and careers in film and digital communication.

## The Bucksmore Young Professionals Film & Digital Communication course experience

The Young Professionals Film & Digital Communication course is a two-week film summer course in London for teenagers who want to build creativity, confidence and communication skills through filmmaking and digital storytelling.

Delivered by expert tutors from London Film School, the programme introduces students to filmmaking as a powerful way of thinking, communicating and working with others. Through hands-on practical projects, students learn how ideas are shaped visually, how stories engage audiences, and how teams collaborate to bring creative work to life.

While this digital communication course for teenagers offers an exciting introduction to filmmaking, it is not only for students planning a career in film. The skills developed are highly transferable and valuable across a wide range of future pathways, including business, technology, media, law, science, social impact and the creative industries.

No previous filmmaking experience is required to take part in this film and digital communication summer course .

## What is a Film & Digital Communication Summer Camp?

This summer film course in London summer camp at Bucksmore immerses students in the fast-paced world of professional filmmaking. Through collaborative group projects, screenwriting workshops and first-hand filmmaking experience students will get to practice the entire process of making a film, from script to screen.

Living on campus encourages teamwork and collaboration, while excursions in London - such as to a Virtual Production wall - offer first-hand exposure to one of the world’s most important centres of filmmaking.

![Image](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/P1016346-scaled-optimized.jpg)

## Subject Overview

In a video-first world, young people are increasingly expected to communicate ideas visually, present themselves confidently and understand how digital content shapes opinion and engagement.

This course helps students to:

- Think creatively and critically
- Communicate ideas with clarity and impact
- Build confidence speaking and presenting
- Understand how stories influence audiences
- Work effectively as part of a team

This film course for international students provides essential skills for education, future careers and leadership in any field.

Download our [Subject Overview](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/BM-YP_Filmmaking_DigitalCommunication_Course-Overview.pdf) for more details.

### What this course prepares you for

By the end of the programme, students will have:

- A completed short film created as part of a team
- Strong visual storytelling and digital communication skills
- Increased confidence presenting ideas and creative work
- Experience of professional creative processes
- Insight into careers across film, media and beyond
- A certificate of participation from London Film School.

### Capstone Industry Project

Students will work in small production teams to develop, shoot and present a short film in response to a real-world creative brief. Acting as a professional filmmaking unit, each group will take a project from concept through to final screening, learning how ideas are shaped, communicated and refined in a video-first world.

The project emphasises collaboration, creativity and communication, mirroring how film and digital content are developed in professional environments. While students gain hands-on filmmaking skills, the focus is equally on transferable abilities such as teamwork, leadership, problem-solving and presenting ideas with clarity and impact.

Download our [Capstone Overview](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Filmmaking.DigitalCommunication-Capstone-Overview.pdf) for more details.

## Example Industry Workshops

![Featured image for “Industry Pitching Experience to a panel of professionals”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/DSC07722-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Industry Pitching Experience to a panel of professionals

At the end of the programme, students pitch their film projects in a boardroom to a panel of industry professionals, who have first-hand experience of pitching to commissioners.

This develops confidence, presentation skills and an understanding of how creative ideas are shared in professional settings.

![Featured image for “Virtual Production Experience with Epic Games partnership”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/P1016717-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Virtual Production Experience with Epic Games partnership

Students take part in an introduction to Virtual Production delivered in partnership with Epic Games, creators of Fortnite.

This session explores how real-time digital environments are used in modern film, television and games, giving students insight into cutting-edge creative technology.

Jack attended the London Film School’s MA Filmmaking Programme. Following the completion of his graduation film, Jack graduated from the course as a director in 2024 and has been working in the film industry ever since. Jack has worked in a number of different film roles, ranging from sound recording and mixing to producing and production managing. On this half-day excursion you will visit the only museum all about Oxford. The Museum tells the story of Oxford and its people through a range of social history objects and interactive displays.

Step aboard a cruise to Greenwich via the River Thames, taking in some of London’s world-famous sights along the way.

After lunch, you will explore the Cutty Sark, a historic sailing ship which was the fastest of its time!

You will be able to experience record-breaking roller coasters, water rides, and seasonal events alongside your new friends!

### London: Buckingham Palace & Politics Walking Tour

You will embark on a walking tour of key locations relating to London’s rich political and royal history.

You’ll also visit the historic vibrant market area of Covent Garden. Following lunch you will enjoy a tour of the magnificent State Rooms of Buckingham Palace.

![Featured image for “Oxford: Christ Church College”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ox-christ-optimized.jpg)

### Oxford: Christ Church College

On this half-day excursion you will visit Christ Church College, an Oxford college and a city Cathedral sharing a unique historic space.

You’ll discover that Christ Church is an academic institution and a place of great historical significance.

### Cambridge

Visit Cambridge and experience its historic waterways via a punting trip on the River Cam.
