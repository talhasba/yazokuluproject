## The Bucksmore Young Professionals Theatre and Performing Arts course experience

The objective of the Young Professionals Theatre and Performing Arts course is to simultaneously enhance students' subject knowledge and language proficiency, with a primary focus on the development of English language fluency, through meaningful, context-rich learning experiences. This approach fosters critical thinking and communication skills while promoting cultural awareness and the practical application of skills in real-world contexts.

This course teaches West End performance through practical demonstrations and exercises. Here’s an overview of what we put into it and what you will get out of it:

-

Vibrant and bustling University accommodation in Central London.

-

An international home-from-home in a nurturing and safe environment.

-

Small international groups and world-class teaching.

-

Capstone Industry project with external certification.

-

Separate male and female accommodation on site.

-

London travelcard included.

-

2 full-day and 1 half-day excursion per week to fun and varied attractions.

## What is a Young Professionals Theatre and Performing Arts Summer Camp?

A Young Professionals Theatre & Performing Arts summer camp at Bucksmore in London offers high school students the opportunity to develop their talents in acting, dance, and drama.

Taught by professionals from London’s West End, our theatre arts summer camps include workshops that focus on performance techniques, stage presence, and creative expression. Students will build confidence and refine their skills in a supportive environment.

Alongside living on campus, students will also explore London’s vibrant theatre scene through excursions, making it an unforgettable learning experience.

## Subject Overview

Taught by West End professionals, you will gain a first-hand experience of the performing arts industry through tuition in acting, singing, dancing, stage-combat and puppetry, as well as taking part in West End workshops and watching West End shows.

Bucksmore Education recognises that the performing arts play a significant role in developing a persons creativity, confidence, coordination, language and communication skills.

By partnering with a top West End theatre company West End On Demand, you will receive first hand experience of the industry and a true taste of West End Theatre.

### Learning outcomes

- Develop language, skills and knowledge relating to the Performing Arts industry
- Improve confidence and communication skills
- Gain confidence in team-work and ensemble skills
- Understand and gain insight into the key skills required for a career in theatre

At all stages of life the skills learned in this genre can be transferred and help you develop and reach your full potential in education and beyond.

At the end of the 2-week course there will be a final performance followed by an award ceremony.

### Activities and Excursions

The addition of an exciting and varied activity and excursion programmes embedded in the timetable, ensures students will have a well-rounded and powerful two weeks studying on the Young Professionals Theatre & Performing Arts course.

## Example Industry Workshops

![Featured image for “West End Audition Masterclass”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Masterclass-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### West End Audition Masterclass

Experience what it is like to audition for a West End show in a fun and relaxed environment.

Your group will learn tricks and tips from a West End cast member and the workshop includes West End audition dance routines, singing, audition songs, sight reading and preparing audition text.

![Featured image for “Stage Combat”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Stage-Combat-optimized.webp)

### Stage Combat

Taught by a qualified stage combat instructor, you’ll get to grips with the basics of stage combat and discover how actors make a fight look convincing.

The workshop will include unarmed combat techniques, how to perform stage fight choreography and how to fight safely.

![Featured image for “Singing Workshop”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Singing-Workshop-optimized.webp)

### Singing Workshop

Sing like a West End professional in our West End singing workshop.

Your group will be provided with a lyric sheet or sheet music from a West End show of your choice whilst working on technique, harmony, acting through song and performance.

![Featured image for “Acting for Camera”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Acting-for-Camera-optimized.webp)

### Acting for Camera

Discover the basics of film and TV acting whilst we focus on performance choices and the differences between theatre and television.

Your group will then get to learn what happens at a TV casting and practice a mock audition.

![Featured image for “Puppetry”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Puppetry-optimized.jpg)

### Puppetry

Learn the basics of puppetry and how to create a puppet character in this fun workshop taught by an experienced puppeteer.

This is a fantastic way to be creative with your group, and work on concentration, focus, imagination and creativity.

## Our Teachers

Our teachers are selected to replicate the premium educational experiences of top UK universities and boarding schools. They also know how to take learning and teaching outside the classroom and see every input and experience as an opportunity to learn and grow.

Here are some examples of the teachers you might meet on this programme:

![A head and shoulders picture of Rachel, the founder of West End On Demand..](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Rachel-Bingham-optimized.jpeg)

### Rachel Bingham

Rachel Bingham is a top West End performer, teacher and the Director of West End On Demand. Rachel trained in musical theatre at the prestigious Mountview Academy Of Theatre Arts and has performed extensively. Her work includes: Les Misérables (West End); Billy Elliot (West End); The Sound Of Music (West End) to name a few. Rachel created West End On Demand to use her passion for the performing arts to encourage children and students of all ages to enhance their confidence, creativity and talents. Her teaching work includes performing arts and foundation degree performing arts, as well as guest teaching around the UK and Europe.

## Excursions

The Bucksmore Young Professionals course in London includes 1 full-day and 3 half-day excursions per week. You can see a selection of excursions options below and an example two-week programme below.

![Featured image for “London: St Paul’s Cathedral”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/St-Pauls-Cathedral-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### London: St Paul’s Cathedral

Stand in awe at this Grade I listed English Baroque style building designed by Sir Christopher Wren.

This architectural feat is an icon of London’s skyline!

![Featured image for “Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thames-river-cruise-optimized.webp)

### Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich

Step aboard a cruise to Greenwich via the River Thames, taking in some of London’s world-famous sights along the way.

After lunch, you will explore the Cutty Sark, a historic sailing ship which was the fastest of its time!

![Featured image for “Thorpe Park”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thorpe-park-optimized.webp)

### Thorpe Park

Thorpe Park is a theme park located in Surrey, England with over 30 rides, attractions and live events.

You will be able to experience record-breaking roller coasters, water rides, and seasonal events alongside your new friends!

![Featured image for “London Eye”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/London-Eye-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### London Eye

Get closer views of London’s most spectacular landmarks from Big Ben to The Houses of Parliament, and even Windsor Castle on a clear day!

![Featured image for “London: Buckingham Palace & Politics Walking Tour”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/buckingham-palace-optimized.webp)

### London: Buckingham Palace & Politics Walking Tour

You will embark on a walking tour of key locations relating to London’s rich political and royal history.

You’ll also visit the historic vibrant market area of Covent Garden. Following lunch you will enjoy a tour of the magnificent State Rooms of Buckingham Palace.

![Featured image for “Cambridge”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/cam-kings-optimized.jpg)

### Cambridge

Visit Cambridge and experience its historic waterways via a punting trip on the River Cam.

You will also have time to explore one of the 31 Colleges that make up the University of Cambridge – King’s College – founded by King Henry VI in 1441.

The moment you touch down in the UK, you’ll be looked after by Bucksmore staff. At the arrivals gate you’ll be met by one of our representatives and be escorted to your complimentary transfer or exclusive airport lounge (Heathrow only) to wait for your transfer.
