## What is an IELTS Exam Preparation Summer Camp?

An IELTS Preparation summer camp in London is designed to equip students with the skills needed to excel in the IELTS exam.

At Bucksmore, students receive focused training in all aspects of the test: reading, writing, listening, and speaking. Continuous assessment is used to monitor progress, build exam confidence, and highlight individual areas for improvement. Targeted practice then ensures steady development in the skills students need most.

Alongside lessons, students enjoy cultural excursions in London, allowing them to practice English in real-world contexts, enhancing both their test readiness and fluency.

## Our very special English Summer Camp

The Bucksmore IELTS Preparation course aims to build strong fluency and enhance English oral and written communication skills through a fun and dynamic language curriculum.

Students study **25 hours of IELTS Exam Preparation per week** to develop their English skills and prepare for the IELTS exams through a variety of engaging sessions, covering **examination technique across Speaking, Listening, Reading and Writing papers.**

Our course also builds strong interpersonal skills such as teamwork, creativity, and confidence through project-based learning and mentorship.

Student progress is tracked and assessed continually throughout the course, and biweekly mock IELTS examinations will be marked and tailored feedback given to student from our specialist IELTS tutors. At the end of the course, **students take the IELTS UKVI Academic examination at a central London test centre.**

Explore the vibrant seaside city of Brighton. Stop off at the typical English seaside pier with fairground attractions, souvenir and novelty shops.
