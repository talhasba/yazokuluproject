## What is an Environmental Science & Sustainability course?

The Pre-University Environmental Science & Sustainability course at Bucksmore explores one of the most pressing issues of our time: how humans interact with and impact the planet.

The course provides students with a scientific foundation in environmental systems while also engaging with the social, political, and economic dimensions of sustainability.

Students live on campus and take part in excursions around Cambridge and top UK destinations, ensuring a well-rounded learning experience that blends academic study with real-world applications.

## The Course in Detail

Students investigate core principles of environmental science and the challenges of sustainable development in a rapidly changing world. The programme combines earth system science, climate studies, and ecological economics with applied skills in research, fieldwork, and data analysis.

Through lectures, case studies, and practical activities, students gain insight into topics such as climate change, global inequalities, conservation, and renewable energy.

Applied projects give students the chance to explore solutions to real-world environmental challenges, while developing the critical thinking and research skills needed for university-level study.

### Key Subject Areas

- **Earth System Science**: Understanding the processes that shape our planet’s atmosphere, biosphere, hydrosphere, and lithosphere.
- **Global Climate Change**: Examining causes, consequences, and policy responses to one of the defining issues of the 21st century.
- **Environmental & Ecological Economics**: Exploring how economic models, policies, and incentives shape sustainability efforts.
- **Sustainable Development**: Investigating the UN Sustainable Development Goals, indicators of progress, and challenges of implementation.
- **An Unequal Planet**: Analysing how environmental issues intersect with global inequality and justice.
- **Research & Data Skills**: Introducing methods such as fieldwork, surveys, GIS mapping, and remote sensing for environmental analysis.
- **Regenerating Environments & Communities**: Exploring ecological restoration and community-based sustainability initiatives.

### Example Lessons

- **The Science of Climate Change**: How greenhouse gases alter earth systems.
- **Measuring Sustainability**: Tools and indicators from the UN SDGs to local initiatives.
- **Economics of the Environment**: Can growth and sustainability coexist?
- **GIS in Action**: Using digital mapping to assess land use and environmental risk.
- **Fieldwork Project**: Investigating ecosystems, biodiversity, or sustainability initiatives in the UK context.

### Final Outcome

By the end of the course, students will:

- Gain a strong foundation in environmental science, sustainability, and ecological economics.
- Understand the complexities of climate change, global inequality, and sustainable development.
- Develop practical skills in research methods, data analysis, and fieldwork.
- Be equipped with the critical and analytical tools needed for undergraduate study in environmental science, geography, or related disciplines.

## Excursions

The Bucksmore Pre-University course in Cambridge includes 2 full-day and 1 half-day excursion per week. You can see a selection of excursions options below and an example two-week programme.

![Featured image for “London: Natural History Museum & Kensington Palace”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/natural-history-museum-1-optimized.webp)

### London: Natural History Museum & Kensington Palace

The world-famous Natural History Museum is a centre for research and exploration into a vast range of specimens from various segments of natural history.

You will also visit Kensington Palace, a royal residence since the 17th century. Explore the state rooms open to the public in this historic building.

![Featured image for “Windsor”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Windsor-optimized.webp)

### Windsor

Visit the oldest and largest occupied castle in the world, home to 1,000 years of royal history.

Then explore the rich history of Windsor including visits to the Theatre Royal, Eton College, and time for shopping.

![Featured image for “Oxford: Museum of Oxford”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Museum-of-Oxford-scaled-optimized.jpg)

### Oxford: Museum of Oxford

On this half-day excursion you will visit the only museum all about Oxford. The Museum tells the story of Oxford and its people through a range of social history objects and interactive displays.

![Featured image for “Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thames-river-cruise-optimized.webp)

### Thames River Boat Cruise & Royal Museums Greenwich

Step aboard a cruise to Greenwich via the River Thames, taking in some of London’s world-famous sights along the way.

After lunch, you will explore the Cutty Sark, a historic sailing ship which was the fastest of its time!

![Featured image for “Thorpe Park”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/thorpe-park-optimized.webp)

### Thorpe Park

Thorpe Park is a theme park located in Surrey, England with over 30 rides, attractions and live events.

You will be able to experience record-breaking roller coasters, water rides, and seasonal events alongside your new friends!

![Featured image for “Cambridge”](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/cam-kings-optimized.jpg)

### Cambridge

Visit Cambridge and experience its historic waterways via a punting trip on the River Cam.

You will also have time to explore one of the 31 Colleges that make up the University of Cambridge – King’s College – founded by King Henry VI in 1441.
