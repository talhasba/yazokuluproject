# International Relations and Politics
Summer Camp in London

Welcome to Bucksmore’s Young Professionals International Relations and Politics summer course in the UK.

This career-focused programme in London introduces high school students to the world of politics, diplomacy, and global governance, while developing English language fluency in an academic and professional context. The course combines theory with practical simulations—helping students develop both academic insight and transferable skills in debate, negotiation, and public speaking.

Outside the classroom, cultural excursions to places such as London’s Parliament and City Hall provide first-hand exposure to the realities of governance and diplomacy, making this a truly immersive learning experience.

## What is a Young Professionals International Relations and Politics Summer Camp?

This programme is the best of an international relations summer camp and a politics summer course, which gives students the opportunity to explore politics and global affairs through both academic study and vocational practice.

You will:

- Debate contemporary global issues in structured discussions.
- Take part in a Model United Nations (MUN)-style simulation, developing negotiation and diplomatic skills.
- Explore international law, globalisation, and human rights through case studies and real-world scenarios.
- Strengthen English language fluency in formal, diplomatic, and debate contexts.

This politics and international relations summer camp is ideal for students interested in politics, law, economics, or future careers in diplomacy, NGOs, or international organisations.

Download our [Subject Overview](http://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/BM-YP_Intl-Relations-and-Politics_Course-Overview.pdf) for more details.

## Subject Overview

This subject introduces international relations and politics as both an academic discipline and a professional practice. Taught by specialists in politics and global affairs, lessons balance theory with applied practice.

Course content may include:

- Political theory and key concepts in international relations
- Globalisation, trade, and interdependence
- International organisations and governance (UN, NATO, EU)
- Human rights and humanitarian intervention
- Security studies and contemporary conflicts
- Diplomacy, negotiation, and conflict resolution

### Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students will:

- Develop English language fluency through debates, negotiations, and presentations.
- Demonstrate knowledge of key theories and concepts in international relations and politics.
- Analyse contemporary issues such as globalisation, conflict, and international cooperation.
- Understand the role of international law and institutions in shaping world affairs.
- Strengthen critical thinking, research, and communication skills through case studies and simulations.
- Gain practical experience in diplomacy and negotiation, preparing for future academic or career pathways.

### Capstone Industry Project

The Capstone Project allows students to apply their learning to a practical challenge in international relations. Working collaboratively, they will research, discuss, and present solutions to a simulated global issue, drawing on the theories and case studies explored throughout the course.

Certificates of completion recognise their achievements, and the portfolio of work showcases readiness for future studies in politics, law, or international affairs.

Download our [Capstone Overview](https://www.bucksmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Politics.InternationalRelations-Capstone-Overview.pdf) for more details.
