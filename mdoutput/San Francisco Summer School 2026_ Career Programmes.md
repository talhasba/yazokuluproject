## Overview of studying in a global business hub

Students attending our San Francisco summer school are accommodated in residence halls located on the campus of the University of California, Berkeley, in Berkeley, California. This provides a campus-style living experience while students take part in an academic programme and activities across the San Francisco Bay Area, a region known for its innovation and global industries. Accommodation is provided in comfortable college-style rooms with essential amenities, along with access to communal lounges and shared social spaces designed for student life. Male and female participants are housed separately, and the residence halls provide a secure and supportive environment throughout the programme.

##  Why choose Immerse Education’s San Francisco summer school?

####  Why choose Immerse Education’s San Francisco summer school?

The San Francisco summer school offers students the chance to explore future careers in one of the world’s most innovative cities. It’s more than studying – it’s about discovering how ideas become real industries in San Francisco.

Our summer courses in San Francisco focus on practical, career-focused learning. Students take part in collaborative projects and workshops that encourage problem-solving, creativity, and professional thinking. Lessons connect industry concepts with real-world applications, helping students understand how their interests translate into careers.

The city itself becomes part of the experience. San Francisco’s global technology and business culture helps students see industries from new perspectives while exploring key landmarks and innovation hubs.

Outside sessions, students build connections through shared activities and discussions with peers. By the end of the summer programme in San Francisco, participants leave with clearer career insight, stronger confidence, and new friendships. The San Francisco summer programmes for high school students combine career exploration, skill development, and cultural discovery in one inspiring city.

##  Activities in San Francisco

####  Explore San Francisco’s famous landmarks

 Immerse Education’s San Francisco summer school takes you beyond the classroom and into the heart of the city. Visit the Golden Gate Bridge and admire its breathtaking views. Take a ferry to Alcatraz Island and uncover its fascinating history. Explore the lively atmosphere of Fisherman’s Wharf, and ride the historic cable cars through the city’s steep streets. These adventures bring San Francisco’s unique culture and history to life.

####  Discover the сity’s rich history

 San Francisco is a city filled with stories. From its gold rush beginnings to its role as a hub for innovation, every corner has something to share. Students will explore neighbourhoods and museums that highlight the city’s vibrant past. These experiences make history feel real and exciting, connecting lessons to the world around you.

####  Enjoy cultural adventures

 San Francisco is a melting pot of cultures, offering endless opportunities to experience diversity. Wander through Chinatown, sample delicious food, or visit the colourful streets of the Mission District. These activities give students a taste of the city’s vibrant and welcoming spirit while creating lasting memories.

####  A summer like no other

 San Francisco offers more than famous sights – it is a global centre of innovation where ideas become industries. Time spent here exposes students to the entrepreneurial spirit and forward-thinking culture that have shaped the modern world. It’s not just about what you see, but what you take with you: new perspectives, meaningful friendships, and the confidence to think bigger.
