Program Overview

## Architecture & Urban Design

This architecture and urban design summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for aspiring architects and urban designers alike. Hosted in Milan, the world capital of design, this two-week program takes students across every scale of the discipline, from buildings and open spaces to infrastructure. Working in hands-on studios led by leading architects and designers, participants sketch, model and prototype, culminating in a capstone that reimagines a run-down urban area as their own renovation concept.

The program takes place in Milan, the global capital of design and a world reference point for architecture and interiors. Home to Salone del Mobile, Milan Design Week and the ADI Design Museum, the city is where design culture is made. Students work alongside practicing architects and designers, and visit iconic urban renovations that have attracted international attention.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the architecture & urban design summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18, taking them across every scale of the discipline, from buildings and open spaces to infrastructure, and ending with a capstone that reimagines a run-down urban area as their own renovation concept.

  What do students learn and create?

Students learn the fundamentals of urban and architectural design, develop skills in sketching, model-making and 3D digital concepts, and study how exterior spaces are conceived and composed, building toward a portfolio-ready renovation proposal.

  Does my child need design experience to join?

No. Sketching, model-making and digital design are taught from the ground up in hands-on studios. What matters most is genuine interest in architecture and design.

  Where is the program based?

In Milan, the global capital of design and a world reference point for architecture and interiors, home to Salone del Mobile, Milan Design Week and the ADI Design Museum.

  What excursions are part of the program?

Visits to urban requalifications in Milan such as Scalo Farini, Porta Genova and Scalo Romana, a Lake Como excursion, Milan sightseeing, visits to Italian design brands and a gelato afternoon.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
