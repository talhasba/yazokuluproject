## Music Innovation: Voice & Orchestra

This music summer program in Venice runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for young singers and musicians ready to grow as performers and creators. Set in Veneto, a short trip from Venice and the Verona Arena, this two-week summer school pairs daily voice and orchestra labs with a forward-looking curriculum in music, AI, neuroscience and branding. Students rehearse, record their own track in a professional studio, and take to the stage in a live final concert.

The program takes place in Castelfranco Veneto, a historic town in the heart of the Veneto, one of Italy’s richest musical and cultural regions, within reach of Venice and the legendary Verona Arena. Students train at a dedicated music academy, Art Voice Academy, surrounded by the landscapes and traditions that have inspired centuries of Italian music, while experiencing the artistic life of the region first-hand.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Music Innovation: Voice & Orchestra program?

A two-week residential summer school in Castelfranco Veneto for students aged 15 to 18, pairing daily voice and orchestra labs with a curriculum in music, AI, neuroscience and branding, run in partnership with Art Voice Academy.

  What do students learn and create?

Students work together toward a shared repertoire in daily Voice & Orchestra labs, explore improvisation, music and AI, the neuroscience of music and emotion, acoustic engineering, branding and public speaking, record their own track in a professional studio and perform in a live final concert.

  Does my child need previous experience to join?

The program is designed for young singers and musicians ready to grow as performers and creators, so some musical practice is expected, but everything is taught in labs alongside the teaching staff.

  Where is the program based?

In Castelfranco Veneto, a historic town in the heart of the Veneto, within reach of Venice and the Verona Arena. Students train at Art Voice Academy and stay at the 4★ Hotel Alla Torre.

  What excursions are part of the program?

A visit to the Arena di Verona and to the Fenice Theater in Venice, plus pizza night.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
