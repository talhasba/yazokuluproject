## For schools and groups

You bring the group, we build the programme around your teaching objectives and run it end to end. Schools, sports clubs, academies, agencies and private groups all work this way.

-     Any of our 15 programs, run for your group alone

## For universities

Your faculty sets the level and the goals. We build the term around them and teach it in English, with the partners who run our summer terms.

-     Four areas: Engineering, Transportation Design, Business, Design
-     Your group picks the specialised track
-     Dallara, Sapienza and SRO Motorsports Group deliver the teaching
-     Motor Valley, Rome and Milan
-     One week minimum, two weeks maximum, any month of the year

Frequently Asked Questions

  Who can request a tailor-made program?

Agencies, high schools, sports clubs, academies, universities and private groups. Any of our experiences can be arranged as a fully bespoke edition for your own group.

  When can the program run?

At any time of the year. Tailor-made editions are not limited to our summer calendar.

  How long is a tailor-made program?

One week is the minimum and programs run up to two weeks, on every track and for both the Juniors and the University lines.

  What do we choose and what do you handle?

You choose the program track, the dates, the length of stay and the age range. We handle the academic content, company visits, partner institutions, accommodation, meals, airport transfers and cultural excursions.

  What does accommodation include?

Double rooms with en-suite bathroom, house parents present on-site every night, full board with three meals per day and free Wi-Fi, plus airport shuttle and shuttle to and from classes.

  How do we start?

Tell us about your group, your objectives and your preferred dates. We then design a bespoke proposal, agree the programme and confirm numbers and dates, and support your group from the moment it arrives in Italy.
