Program Overview

## Business of Luxury Goods

This business summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. The Italian Business Excellence Management (IBEM) program has a strong focus on the luxury sector, where Italian companies excel: Fashion, Automotive, Yachting, Jewelry & Watches, and Hospitality. Unlike traditional business summer programs, ours is extremely hands-on, built to teach skills you won’t find in university textbooks. Students learn the “Made in Italy” managerial model directly from top executives and founders, through company visits, applied workshops and a real business challenge.

The IBEM is headquartered in the heart of Milan, Italy’s economic and financial capital, hosting the stock exchange, major banks, numerous multinational HQs, and a significant portion of Italy’s GDP. Students alternate in-class lectures walking distance from their accommodation with company and innovation district visits across Northern Italy, the country’s primary industrial and economic powerhouse.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the business of luxury goods summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18, focused on the luxury sector where Italian companies excel: Fashion, Automotive, Yachting, Jewelry & Watches and Hospitality. Students learn the “Made in Italy” managerial model directly from top executives and founders.

  What do students learn and create?

Students build a foundation in leadership, strategy and entrepreneurship, then move through company visits and applied workshops, closing with a final group project in which they create a real business from scratch.

  Does my child need previous business experience to join?

No. The program starts with an introduction to business management and entrepreneurship, and is built to be hands-on rather than textbook-based.

  Which companies do students visit?

Company visits include Pagani, Baglietto, Pomellato and other leading Italian brands, across Milan and the industrial districts of Northern Italy.

  Where is the program based?

In the heart of Milan, Italy’s economic and financial capital. In-class lectures are within walking distance of the accommodation, with company and innovation district visits across Northern Italy.

  How are students supervised?

Students stay in a University Residence with 24/7 staff support and supervision, in a safe, international environment.

  Is the program taught in English?

Yes, the program is taught entirely in English.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
