Program Overview

## Cosmetic Sciences

This cosmetic sciences summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for students fascinated by the science and business of beauty. Hosted in Milan, this two-week program explores how cosmetics are formulated, tested and brought to market, from the chemistry of ingredients to AI-driven formulation and the trends reshaping the global beauty industry. Hands-on lab work lets students create their own products, from molecules to finished formula.

The program takes place in Milan, the world capital of fashion and beauty and the gateway to Italy’s cosmetics industry, one of the world’s leading centres for cosmetics and make-up manufacturing. Here students study in a real academic environment, accessing the labs of one of the world’s leading cosmetics universities, Università di Pavia, with hands-on access to formulation and testing laboratories.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Cosmetic Sciences summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18, exploring how cosmetics are formulated, tested and brought to market, from the chemistry of ingredients to AI-driven formulation and the trends reshaping the global beauty industry.

  What do students learn and create?

Students learn the fundamentals of formulation chemistry and product development, work hands-on in the lab to create their own skincare and make-up, study how products are tested for efficacy and safety, and decode the trends reshaping the global market.

  Does my child need a science background to join?

No. The program is built for students fascinated by the science and business of beauty, and everything is taught from the ground up in hands-on labs.

  Where is the program based?

In Milan, the world capital of fashion and beauty and the gateway to Italy’s cosmetics industry, with students studying in a real academic environment in the laboratories of Università di Pavia.

  What excursions are part of the program?

Company visits to Italy’s leading cosmetic companies, a Lake Como excursion and Milan sightseeing (residential only), plus pizza night.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
