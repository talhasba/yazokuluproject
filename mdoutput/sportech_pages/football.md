## AC Milan Academy Experience Élite

This football summer camp in Milan is open to students aged 8 to 16, for one or two weeks. Train under AC Milan’s official Youth Team coaches at the club’s iconic Vismara training center, home to the Puma House of Football. A true Rossoneri experience built on 125 years of footballing excellence.

Players train inside AC Milan’s elite facilities (7 training pitches, changing rooms, gym and recovery areas) using the same methodology, GPS tracking and video analysis as the Rossoneri youth academy.

Typical Weekly Schedule

Monday

Reception & evaluation. Registration and camp presentation. Athletic tests, technical analysis and first training session: ball control and dribbling.

Tuesday-Wednesday

Technique & tactics. 1v1 game situations, intensive training with GPS tracking, role-specific and individual technique sessions with custom video analytics.

Thursday

Development & competition. Leadership work on the pitch, mental coaching, recovery and injury-prevention techniques, set-piece sessions.

Friday

Optimization & competition. Individual work with AC Milan technicians, personalized performance feedback and progress review.

Weekend (Residential)

Final tournament & celebration. Awards and individual recognition, Meet & Greet with AC Milan Legends, excursions to Casa Milan, Milan sightseeing and Lake Como.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What age groups do junior programs cater to?

Junior programs are designed for students aged 15-18. Participants are grouped by age and experience to keep every session appropriately challenging and well supervised.

  Is accommodation included?

The residential formula includes supervised accommodation, all meals, and a full evening and weekend programme. A day formula is also available for students who prefer to arrange their own stay.

  How are students supervised overnight?

House parents are present on-site every night and Sportech Academy support staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure, vetted, and selected for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  What languages are programs taught in?

All teaching is delivered in English. No Italian language knowledge is required to take part.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related requirements are always accommodated. Please indicate any dietary needs during the booking process.

  How do students get to Italy?

Families arrange travel to Italy, and airport transfer coordination is available for residential participants. Our team provides full guidance on arrival times and logistics ahead of the program.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on how far in advance you cancel relative to the program start date. Full details are provided in your booking confirmation, and our team is happy to walk you through the options.
