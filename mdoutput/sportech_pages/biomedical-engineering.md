Program Overview

## Biomedical Engineering

This biomedical engineering summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for students fascinated by the meeting point of medicine and engineering. Hosted in Milan, Italy’s hub for healthcare, life sciences and biomedical innovation, this two-week program shows how medical technology is designed, prototyped and tested, and ends with a capstone project developed together with a biomedical engineering company.

The program takes place in Milan, the heart of Italy’s healthcare and life sciences ecosystem, a city with world-class hospitals, research universities and a dense network of medtech and biotech companies. Here students study in a real academic environment, working in engineering labs alongside researchers and industry professionals.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Biomedical Engineering summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18, showing how medical technology is designed, prototyped and tested, and ending with a capstone project developed together with a biomedical engineering company.

  What do students learn and create?

Students learn how medical devices work and how the biomechanics of the human body shape their design, explore biotechnology and diagnostics, prototype and test their own device, and develop a capstone project with the industry.

  Does my child need an engineering background to join?

No. The program is built for students fascinated by the meeting point of medicine and engineering, and everything is taught from the ground up in hands-on labs.

  Where is the program based?

In Milan, the heart of Italy’s healthcare and life sciences ecosystem, with world-class hospitals, research universities and a dense network of medtech and biotech companies.

  What excursions are part of the program?

Company visits to medtech and biotech companies, a Lake Como excursion and Milan sightseeing (residential only).

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
