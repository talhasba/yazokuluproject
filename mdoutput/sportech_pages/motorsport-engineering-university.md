Program Overview

## Motorsport Engineering

This motorsport engineering summer term in the Motor Valley runs for two weeks and is designed for undergraduate and master students. A two-week summer term that fills the motorsport gap most engineering degrees leave open. In the heart of Italy’s Motor Valley, university engineering students go deep into both sides of the sport: Race Car Design & Manufacturing, designing, building and validating a real aerodynamic component and Race Strategy & Performance, learning the race engineer’s craft from active Formula 1 race engineers and running a full race weekend. Deliberately advanced, hands-on, and taught entirely in English. Ideal for Mechanical, Automotive, Aerospace and Motorsport Engineering students.

The program is based in the Motor Valley (Emilia-Romagna), at a leading engineering and innovation campus near Parma. The companies of this cluster design Formula 1 cars, build chassis for IndyCar, Formula 2 and Formula 3, and produce Le Mans hypercars including the Cadillac and BMW LMDh, the Le Mans-winning Ferrari 499P and McLaren's 2027 car. The surrounding Motor Valley is the densest motorsport-engineering cluster on earth, around 188 companies and over €12bn, home to Ferrari, Lamborghini, Pagani, Maserati and Ducati.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Motorsport Engineering summer term?

A two-week advanced summer term in Italy’s Motor Valley for undergraduate and masters engineering students near Parma, taught entirely in English.

  What do students learn and create?

Students work through advanced aerodynamics and CFD, vehicle dynamics, composite engineering and real manufacturing, then move trackside to telemetry and data, tyres, race strategy and pit-wall communications, designing and validating a real aerodynamic component and running a full race weekend.

  Who is the program for?

Mechanical, Automotive, Aerospace and Motorsport Engineering students at undergraduate and masters level. It is deliberately advanced.

  Where is the program based?

In the Motor Valley in Emilia-Romagna, at the Dallara Academy and Innovation Farm near Parma, the densest motorsport-engineering cluster on earth.

  What excursions are part of the program?

A Dallara HQ visit, the Autodromo di Varano and a Ferrari factory visit including Corse Clienti and Scuderia Ferrari.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
