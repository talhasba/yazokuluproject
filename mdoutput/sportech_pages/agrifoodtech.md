Program Overview

## AgriFoodTech

This agrifood and agritech summer program in Milan, Piacenza and Turin runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. A two-week program for students aged 15 to 18, exploring how food is produced, kept safe and reinvented, from laboratory science to the technologies transforming farming and the supply chain.

The program runs between Milan, Piacenza and Turin, combining lessons and laboratory work with visits to leading Italian food innovation companies. It builds toward a Food Innovation Project developed and presented by the students.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the AgriFoodTech summer program?

A two-week program in Milan, Piacenza and Turin for students aged 15 to 18, covering food science and safety, AgriTech and the technologies reshaping farming and the supply chain, ending with a food innovation project.

  What do students learn and create?

Students study food science, safety and quality, explore precision farming, traceability and blockchain applied to the supply chain, and develop a food innovation project from concept to final pitch.

  Does my child need a science background to join?

No. Laboratory work and AgriTech tools are taught from the ground up in hands-on sessions. What matters most is genuine interest in food, science and technology.

  Where is the program based?

The program runs between Milan, Piacenza and Turin, with accommodation in Milan.

  What excursions are part of the program?

Visits to leading Italian food innovation companies, a Lake Como excursion, Milan sightseeing and a pizza night.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
