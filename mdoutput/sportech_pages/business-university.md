Program Overview

## Business in Italian Excellence

This business summer term in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for undergraduate and master students. Discover the world of Business of Luxury Goods at the heart of Italy’s most prestigious institutions and companies. Students work inside real business environments, alongside practising professionals, using the same tools, methodologies and approaches as the actual industry. In collaboration with Spring Studios, Baglietto, Pagani and Cipriani, this programme offers a unique blend of hands-on learning, expert mentorship and immersive cultural experience in Milan. All teaching is in English. Ideal for Business, Management, Marketing, Economics and Luxury/Fashion Management students.

The IBEM is headquartered in the heart of Milan, Italy’s economic and financial capital, hosting the stock exchange, major banks, numerous multinational headquarters and a significant portion of Italy’s GDP. Students alternate in-class lectures walking distance from their accommodation with company and innovation district visits across Northern Italy, the country’s primary industrial and economic powerhouse.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Business in Italian Excellence summer term?

A two-week summer term in Milan for undergraduate and masters students, hosted by IBEM, exploring the world of Business of Luxury Goods inside real business environments alongside practising professionals.

  What do students learn and create?

Students build a foundation in Italian business management, leadership and strategy, then examine brand equity, positioning, sustainability and international expansion through corporate visits, applied workshops and executive sessions, closing with a group project centred on launching a real venture.

  Who is the program for?

Business, Management, Marketing, Economics and Luxury/Fashion Management students at undergraduate and masters level.

  Where is the program based?

In Milan, Italy’s economic and financial capital. IBEM is headquartered in the heart of the city, with lectures walking distance from the accommodation and visits to companies and innovation districts across Northern Italy.

  What excursions are part of the program?

Visits to Pagani, Baglietto and Pomellato, among others.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
