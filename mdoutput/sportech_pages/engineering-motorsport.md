Program Overview

## Engineering & Technologies in Motorsport

This motorsport engineering summer program in Parma runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Discover the world of Engineering & Technologies in Motorsport at the heart of Italy’s most prestigious institutions and companies. Students work inside real engineering environments alongside practising professionals, using the same tools and methodologies as the actual industry. In partnership with Dallara Academy, this programme offers hands-on learning, expert mentorship and immersive cultural experience in Parma. All teaching is in English.

The Dallara Academy and Dallara Innovation Farm form a cutting-edge educational hub in the heart of Italy’s Motor Valley. Created by race car manufacturer Dallara, they combine motorsport innovation, sustainability and interactive learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

  Where is the motorsport engineering summer program held?

At the Dallara Academy in Parma, the headquarters of Dallara, one of the most respected names in motorsport engineering.

  What do students do on the motorsport program?

An intensive two-week, 60-hour programme of classes and labs with Dallara staff, covering engineering foundations, design, technical skills and simulation, where students build a scale race car.

  What ages is the motorsport program for, and is it residential?

For aspiring engineers aged 15 to 18 passionate about Formula 1, motorsport, aerospace or mechanical engineering. The program is residential only, in Parma.

  What excursions are part of the motorsport program?

A visit to the Ferrari Museum and a visit to the Varano Racetrack.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
