Program Overview

## Fashion

This fashion summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. An immersive two-week program in Milan, the global fashion capital, taking students on the full journey of launching their own fashion brand: from the first sketch to a finished garment, a brand identity and lookbook, and a go-to-market launch with a social media page and website mockup.

Students work in professional fashion and photography studios in the heart of Milan, surrounded by the ateliers, showrooms and flagship stores of the Quadrilatero della Moda, where global fashion is actually made.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the fashion summer program?

A two-week program for students aged 15 to 18 in Milan, the global fashion capital, taking them through the full journey of launching their own fashion brand: from the first sketch to a finished garment, a brand identity and lookbook, and a go-to-market launch.

  What do students learn and create?

Students design and create their own garment, from sketch and tailoring to a finished piece, while learning styling and editorial shooting. They then build a brand around it with a name, identity and professional lookbook, and take it to market with a social media page, website mockup and launch campaign.

  Does my child need design experience to join?

No. Garment design, styling and brand building are taught from the ground up. What matters most is genuine interest in fashion and a willingness to create.

  Where is the fashion program based?

In Milan, one of the world's four fashion capitals and home of Milan Fashion Week, in professional fashion and photography studios in the heart of the Quadrilatero della Moda, surrounded by ateliers, showrooms and flagship stores.

  What excursions are part of the fashion program?

A Giorgio Armani Prive visit, visits to leading fashion brands, and a gelato afternoon in Milan, with company visits to Italian fashion houses and guest speakers bringing the industry into the classroom.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
