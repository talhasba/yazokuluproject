Program Overview

## Pet Design

This pet design summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for creative students curious about design, animals, veterinary and the fast-growing pet industry. Hosted in Milan in partnership with The HAD - Human Animal Design®, Italy’s leading studio in Pet Design, this two-week program introduces an entirely new discipline: designing products, spaces and services for the well-being of pets. Combining design, ethology and zoo-anthropology, students learn to design not for humans alone, but for the animals who share our world, culminating in their own multiexperiential concept for the pet industry.

The program takes place in Milan, the global capital of design and home to The HAD - Human Animal Design®, the studio that pioneered Pet Design as a discipline. Here students work at the meeting point of two of Italy’s great strengths, world-class design culture and a deep respect for animal welfare, in a city that hosts Salone del Mobile, Milan Design Week and the ADI Design Museum.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Pet Design summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18, run in partnership with The HAD - Human Animal Design®, introducing the design of products, spaces and services for the well-being of pets and ending with a multiexperiential concept for the pet industry.

  What do students learn and create?

Students study the foundations of pet design, relational ethology and zoo-anthropology, explore the perception, physiology and behavioural needs of dogs and cats, and apply design thinking to real case studies, building toward their own multiexperiential solution.

  Does my child need design experience to join?

No. The program is built for creative students curious about design, animals, veterinary and the pet industry, and everything is taught from the ground up in hands-on studios.

  Where is the program based?

In Milan, the global capital of design and home to The HAD - Human Animal Design®, the studio that pioneered Pet Design as a discipline, in a city that hosts Salone del Mobile, Milan Design Week and the ADI Design Museum.

  What excursions are part of the program?

A company visit at Arcaplanet and MyPet Clinic, the ADI Design Museum, a Lake Como excursion (residential only), Milan sightseeing, visits to Italian design brands and a gelato afternoon.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
