Program Overview

## Automotive Design & Future Mobility

This automotive and car design summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. An immersive two-week program introducing the fundamentals of automotive design through studio work, clay modelling, digital tools and curated industry visits in Milan, Turin and Motor Valley.

Students work in modern labs equipped with professional software for sketching, digital rendering and clay modelling, then extend the experience to Turin, the historic heart of Italian automotive culture, and to Motor Valley.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the automotive design summer program?

A two-week residential program for students aged 15 to 18 introducing the fundamentals of automotive design through studio work, clay modelling, digital tools and curated industry visits across Milan and Motor Valley.

  What do students learn and create?

Car design foundations, sketching, clay modelling, digital rendering, basic 3D and interior/UX design. Students develop a personal automotive concept, ending with refined sketches, a digital render and a 1:10 scale clay model.

  Does my child need drawing skills to join?

No. Sketching, clay modelling and digital rendering are taught from the ground up. What matters most is genuine interest and a willingness to engage creatively.

  Where is the automotive design program based?

Primarily in Milan, Italy's design capital, in modern labs with professional software, with visits to the historic heart of Italian car design, and Motor Valley. Students stay in Milan with full board and 24/7 supervision.

  Will this program help with design school applications?

Yes. It gives students a clearer understanding of the field and produces portfolio-relevant work that goes well beyond simple enthusiasm.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
