Program Overview

## Sports Management

This sports management summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Discover how the business of sport really works, inside two of the industries Italy leads the world in: football and motorsport. Over two weeks, students go behind the commercial engine of professional sport: how a top football club and a professional racing team are financed, how players and drivers are contracted, sponsored and turned into global brands, and how championships and events are built and sold.

Taught by faculty who work inside leading sports organizations, with an HQ visit to a top Serie A club and behind-the-scenes access at a live GT race weekend. The program is based in Milan, home turf of both industries: Serie A football and the €12.4bn Motor Valley, home to 188 racing teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the sports management summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18 on the business of football and motorsport, available in residential and day formula, with an HQ visit to a leading Serie A club and behind-the-scenes access at a live GT race weekend.

  What do students learn and create?

Club economics, player contracts and the transfer system, the academy model, motorsport team economics, driver management and the sponsorship model. Students work on two team projects: growing a top club’s fanbase and launching a new racing team, each pitched to a mentor panel.

  Does my child need previous business studies to join?

No. Everything is taught from the ground up, entirely in English. What matters most is genuine interest in the business of sport.

  Where is the sports management program based?

In Milan, with a race-weekend visit to an Italian circuit. Students stay in a university residence in Milan with full board.

  Will this program help with university applications?

Yes. It gives students a clearer understanding of how the sports industry works and produces real project work that goes well beyond simple enthusiasm.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
