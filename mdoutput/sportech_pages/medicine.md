Program Overview

## Medicine

This medicine summer program in Milan runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. The Medicine Summer Program, with academic coordination by Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele (UniSR), is designed for motivated high school students who are considering a future in medicine or healthcare. Through direct training with doctors and specialists, participants gain early exposure to medical practice and develop essential skills in diagnostics, emergency response and patient care.

The program takes place at UniSR, a leading medical school in Italy known for its integration of teaching and research. The university is closely affiliated with the San Raffaele Research Hospital, a pioneer in cutting-edge medical and scientific research. UniSR ranks among the top 40 universities in the world and offers a 6-year International MD program.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the medicine summer program?

A two-week program in Milan for students aged 15 to 18, with academic coordination by Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele (UniSR), designed for those considering a future in medicine or healthcare.

  What do students learn and create?

Students explore anatomy, physiology, radiology, surgery and lab medicine, work in hands-on simulation labs on digital anatomy, suturing, blood draws and BLS, study AI in medical imaging, and close the program by solving and presenting their own clinical case.

  Does my child need previous experience to join?

No. The program starts from the theoretical foundations of key medical specialties and builds up to practical work in the simulator lab.

  Where is the program based?

At UniSR in Milan, a leading medical school in Italy, closely affiliated with the San Raffaele Research Hospital and ranked among the top 40 universities in the world.

  What excursions are part of the program?

Milan sightseeing and a Lake Como excursion, plus a pizza night. These services are available to students enrolled in the residential program.

  How are students supervised?

Students stay in a University Residence with 24/7 staff support and supervision, in a safe, international environment.

  Is the program taught in English?

Yes, the program is taught entirely in English.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
