Program Overview

## Aerospace Engineering

This aerospace engineering summer program in Rome runs for two weeks and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for students passionate about space exploration, aviation and engineering. Hosted at Università Sapienza’s School of Aerospace Engineering in Rome, whose graduates work at the Italian Space Agency and the European Space Agency. Participants design and build their own rocket prototype, culminating in a rocket launch experience.

The program takes place in Rome at the facilities of the School of Aerospace Engineering, one of Europe’s most historic institutions dedicated to aerospace research and education. Founded in 1926, the school celebrates 100 years of aerospace innovation and has trained generations of engineers who now work across Italy’s aerospace sector, including ASI and ESA. Students experience a real academic environment at the heart of Italy’s aerospace ecosystem, while discovering the cultural richness of one of the world’s most iconic cities.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the aerospace engineering summer program?

A two-week program in Rome for students aged 15 to 18, hosted in partnership with Università Sapienza’s School of Aerospace Engineering, in which participants design and build their own rocket prototype and end with a real launch.

  What do students learn and create?

Students cover rocket engineering and propulsion, space mission simulation with tools such as OpenRocket, and embedded systems with Arduino to control rocket electronics and collect flight data, building toward their own rocket prototype.

  Does my child need previous engineering experience to join?

No. The program starts from the fundamentals of rocket engineering and space technologies through lectures, laboratory activities and project-based learning.

  Where is the program based?

In Rome, at the facilities of the School of Aerospace Engineering, founded in 1926 and one of Europe’s most historic institutions dedicated to aerospace research and education.

  What excursions are part of the program?

The rocket launch itself, Rome sightseeing including a Colosseum tour, and star and moon observation from the Pincio Terrace. These services are available to students enrolled in the residential program.

  How are students supervised?

Students stay in a 4★ hotel with 24/7 staff support and supervision, with a daily shuttle to and from classes.

  Is the program taught in English?

Yes, the program is taught entirely in English.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
