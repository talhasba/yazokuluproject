Program Overview

## Motorsport Management

This motorsport management summer term in Milan and the Motor Valley runs for two weeks and is designed for undergraduate and master students. Motorsport Management & Leadership is a two-week summer experience for university students who want a career in the business and leadership of motor racing. Not a degree or a multi-year commitment, it is the most immersive way into the industry while you are still studying, open to far more people than a postgraduate program. You learn the sport where it lives: Italy’s Motor Valley and Lombardy, where the cars are built, the teams are based and the championships are run. Across two weeks you go inside how a motorsport business runs: financing, sponsorship, media rights, talent management, and how winning teams are led under pressure, taught on the ground by the people who run the sport, led by SRO Motorsports Group, creator of modern GT racing. It blends seminars with factory visits, a live race weekend, a leadership module and The Brief: a real commercial challenge pitched to industry decision-makers. Ideal for Business, Management, Sports Management, Marketing, Economics and Automotive Engineering students.

The program is taught inside motorsport’s heartland, Italy’s Motor Valley and Lombardy, where the cars are built, the teams are based and the championships are run. The Motor Valley generates around €12bn and is home to 188 racing teams and constructors, including Ferrari, Lamborghini, Pagani, Dallara, Maserati and Ducati. And the timing matters: motorsport is in its strongest decade, with Formula 1 posting record revenue of $3.87bn in 2025 and sponsorship passing 20% of core revenue for the first time, making commercial and leadership roles some of the most in-demand in the sport.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Motorsport Management summer term?

A two-week summer experience in Italy’s Motor Valley and Lombardy for university students who want a career in the business and leadership of motor racing, led by SRO Motorsports Group, creator of modern GT racing.

  What do students learn and create?

Week 1 covers the economics and commerce of motorsport: financing, sponsorship, media rights, championship commercialisation and driver management. Week 2 turns to leadership and the team, including Pit Wall, a live strategy simulation, then a live race weekend and The Brief, a real commercial challenge pitched to industry decision-makers.

  Who is the program for?

Business, Management, Sports Management, Marketing, Economics and Automotive Engineering students at undergraduate and masters level.

  Where is the program based?

In Italy’s Motor Valley and Lombardy, where the cars are built, the teams are based and the championships are run, home to 188 racing teams and constructors including Ferrari, Lamborghini, Pagani, Dallara, Maserati and Ducati.

  What excursions are part of the program?

A Dallara HQ visit, race weekend paddock access and a Ferrari factory visit including Corse Clienti and Scuderia Ferrari.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
