## Esports in Motorsport: Sim to Reality

This esports and sim racing summer program in Indianapolis runs for one week and is designed for students aged 15 to 18. Designed for students passionate about motorsport, engineering and sim racing. Hosted at Dallara AK Esports’ Indianapolis facilities during US spring break, this one-week program puts students inside the loop real race engineers live in: make performance decisions first in the simulator, then validate them against the real car, the factory and the Indianapolis Motor Speedway.

It complements our Engineering & Technologies in Motorsport program at Dallara in Italy: together, a two-campus Dallara Pathway from building a race car to engineering its performance. In Indianapolis the Dallara Experience Hub, the Dallara IndyCar Factory and the Speedway sit within reach of one another: the simulator and the real car it models quite literally share an address.

Typical Weekly Schedule

Simulation & Performance Engineering

Vehicle dynamics, aerodynamics and setup applied hands-on in the simulator, working towards a measurable performance gain over a baseline.

Data, Telemetry & Race Strategy

Telemetry, data analysis and race strategy: reading the numbers the way a race engineer does and turning them into decisions.

Sim-to-Reality Correlation & Race Weekend Capstone

Understanding the levels of fidelity from a gaming simulator to a professional driver-in-the-loop simulator to the track, then validating the work against reality.

Special Excursions

Tour of the Dallara US Factory & Experience Hub, the Indianapolis Motor Speedway and a team dinner.

Final Project

The program culminates in a virtual race weekend: teams apply their setup, strategy and telemetry work, then validate the result against the real IR-18 chassis, Dallara’s own sim-to-reality correlation process and published Speedway lap data.

Frequently Asked Questions

  What is the Esports in Motorsport program?

A one-week residential program in Indianapolis for students aged 15 to 18, hosted at Dallara AK Esports during US spring break, on simulation, telemetry and race engineering.

  What do students learn and create?

Vehicle dynamics, aerodynamics and setup, telemetry, data and race strategy, and sim-to-reality correlation. Students work in engineering teams to enhance race car performance and prove the gain through data, closing with a virtual race weekend.

  Does my child need previous engineering studies to join?

No. Everything is taught from the ground up, entirely in English. What matters most is genuine interest in motorsport, engineering and sim racing.

  Where is the program based?

In Indianapolis, at the Dallara AK Esports facilities, with visits to the Dallara US Factory & Experience Hub and the Indianapolis Motor Speedway.

  Will this program help with university applications?

Yes. It gives students a clearer understanding of how race engineering works and produces real project work that goes well beyond simple enthusiasm.

  How are students supervised?

House parents stay on-site every night and Sportech Academy staff are on call 24/7. Residences are secure and vetted, chosen for safety and proximity to the program venue.

  Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and allergy-related diets are all catered for. Note any requirements during booking and the team arranges them.

  What is the refund and cancellation policy?

Cancellations follow the terms in the enrolment agreement. Contact the admissions team for the current deadlines and conditions before booking.
