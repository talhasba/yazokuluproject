#  Academic Programme

[Home](https://www.mpw.ac.uk/) » [International Students](https://www.mpw.ac.uk/international-students/) » [Summer School](https://www.mpw.ac.uk/international-students/summer-school/) » Academic Programme

## Academic Programme

Our programme features eight exciting academic streams. Choose one stream to study for the two-week duration of the programme.

SIGN UP


Architecture

## Architecture **Available in London only**

Take your­ first steps into the world of architecture. Learn about design principles, practical skills, building a portfolio, and how to progress into an architectural career. With London as your base, you’ll also explore the world-class architecture the city has to offer.

Art and Design

## Art and Design **Available in London only**

Experiment with different media, techniques and forms to express your creativity. Explore illustration, painting, typography, colour and more. Be inspired as you tour art galleries and learn about building a portfolio for entry to creative arts courses and institutions.

Business, Finance and Economics

## Business, Finance and Economics **Available in London and Cambridge**

Develop your understanding of the commercial world. Gain insight into how businesses operate – from business planning, investments and supply chains, all the way through to branding and marketing.

Computing, Robotics and AI

## Computing, Robotics and AI **Available in London and Cambridge**

Take an exciting look into the future. Explore the technology that will shape our lives and discover your areas of interest, with topics ranging from crypto to coding. Bring your imagination to tasks such as building a robot and developing characters for computer games.

Medicine and Sciences

## Medicine and Sciences **Available in London and Cambridge**

Start your journey to medical school. Take part in practical, hands-on workshops and build your theoretical knowledge. Gain valuable guidance from medical professionals and insight into how to succeed through the medical school admissions process.

Physics and Engineering

## Physics and Engineering **Available Cambridge and London**

Bring your understanding of these three complementary subjects to life. Mix practical workshops with theoretical sessions to explore topics such as speed and acceleration, light properties, trigonometry, and electrical circuits.

Politics and Law

## Politics and Law **Available in London and Cambridge**

Understand the institutions and decisions that affect how we live and are governed. Explore the social, legal and political issues that dominate the headlines in our increasingly interconnected, but volatile world.

Psychology and Sociology

## Psychology and Sociology **Available in London and Cambridge**

Take a fascinating look at human behaviour and how it shapes modern society. Discover the theory behind criminal behaviour, explore social identity, analyse family function in modern societies, and the influence of the media.
