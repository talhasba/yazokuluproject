The engineering curriculum

Inspired by university learning

Week one · Foundations

The Foundations of Your Career in Engineering

This model introduces you to the range of disciplines involved in engineering. In lectures and seminars delivered by guest speakers and our expert faculty, you will hear about civil, mechanical, aerospace and electrical engineering.

This module gets you up to speed with the mathematical skills used at university-level study of engineering, and the techniques real engineers use every day. You will look at topics from algebra, trigonometry and statistics and apply your learning to real world problems.

This module immerses you in one of the most important skillsets possessed by every engineer; project management. Using real case studies and completing simulation challenges, you will look at how engineers plan and schedule projects, as well as costing them.

Week two · Developing Skills

Working Like an Engineer

In this exciting module, you will get to grips with the branches of mechanical engineering and the mathematics behind thermodynamics and power generation. A hydraulics design challenge will immerse you in fluid mechanics. You will learn about the engineering technologies of the future, and how they are already changing how engineers work. You will also be introduced to the materials engineers use and how they test them including stress and Young's modulus. You will put this into practice during a tensile testing simulation.

This module covers the built environment, including buildings and infrastructure. Using real-world case studies, you will explore the role of civil and structural engineering in society and consider the ways structures are designed. You will learn about the principles of mechanics and consider the impact of and mathematics of load in the design of structures. Everything you learn will be applied in hands-on design projects.

Electrical engineering involves the practical applications of electricity; electronic engineering refers to the design and application of electrical systems for industry. You will dive into these two related areas through an introduction to the core principles and laws of electricity including circuits and currents. Looking towards future technology, you will explore how solar radiation is harnessed and its potential future applications. You will end this module with an electrical engineering practical.

Faculty & speakers

Taught by innovative academics and working engineers

Academy Royale International’s Engineering course is taught by an expert faculty of leading experts and real engineers

Dr Digby Chappell

University of Oxford

Dr Digby Chappell is an Associate Professor in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, a member of the Oxford Robotics Institute and a Tutorial Fellow at St Anne’s College. He holds a PhD from Imperial College London. Digby leads the Human Enhancing and Augmenting Technology Lab, developing body-interfacing robotics designed to restore, improve and transform human abilities.

Dr Nikita Hari

University of Oxford

Dr Nikita Hari is Head of the Teaching and Research Design Group in the Department of Engineering at the University of Oxford. She holds a doctorate in Electrical Engineering from Cambridge and has more than a decade of experience across industry and higher education, alongside several international academic and professional roles. She is featured in the ‘Engineers Making a Difference’ book by Imperial College London (2022), ‘Top 50 Women in Engineering in the UK’ (2017) and listed as a ‘Clinton Global Changemaker’ (2018).

Christoph Birkl

University of Oxford

Christoph Birkl is an expert in energy storage and battery technology with more than 15 years of experience in the sector. He holds a DPhil in Engineering Science from the University of Oxford, where his doctoral research specialised in lithium-ion batteries. Christoph went on to co-found Brill Power, an Oxford spin-out developing battery intelligence technology, and led the company for nearly ten years before its acquisition by Palmer Energy Technology. He now leads the innovation office at Palmer Energy Technology, continuing his innovative work.

Engineering Summer School FAQs

Everything families need to know

Detailed answers for parents and students comparing Engineering summer schools.

This programme is designed for bright, academically ambitious students who are excited to spend their summer advancing their understanding of Engineering alongside an international cohort of similarly-minded peers. You don’t need to be sure that you want to be an Engineer in the future, you just need to be interested in learning more about the subject.

No previous experience of studying Engineering is needed. The course is designed to stretch students beyond the school curriculum in related fields including maths and physics and introduce new Engineering-specific concepts. All students need is an interest in Engineering and enthusiasm for learning new things.

The course balances academic intensity with practical and hands-on activities which solidify learning by putting it into real-world contexts. The course is designed to stretch and challenge ambitious students by introducing them to university-level concepts and teaching styles. Their learning is guided by expert members of our faculty and takes place in small classes, ensuring that every student feels supported and enjoys their learning while encountering new ideas.

The course is the perfect preparation for university applications. By giving students a solid foundation in the mathematical skills and scientific background of Engineering, it strengthens applications. Students have the opportunity to ask their tutors and industry professionals for application advice. They also receive a certificate, grades and a report which make excellent supporting materials for a strong university application.

The course combines lectures and intellectual debate with a heavy focus on developing hands-on skills. Students experience what it is like to work in various fields of Engineering through simulations and skills workshops, creating a very hands-on course.

A typical morning involves a lecture and two seminars or workshops. In the afternoon students have another extended workshop or practical session. The rest of the day is spent socialising with friends during activities, enjoying delicious meals in inspiring campus settings and exploring the city.

The programme takes place in one of two inspiring campuses in the historic colleges of the Universities of Oxford and Cambridge. Whichever location they choose, students have access to university-level facilities and teaching spaces.

By teaching in small groups, we ensure that each student has a personalised relationship with their tutor. This means they receive high-quality constructive feedback and any support they need in class. It also means that students bond with their classmates, creating a friendly atmosphere in which students get involved and ask questions with confidence.

Our students come from all over the world. Since our first summer school in 2004, we have welcomed students from over 175 countries. They speak a wide range of languages and come from a wide range of backgrounds, creating a vibrant community perfect for international students.

At the end of the course, students receive a graduation pack which contains a certificate acknowledging their academic achievements during the course. They also receive a personalised report and detailed grades.

Engineering insights

A helpful guide for students considering Engineering at Academy Royale International and beyond

Is Engineering the right subject for me?

Engineering is an academically challenging but diverse and highly rewarding subject which combines complex maths and physics with design and logistics elements. Ask yourself these questions to determine if Engineering is the right path to follow.
  • When I walk around a city or town, do I find the buildings and infrastructure around me interesting?
  • Do I want to get hands-on with my learning, experimenting with circuits, tech and models?
  • Am I enthusiastic about working in teams to solve complex issues?
  • Do I enjoy applying maths to real-world problems and demanding puzzles?

What will I learn?

At an Academy Royale International summer school, we demonstrate how these core strands of scientific knowledge and practical skills interconnect to give you a full picture of the subject.
  • Technology and innovation including the future of AI in Engineering
  • Electrical and electronic engineering including circuitry
  • Mechanical and aeronautical engineering including statics and dynamics
  • Civil and structural engineering including building and infrastructure design

How should I choose a location?

Each of our locations delivers serious academic study within an inspiring campus. Choose your perfect location according to the setting, atmosphere and dates which suit you best. These are some questions to ask yourself when choosing.
  • Would I prefer the busy atmosphere of Oxford or the more peaceful and scholarly atmosphere of Cambridge?
  • Which dates on offer fit with the rest of my summer plans?
  • Which city am I the most excited to explore with an international community of new friends?
