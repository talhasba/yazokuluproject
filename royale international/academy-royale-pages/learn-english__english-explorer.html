Practise English in Real Situations

Ready to put your English lessons to real use? English Explorer was created for ambitious teenagers, designed to build confidence and fluency through immersive projects, personalised electives, and a summer of independence, teamwork, and exploration.

English Explorer treats language not just as a subject, but as a tool to communicate, debate, and resolve real-world issues. Hands-on projects and fascinating excursions build speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills, while a focus on collaboration, creativity, and critical thinking help English develop naturally in authentic situations.

The english explorer curriculum

From Building Confidence to Real Conversations.

Every day balances two core English lessons with personalised electives, chosen from three pathways: Academic, Creative, or Get Active. By exploring an area of real interest, students connect to their passions, growing more confident in English, and building the skills necessary for success.

English Course

Customise your English course with electives

Two lessons each morning, built around global themes designed to encourage discussion and critical thinking. Explore topics as varied as Epic Journeys, Nation of Innovation, Cities of the Future, and Sustainable Planet. Students work together to investigate, debate, and problem-solve in English, strengthening analytical thinking alongside the core skills of speaking, listening, reading, and writing.

Academic electives: Deepen critical thinking through Mixed Media Journalism, Young Changemakers, Presentations & Debates, and Build, Test, Improve. Creative Electives: Channel your interests with hands-on experience in film-making, design and textiles, content creation, photography, and sketching. Get Active electives: Strengthen mind and body while playing football, tennis, netball, swimming, water polo, cricket, badminton, basketball, dance, yoga, and more!

From ARI's Got Talent to the Graduation Party, evenings are where real friendships happen. Every activity is built for teenagers, giving students the confidence and independence to shape their own social summer, entirely in English.

Learning Through Doing

Take it Beyond The Textbook

English Explorer takes students beyond the classroom into moments that matter: pitching a business idea to industry experts, presenting a project at Exhibition Evening, negotiating plans with people they only just met. Every one of those situations demands and develops a strong grasp of English, and builds the confidence that comes only from using a language to achieve real results.

  • English vocabulary is absorbed and applied through authentic interactions
  • Repeated real-world use builds natural fluency, mirroring the way that children pick up their first language
  • On their travels, students are exposed to regional accents and dialects

Building Confidence and English Skills

By the end of the course, your child won't just have revised their English: they'll have used it to communicate complex ideas to real audiences, and connect to like-minded students from across the globe. Their newfound passion for learning will be rivalled only by the independence and confidence that comes from knowing they can flourish anywhere.

Expand their horizons this summer at Academy Royale International.

A setting fit for Royalty

Live and Learn in Marlborough College

A full social and cultural programme brings each destination to life.

  • Historic Settings

    Founded in 1843, Marlborough College is an elite boarding school offering a safe and inspiring environment with historic architecture and outstanding modern facilities.

  • Explore, Play, Thrive

    Set within 286 acres, the campus offers extensive sports pitches and an on-site swimming pool, giving students plenty of space to stay active, explore and unwind.

  • Outstanding Boarding Facilities

    Students live in high-quality boarding houses and receive constant support from our experienced residential team.

  • An Inspiring Legacy

    Live and learn where Nobel and Poet Laureates and two princesses once studied.

Exciting Excursions

Explore Britain’s Most Historic Sites

Carefully planned excursions bring learning to life while introducing students to British culture. Taking place twice each week, these trips offer opportunities to apply new skills, solve entertaining team challenges and create lasting memories, along with finding a few souvenirs to take home. Destinations include:

  • Oxford

    Discover the dreaming spires that inspired Harry Potter on a walking tour of Oxford's historic colleges.

  • London

    Explore England’s iconic capital, taking in Buckingham Palace, Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament, with views of the London Eye and Tower of London.

  • Stratford-upon-Avon and Warwick Castle

    Discover Shakespearean language in the Bard’s birthplace, then let imaginations run wild at one of England’s finest medieval castles.

  • Stonehenge and Bath

    Discover mysterious Stonehenge and explore Bath’s Roman heritage and Regency architecture in two of Britain’s most iconic historic destinations.
