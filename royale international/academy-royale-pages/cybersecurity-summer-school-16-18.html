Week one · Foundations

AI Confidence and Cyber Security Essentials

Machine learning is a type of AI which lets computers find patterns in data to learn from it, meaning computers get better at tasks the more they learn. In this module, you will engage with practical examples and discussions to explore the different ways AI can learn, including supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning. You will consider how these methods can influence the behaviours and accuracy of AI models, while critically analysing data sets for yourself.

You will probably have seen news articles and been involved in conversions with friends and family about the ethics of AI. This module gives you an opportunity to debate ethical uses of AI at length, discussing issues such as privacy concerns, bias in algorithms and the impact of AI on employment and the environment. You will learn why responsible development and regulation are essential for the future of AI, and critically evaluate case studies where industry regulation has fallen short.

The sometimes controversial uses of generative AI (GenAI) are an important topic for discussion. In this module, you will be introduced to its theoretical principles and real-world applications. You will investigate the large language models which power many AI tools and the vast datasets which train GenAI models to create content. You will understand how GenAI turns patterns in language and data into images, text, videos and code and begin to create your own GenAI code.

Natural language processing (NLP) combines computational linguistics, machine learning and statistical models to understand and generate human language. You will examine how AI systems process text and speech to translate, summarise text and generate conversation. Through real-world examples including chatbots and translation software, you will discover how NLP transforms computer processing into human communication.

In this module, you will explore how digital forensics are used to solve cyber crimes. You will learn how to identify and collect digital evidence, getting to grips with tools including Autopsy and FTK Imager. Finally, you will put your skills to the test by working in groups to solve a cyber mystery.

This module introduces you to the vulnerabilities of websites, and gives you the chance to get to grips with ethical hacking tools like Burp Suite. You will learn now hackers can exploit vulnerabilities including cross-site scripting (XSS) to insert malicious code into websites, and how website-building software can mitigate against this.

Week two · developing skills

Protect Yourself (and Others) Online

This module dives into the exciting world of cryptography. After learning about its history and basic principles such as the difference between encryption and decryption, you will get hands-on cracking simple codes. Looking at 21st century cryptography, you will learn how encryption is used to secure data. You will explore how different cryptographic methods work in real-life scenarios such as security breaches.

This module aims to get your coding skills up to scratch, teaching you command-line basics and introducing you to binary code and memory structures. You will also learn how to decompile applications through reverse engineering.

In this module, you will learn the basics of networking including IP Addresses DNS and Firewalls. Looking from a cyber security angle, you will learn how malware and phishing attacks work and about the monitoring tools, e.g., Wireshark, which aim to protect networks. You will have the chance to defend a network against a simulated cyber attack, an essential skill in the cyber security space.

In this module, you will learn why passwords matter and the lengths hackers go to crack them using software including Brute Force and Rainbow tables. You will learn how to create strong passwords and passphrases, and how to identify and report phishing.

Taking a step back from the keyboard, this module asks you to consider the ethics of cybersecurity. You will understand the differences between White and Black Hat hacking, critically evaluating what constitutes ethical hacking. Finally, you will explore the range of careers available to cybersecurity experts and the paths, university and career based, to land your dream role in cyber security.

Learning outcomes

By the end, you will know:

You will leave Academy Royale International with a new understanding of the role of AI in 21st century cyber security.

Cyber Security Summer School FAQs

Everything families need to know

Detailed answers for parents and students comparing Cyber Security & AI summer schools.

This programme is designed for curious, engaged students who are excited to spend part of their summer developing their understanding of Cyber Security & AI. It is perfect for students who already enjoy learning about science and technology, and want to stretch their learning beyond the classroom and apply it to the real world.

No previous knowledge of AI, cyber security, computer science or coding is needed. While coding skills are great, all modules come with coding-free options for students who are new to the subject. No knowledge of AI theory or practical use is needed, and software access is provided.

The course is designed to push bright students to take on new academic challenges by introducing lots of new concepts and practical tasks. While students are stretched, their learning is guided by expert members of our faculty and takes place in small classes, ensuring that every student feels supported and enjoys their learning while encountering new ideas.

The course is the perfect first step towards university applications for subjects including computer science, software engineering and mathematics. Students have the opportunity to ask their tutors and industry professionals for application advice. They also receive a certificate, grades and a report which make excellent supporting materials for a strong university application.

The course is very hands-on. Each module involves practical demonstrations and tasks, and the final Practical Project is a chance for students to immerse themselves in an extended piece of hands-on work. Students enjoy consolidating their learning through these practical challenges and getting to know their classmates during team challenges..

A typical morning involves a lecture and two seminars or workshops. In the afternoon students have another extended workshop or clinical practice session. The rest of the day is spent socialising with friends during activities, enjoying delicious meals in inspiring campus settings and exploring the city.

The programme takes place in the inspiring campuses of the Universities of Oxford. Students have access to university-level facilities and teaching spaces.

Classes are designed to be small, with a maximum of 12 students for close interaction and discussion. By teaching in small groups, we ensure that each student has a personalised relationship with their tutor. This means they receive high-quality constructive feedback and any support they need in class. It also means that students bond with their classmates, creating a friendly atmosphere in which students get involved and ask questions with confidence.

Our students come from all over the world. Since our first summer school in 2004, we have welcomed students from over 175 countries. They speak a wide range of languages and come from a wide range of backgrounds, creating a vibrant community perfect for international students.

At the end of the course, students receive a graduation pack which contains a certificate acknowledging their academic achievements during the course. They also receive a personalised report and detailed grades.

Cyber Security & AI insights

A helpful guide for students considering Cyber Security & AI at Academy Royale International and beyond

Is Cyber Security & AI the right subject for me?

AI is the technology of the future, and is already shaping modern cyber security. The course is technical, but backs up this computing knowledge with practical demonstrations and engaging challenges. Ask yourself these questions to determine if Explore Artificial Intelligence is the right course for you.
  • Do I enjoy learning about science and technology?
  • Am I curious about the uses, challenges and future of AI?
  • When I see a cyber security case in the news, do I want to learn more?
  • Am I willing to make bold cyber security decisions and stick by them?

What will I learn?

At an Academy Royale International Summer School, we bring learning to life by taking it beyond the classroom. You will leave with a new appreciation for the place of AI in modern cyber security and your own abilities to use it.
  • AI essentials including Natural Language Processing and machine learning
  • Cybersecurity skills including cryptography and binary code
  • Cyber self-defence including password protection
  • The careers available to cyber security experts

How should I choose a location?

Each of our locations delivers serious academic study within an inspiring campus. Choose your perfect location according to the setting, atmosphere and dates which suit you best. These are some questions to ask yourself when choosing.
  • Would I prefer the busy atmosphere of Oxford or the more peaceful and scholarly atmosphere of Cambridge?
  • Which dates on offer fit with the rest of my summer plans?
  • Which city am I the most excited to explore with an international community of new friends?
