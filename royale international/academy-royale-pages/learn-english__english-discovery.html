English That Comes Naturally

Learn English by Living It

English Discovery makes language learning natural, active and enjoyable. Instead of relying on grammar exercises, students investigate ideas, solve problems and present exciting projects in English. Excursions and everyday campus life create even more opportunities to practise, helping students build confidence and communicate more fluently without it ever feeling like a lesson.

Developed by an English language education expert with more than 30 years’ experience in teaching, teacher training and curriculum design, the programme combines proven expertise with an approach younger students genuinely enjoy.

The english discovery curriculum

From First Words to Real Confidence

Every day mixes structured English lessons with sports sessions, creative clubs, and a lively evening social programme, offering well-rounded opportunities to build lasting friendships and confidence in English.

English Course

English Brought to Life

Two lessons in the morning, built around a project your child will find genuinely fascinating. Children investigate, present, and problem-solve in English, developing speaking, listening, reading and writing together, the way language actually gets used.

Sport, movement, and English, combined. Children test their vocab and grow in confidence through games and physical challenges. This kind of active learning, proven to develop fluency, doesn't feel like learning at all. Academics: deepen critical thinking through Mixed Media Journalism, Young Changemakers, Presentations & Debates, and Build, Test, Improve. Creative: channel your interests with hands-on experience in film-making, cooking, design and textiles, content creation, photography, sketching, and performing arts. Get Active: strengthen mind and body while playing football, tennis, netball, swimming, water polo, cricket, dance, yoga, and more!

Drawing, building, performing, inventing, all in English. Creative Discovery turns practicing language into a passion project; children are motivated by their own innate curiosity to learn and express themselves in increasingly advanced ways.

Extra Curricular

Make friends from around the world

Enjoy time on the beautiful Marlborough College campus with your new friends from around the world. Play games in the common room, explore the campus grounds outside or simply unwind in your room.

After the school day ends, the English doesn't. Evening activities with new friends and shared adventures mean that children keep practising long after the last lesson, without even noticing they're still learning!

English Discovery brings learning to life through unforgettable excursions. Students explore historic Oxford, see London’s landmarks on an open-top bus tour and visit the Science Museum. They will also step back in time at Warwick Castle and enjoy a photo-challenge treasure trail at Cotswold Wildlife Park.

Learning English Beyond the Classroom

English Discovery takes children beyond the classroom into real situations: ordering food at the tuck shop, working together to solve challenges, making new friends from different countries. Every one of those moments is a chance to practise, and practice builds confidence.

  • English vocabulary is absorbed and applied through authentic interactions
  • Repeated real-world use builds natural fluency, mirroring the way that children pick up their first language
  • On their travels, students are exposed to regional accents and dialects

Skills and Confidence for the Future

By the end of the course, your child won't just know more English words: they'll be confident actually using them. Students build independence and confidence by forging new friendships and living away from home, gaining a passion for learning and the knowledge they can flourish anywhere.

01

Project-Based Practice

Learning made fun, with lively discussions and hands-on presentations developing core listening, reading, writing, and speaking skills.

02

Cultural Immersion

Our educational excursions can't be rehearsed: classroom skills get tested in real contexts, so children learn to think on their feet in English, not recite a prepared line.

03

Small Classes, Close Attention

Classes of 15 and a 1:6 staff-to-student ratio means that every child speaks often, receiving personalised feedback and constant encouragement.

04

Learning Through Movement and Making

Try various learning styles with Get Active and Creative Discovery, turning vocab into sport, games, and creative projects that build fluency without feeling like a chore.

05

Picking it up from Your Peers

With friends from all over the world, English becomes the shared language between the students themselves. It is a reason to communicate, not just a subject to study.

06

A Safe Space to Get It Wrong

Constant pastoral support from our residential team means children feel secure enough to take risks with a new language, so mistakes become part of the process instead of something to fear.

Live and Learn in Marlborough College

Founded in 1843, Marlborough College is an elite boarding school set on 286 acres of beautiful Wiltshire countryside. The College offers a safe, welcoming, and inspirational environment, with historic architecture sitting alongside outstanding modern facilities. Students live in high-quality boarding houses and receive constant support from our experienced residential team.

Exciting Excursions

Explore Britain’s Most Historic Sites

Twice a week, students enjoy carefully planned trips that complement their learning and introduce them to British culture. Newfound skills are applied in real contexts as they work together solving entertaining challenges, making memories (and finding souvenirs) that they will bring home and talk about for years to come.

Excursions are fully supervised by our expert staff to ensure safety, comfort, and an enriching experience. Locations include:

  • Oxford

    Discover the magic of Oxford through a Harry Potter Trail and The Story Museum.

  • London

    Explore England’s iconic capital with an Open-Top Bus Tour, and take on interactive challenges at the Science Museum.

  • Warwick Castle

    Let imaginations run wild at Warwick Castle, one of England's most impressive medieval castles.

  • Cotswold Wildlife Park

    Embark on an adventure through Cotswold Wildlife Park, competing with friends to win the Photo Competition and taking on the Treasure Trail.

A typical day at Marlborough College

Learn and have fun as you make new friends

A balanced junior programme from morning to evening, carefully structured so that younger students feel safe, supported, and excited by what comes next.

  1. 08:00

    Breakfast: wake up and enjoy a hearty meal.

  2. 09:00

    Language Lesson 1: meet your classmates and get started on a lively project (e.g. Great Inventions: Steam, Steel, and Speed)

  3. 11:00

    Language Lesson 2: develop your English with another exciting topic (e.g. Epic stories and Storytelling)

  4. 12:30

    Lunchtime

  5. 13:30

    Get Active: build friendships as you channel your energy into sport and games.

  6. 15:30

    Creative Discovery: explore art, performance, and the joy of making something new to impress your class.

  7. 18:00

    Dinner: enjoy a well-earned meal in Marlborough’s impressive dining hall.

  8. 19:30

    Evening Activities: make friends, solve challenges, and build confidence with our exciting social programme (e.g. Games night, Popcorn Premier, or Black and White Disco).

  9. 22:00

    Lights out: get some well deserved rest, ready for another day of exploration!
