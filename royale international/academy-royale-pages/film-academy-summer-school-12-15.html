The curriculum

Inspired by film school

Week one · lights, camera

Directing Theory & Technical Training

The first week of your programme is all about getting to grips with the theory and practical skills behind filmmaking. You will explore the key elements of filmmaking including composition, lighting and audio, focusing on the different roles undertaken by directors. Your technical training will involve practical instruction on how to set up cameras and take different kinds of shots, as well as other filmmaking equipment including audio equipment. You will then scout out potential filming locations around the city.

This is your chance to put theory into action. You will do a practice shoot, making sure to review your rushes (unedited footage) throughout the day. You will then begin work on your main project, drafting shooting scripts and conducting a table-read. These are parts of the real filmmaking process and involve collaborative editing and familiarisation with the script’s content. The rest of the first week will be spent doing what you love, by shooting your Film in iconic locations around the city and University of Oxford.

Week two · action!

Post-Production

Week 2 kicks off the post-production process, turning your footage into a compelling Film. You will use cutting-edge software including FinalCut Pro to edit your Film. You will also learn to shoot pick-ups to replace any unsuccessful shots. After several dedicated days for editing, you will have the chance to premier your Film with the rest of your cohort and tutor, exhibiting it on a big screen to celebrate your achievements.

To ensure you leave with a full understanding of the filmmaking process, our Film Academy provides additional tuition in key areas including a screenwriting masterclass from an industry expert and an editing workshop, to ensure every script you produce comes out perfectly polished. You will also receive valuable instruction on the different roles within the Film industry, opening your eyes to the career possibilities available to you and setting you up for future success.

Faculty & speakers

Taught by leading filmmakers and real directors

Academy Royale International’s Film Academy is taught by an expert faculty of experienced film industry professionals

Bruce Windwood

Filmmaker, Writer and Composer

Bruce Windwood is a multi-award-winning filmmaker whose credits include the comedy feature film 12 In A Box. Alongside his filmmaking work, he is a commissioned writer for television and audio and a composer for film, audio and television soundtracks. Bruce has helped to design, develop and expand Academy Royale International's filmmaking classes over the past 11 years, making him one of the programme’s most experienced tutors. He also teaches filmmaking at the National Film & Television School and Film Oxford.

Gerard Foster

Writer for Theatre, Radio and Television

Gerard Foster is a prolific writer. As part of the Cheese Shop comedy team, he wrote and performed sell-out shows at the Edinburgh Fringe and contributed to four series on BBC Radio 4. His other radio credits include songs, sketches, plays and sitcoms, including At Home With The Snails. From 1997 to 1999, Gerard was part of the resident comedy team on BBC One’s Live & Kicking. He has since written extensively for children’s television, with credits including Gigglebiz, Bing, PJ Masks, Postman Pat and the multi-award-winning Horrible Histories, for which he was twice nominated for a BAFTA, alongside a host of new credits.

Govind Chandran

Oxford Brookes University

Govind Chandran is the Subject Coordinator and Senior Lecturer in Film at Oxford Brookes University with 15 years of teaching experience. An independent filmmaker for over two decades, his work has screened globally at Cannes and the AFI. Through his company, CRO Productions, Govind has produced content for major brands and consulted on high-profile feature films. His latest award-winning short, Safe (2025), has screened at IMAX and the British Museum, winning top honours at BAFTA-qualifying festivals.

Film Academy Summer School FAQs

Everything families need to know

Detailed answers for parents and students comparing Film Academy summer schools.

This programme is designed for curious, enthusiastic students who are excited to dive into the world of filmmaking alongside an international cohort of similarly-minded peers. You don’t need to be sure that you want to pursue a career in Film, you just need to be interested in learning more about the subject.

No previous experience of Film is needed. The course is designed as a foundational step, providing students with skills and training in an entirely new area which is not traditionally taught in schools. All students need is an interest in Film and enthusiasm for learning new things.

The course is academically rigorous. It is designed to stretch and challenge ambitious students to reach their potential by introducing them to university-level concepts and teaching styles. Their learning is guided by expert members of our faculty and takes place in small classes, ensuring that every student feels supported and enjoys their learning while encountering new ideas.

The course is the perfect preparation for a degree or career in Film. By developing the practical skills which are not taught in schools, students leave the programme with advantageous strengths. The project-based learning style in which students work collaboratively to create and create a Film can form the basis of a compelling portfolio for university or job applications.

The course is very hands-on. While there is some classroom learning focused on essential filmmaking knowledge, it is mostly focused on developing practical skills. This gives students the opportunity to get comfortable using equipment they would be unlikely to ordinarily have access to, and gives them time to develop a project from idea to screening.

A typical morning involves a lecture from an industry expert or other inspiring speaker, or a skills-focused. The rest of the morning and the first half of the afternoon is spent working on students’ projects. They will either be shooting on location or working on post-production, depending on how far into the programme they are. The rest of the day is spent socialising with friends during activities, enjoying delicious meals in inspiring campus settings and exploring the city.

The programme takes place within one of the colleges of the University of Oxford. Students stay in real undergraduate accommodation, eat in historic dining halls, and have plenty of opportunities to go out and discover the city.

The course is taught by industry experts hand-selected by Academy Royale International for their skills and experience. This includes people with experience behind and in front of the lens, as well as screenwriters and audio technicians. Students also have the opportunity to join students from other subjects to hear inspiring guest speakers.

By teaching in small groups, we ensure that each student has a personalised relationship with their tutor. This means they receive high-quality constructive feedback and any support they need in class. It also means that students bond with their classmates, creating a friendly atmosphere in which students get involved and ask questions with confidence.

Our students come from all over the world. Since our first summer school in 2004, we have welcomed students from over 175 countries. They speak a wide range of languages and come from a wide range of backgrounds, creating a vibrant community perfect for international students.

At the end of the course, students receive a graduation pack which contains a certificate acknowledging their academic achievements during the course. They also receive a personalised report and detailed grades.

Film Academy Insights

A helpful guide for students considering Film at Academy Royale International and beyond

Is Film Academy the course for me?

Film is a diverse subject and industry which combines creativity, project-management and hands-on skills. Ask yourself these questions to determine if Film is the right path for you to follow.
  • When I go to the cinema or watch something on television at home, am I fascinated by the creative choices behind what I am watching?
  • Am I full of ideas for my own filmmaking, and waiting for an opportunity to make them real?
  • Am I enthusiastic about working with an international group to bring ideas into reality?
  • Do I want to get hands-on with my learning, using professional equipment?

What will I learn?

At Academy Royale International’s Film Academy, we develop core skills and knowledge to empower you to create something amazing.
  • Camera and audio work including use of microphones
  • Screenwriting including read-throughs, editing and storyboarding
  • Post-production skills including video editing software and filming pick-ups
  • The varied roles that directors take on, and other careers available in the Film industry

How should I choose a location?

Each of our locations delivers serious academic study within an inspiring campus. Choose your perfect location according to the setting, atmosphere and dates which suit you best. These are some questions to ask yourself when choosing.
  • Would I prefer the busy atmosphere of Oxford or the more peaceful and scholarly atmosphere of Cambridge?
  • Which dates on offer fit with the rest of my summer plans?
  • Which city am I the most excited to explore with an international community of new friends?
