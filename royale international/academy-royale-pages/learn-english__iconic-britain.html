A summer that changes everything

Where Britain Becomes the Classroom

Iconic Britain doesn't just teach the language, it teaches the story behind it. Each lesson connects to a place students will visit that week. A study of Medieval castles is brought to life by Warwick Castle, never to be forgotten. Classroom learning and real experience reinforce each other, so English becomes the natural language of discovery, communication, and explanation.

The Iconic Britain Curriculum

From Building Confidence to Real Conversations

Every day blends two core English lessons inspired by the place you'll visit next, a hands-on project connected to that day's topic, and a Get Active session, building language skills, confidence, and a passion for history and culture side by side.

The Iconic Britain Course

The Lessons

Two lessons each morning are built around fascinating topics and grounded by in-person excursions, designed to encourage discussion and critical thinking. Topics range from the Monarchy & Parliament to Ancient Wonders & World Heritage, Medieval Britain, Shakespeare & the West End, J.K. Rowling's Wizarding World, and Oxford & Britain's Universities. Students research, present, and debate in English, strengthening core skills and turning every place into a springboard for in-depth learning and authentic conversation.

Morning lessons are followed by a hands-on project designed to inspire and develop communication, putting today's theme straight into practice. Students might present on the role of the monarchy or system of government in their own country, produce a poster of a UNESCO world heritage site before getting the chance to visit it, or study entertainment before film, television, and the internet.

Explore Britain with new friends

The Extracurriculars

Daily Get Active sessions bring energy and teamwork into the mix. Choose between football, tennis, netball, swimming/water polo, badminton, cricket, basketball, dance, yoga, and more! Students gain a well-rounded experience, building resilience, friendships, and confidence, all while developing their English.

From Academy Royale International's Got Talent to the Graduation Party, evenings are filled with games, talent shows, and shared adventures, so the skills learned and friendships formed carry on long after the coach ride home.

Learning Through Doing

Britain Becomes Your Classroom

Iconic Britain takes students beyond the textbook and into the places history actually happened. Every excursion is a chance to use English exactly where it matters most.

Week 1

Ancient Wonders and Royal Traditions

Visit Buckingham Palace and the Houses of Parliament as you tour London's most iconic sights, then enjoy free time to shop in Covent Garden. Students research, present, and debate in English, strengthening core skills and turning every place into a springboard for in-depth learning and authentic conversation.

See two UNESCO World Heritage Sites in one day, soaking up the unforgettable atmosphere of Stonehenge before exploring the long history of an elegant city famous for its Roman baths.

Explore the dreaming spires of Oxford, one of Britain's most iconic cities and home to one of the world's oldest and most prestigious universities. Wander through ancient halls, atmospheric colleges and inspiring libraries.

Week two

From Shakespeare to the Silver Screen

Step back in time to Shakespeare's historic birthplace, and let imaginations run wild at one of England's most impressive medieval castles. Students gain a well-rounded experience, building resilience, friendships, and confidence, all while developing their English.

Spend a day at the theatre: visit Shakespeare's own Globe, then catch an unmissable show in London's West End.

Go behind the scenes of a real film set, and discover how the magic of storytelling really comes to life.

Learn Differently with Academy Royale International

By the end of the course, students won't just have visited some of Britain's most iconic destinations, they will have absorbed them, been inspired by them, and gained the ability to write and talk about them, in English, for years to come.

This summer, gift them cultural fluency and personal confidence that lasts a lifetime.

01

Place-Based Studies

History, geography, culture, and language all reinforce one another, encouraging multidisciplinary thinking and higher-level analytic skills.

02

Project-Based Learning

Core lessons lead into hands-on projects including posters, presentations and comparisons to a student's own country, turning historical theory into a polished piece of spoken or written English.

03

Immersive Excursions

Six full-day excursions to historic sites ensures that English is practiced where it’s needed most: asking, listening, describing, and responding, all in real-time.

04

Personal Attention

Classes are capped at 15, meaning that every student is heard, encouraged, and receives personal feedback when they need it the most.

05

Learning From Peers

Exploring exciting landmarks alongside friends from across the world means that English becomes the shared language to be used and practiced throughout the stay.

06

Active Learning, Every Day

Daily Get Active sessions turn vocab studies into engaging, team-building exercises, building fluency and friendship side by side.

A setting fit for Royalty

Live and Learn in Marlborough College

A full social and cultural programme brings each destination to life.

  • Historic Settings

    Founded in 1843, Marlborough College is an elite boarding school offering a safe and inspiring environment with historic architecture and outstanding modern facilities.

  • Explore, Play, Thrive

    Set within 286 acres, the campus offers extensive sports pitches and an on-site swimming pool, giving students plenty of space to stay active, explore and unwind.

  • Outstanding Boarding Facilities

    Students live in high-quality boarding houses and receive constant support from our experienced residential team.

  • An Inspiring Legacy

    Live and learn where Nobel and Poet Laureates—and two princesses—once studied.
